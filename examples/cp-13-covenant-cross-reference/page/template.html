Rung 13 · Tier 4

A span can be correct and still be unauditable

This is the first rung where the evidence is text, and the first where a claim can be wrong in a way that looks completely right.

All 49 CovenantDefinition nodes in this estate carry a span whose offsets land on exactly the right words. None carries a recorded hash. None carries a named passage. No indexed passage even covers the span.

48 of 49  startOffset, endOffset, documentId
 0 of 49  passageSha256
 0 of 49  evidencedBy
 0        passages matching or containing the span

extracted:  "Consolidated Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio" means, for any Measurement Period …
            "Consolidated Leverage Ratio": as at the last day of any period, the ratio o…

measured through the defective mechanism:
  48 spans extractable      47 read as genuine definitions      0 auditable
Which is what makes it the hard case

A wrong citation is caught by a hash. An unauditable one is caught only by a product that refuses to emit what it cannot check — and refuses while the text on screen is visibly correct. Emitting it would be an asserted citation, and an asserted citation is indistinguishable from a checked one in the payload.

The product emits none of them, classifies all 49 span-unverifiable, and says so in the payload. It does not silently omit them — an unexplained absence would make “we could not evidence this” indistinguishable from “we did not look”.

The citation unit

A named passage, re-hashed — never four literals joined

acquisition._cite is the single path from a chain node to quoted text and it has no second constructor: follow ex:evidencedBy to a passage IRI, resolve that passage by id, read its own offsets and sha256, cut the span by offset — never by re-searching for the text, which can match a different occurrence and silently relocate the citation — re-hash, compare. Anything that fails returns Uncitable.

And the rule is the contract, not a workaround

Joining startOffset, endOffset, passageSha256 and evidencedBy independently is prohibited even though, measured today, that join returns 44 clean rows. A rule that held only while the data was broken would be a workaround. The citation unit is the passage.

A marker is not a repair

ex:declaredAt names the limitation it does not lift

170 subjects, 158 of them DefinedTerm, arrived from the estate owner mid-build. It is a real improvement: the product can now read “this is intentionally a point anchor” off the graph instead of inferring it from a missing end.

It supplies no end offset. endOffset is still absent on all 158, the text still cannot be quoted, and the claim is still Context Debt. No endOffset is guessed. Reading the marker as the repair is the mistake, and CXR-G16 asserts both halves.

Policy before similarity

Identical hits do not excuse the leak

The permitted-document set is an argument to the MongoDB query, not a predicate over its results. A post-filter retrieves broadly, ranks everything, then drops the impermissible rows — and produces byte-identical hits.

credit-officer / filed                candidates examined   0
control-room   / filed-and-internal   candidates examined  22    not-evaluable 18
                                      the whole corpus is larger

relationship-manager / filed-and-internal   ->   REFUSED, not narrowed

What differs is how many documents were SCORED. A post-filter has already computed similarity against documents the principal may not see, and in an MNPI setting that computation is the leak. CXR-G22 asserts the two mechanisms produce the same hits, because that is what makes counting the scored population the only available detector.

And a missing embedding is not an empty result

18 of the 40 memoranda cannot be ranked at all. The payload reports them not-evaluable with the reason, the ids, and the count of ranked candidates beside the count of eligible documents — so a consumer can tell we searched and found nothing from we could not search these at all.

The evidence

Twenty-two gates, and four were green first

Every gate was sabotaged. Nineteen went red on their own assertion. Three could not be falsified from this deployment at all — an SDK guard refuses the bad state before an artifact can exist — and are recorded unavailable, never folded into passed.

Four were green under their first sabotage, and all four were TEST defects

CXR-G07 compared served output on an estate that contains no bad hash, so removing the product's own re-hash changed nothing it serves. CXR-G13 asserted ≤ where only < separates the two mechanisms. CXR-G15 tested a limb with an empty population — every document the anchors cite is public-filed, which every principal may read. CXR-G21 checked a constant and two side queries, none of which is what the product actually seeds on.

The principle they share: a gate must assert the thing the product does, on a population where doing it wrong would look different. All four are recorded in artifacts/gates.yaml with what was wrong and what fixed it.

What to change first

One flag, and it is the most instructive edit in the tree

  • PRINCIPAL=control-room CORPUS_SCOPE=filed-and-internal. Watch the permitted set, the candidates examined and the content address move together. That flag is the difference between a policy envelope and a filter.
  • PRINCIPAL=relationship-manager at the widened scope — a refusal rather than a narrower answer.
  • AS_OF before an amendment's governs_from, on one of the four borrowers whose dates actually separate.
  • The cxr_node λc row — set decay_basis: valid-from and the SDK refuses the serve, because the chain carries no instant at all.

Honest limits

What this example is not

  • No auditable definition text. All 49 definition spans are correct and uncheckable. The product reports them as debt rather than quoting them, and this cannot be re-keyed away — there is no recorded hash to key to.
  • No quotable defined terms. ex:endOffset is absent on all 158 DefinedTerm nodes. Every depth-3 chain terminates in a node that is located and not quotable.
  • No ex:governedBy. The published schema for this product names it; this estate has 0 assertions. The leg is declared absent and no proxy is substituted.
  • 548 of 37,865 passages carry no sha256 and are not citable; 18 of 40 memoranda carry no embedding and are not searchable. Both are reported, neither is read as an empty result.
  • 49 of 249 documents carry no classification. The entitlement rules key on it, so absence is treated as not admitted rather than as unrestricted.
  • Only 4 of 7 borrowers can demonstrate the as-of axis. On the other three the base and the amendment begin governing on the identical date, and a test asserted there would prove nothing.
  • Nothing in this chain decays, and that is measured rather than chosen. No class carries any temporal predicate — no extraction instant, no assertion date. The SDK refused a valid-from basis rather than falling back to the ingestion instant, so supersede-only is the honest declaration and not a calibration value.
