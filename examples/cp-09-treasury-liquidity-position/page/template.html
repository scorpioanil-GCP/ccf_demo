Rung 9 · Tier 3

Cash and rate posture at one instant, over four kinds of time

One as_of governs the whole determination — and does not flatten four different kinds of timestamp into one freshness claim. A market observation is selected and decays. A policy effective date is selected and does not decay: a rule is in force or it is not. A filed reporting period is bounded — a quarter closing after the decision instant is not yet knowable. And a holding whose observation time nobody ever recorded carries no time at all, which the payload states rather than filling in.

The client is the one treasury client in the estate holding US-NY deposits under a reserve-buffer mandate, which puts it under the single regulatory posture that has two versions — so moving the decision instant two days moves the policy in force, the answer, and the content address.

The refusal

A stale signal fails closed, and the determination survives the boundary

Asked at 2026-08-21T16:00Z, one rate signal is 45 minutes old against a 15-minute requirement. The product refuses. The consumer envelope discloses only that a declared invariant does not hold; the replay record names SIG-000, its age in seconds and the bound it broke. Those are two surfaces on purpose — the audit keeps what the boundary does not disclose.

Two scoping facts make that refusal honest rather than lucky. The June rate feed does not cover August, so it is absent rather than a 59-day-old observation — that age would be an artifact of the query, and it would have masked the real breach. And a USD-only request on a USD-home client needs no currency conversion, so no FX rate participates and no FX bound can fire. Scope decides what takes part before freshness decides what is admitted.

The evidence

Twenty-two gates, and four of them could not be falsified here

Every gate was sabotaged and watched go red. Eighteen went red on their own assertion. Four could not be broken from this deployment at all — an SDK guard refuses the bad state before the example can produce an artifact — and those are recorded as unavailable rather than as passed, because they mean different things. Collapsing them would tell a reviewer this example holds a line the SDK is actually holding.

Two sabotages proved to be testing nothing, and that is recorded too

TLP-G07's first draft grepped the whole replay record for "900" and passed under the very sabotage that deleted the bound from its message — because the record also carries the λc table, whose half_life_seconds is 900. It now reads the gate's own sentence out of content_generation_path.assembly_refusal. A check passing for a reason unrelated to what it claims to test is this project's own defect class, and it arrived inside the machinery built to catch it.

What to change first

Three edits, in the order you will want them

  • The staleness profile — artifacts/staleness-profile.yaml is the refusal rule, and ccf_config.yaml's half_life: is the decay model. They are separate on purpose: a class can decay without being able to refuse. Widen the intraday bound and the run that fails closed starts serving.
  • The decision instant — artifacts/question.yaml's as_of. Move it two days earlier and the regulatory posture in force changes, which changes the answer and the content address. That is the fastest way to see that policy effectivity is a step function rather than a decay curve.
  • The currency scope — a USD-only request on a USD-home client needs no conversion, so no FX rate participates and no FX bound can fire. Scope decides what takes part before freshness decides what is admitted.

Honest limits

What this example is not

  • Holdings carry no observation time, and the product says so. deposit_account and investment_position have no timestamp column at all, so the declared “deposit balances ~1 hour λc” cannot be computed against this estate. The product adds a fourth temporal class, unobserved-holding, publishes the absence, and never synthesizes an observed_at from the request instant.
  • One permitted-instrument check is vacuous. The KIND test cannot discriminate on this estate, and it is recorded as an estate limitation rather than presented as a control that fired.
  • Five time universes, no common live instant. The sources do not overlap at any single moment, so some scenario combinations are unreachable. Recorded rather than engineered around.
  • Two of the half-lives are illustrative. This example runs against a point-in-time demonstration estate rather than continuously refreshed production data. The thresholds are calibrated to make the governed behavior observable in that snapshot, and should not be read as recommended production values.

Two SDK rulings came out of this rung

The example refused to work around the defect it found

CP09-F-06 measured F = 1.000000 on a serve carrying three observations 65,100 seconds old against their own declared 86,400-second half-life. It was raised as a deviation from an approved CP-SPEC-009 term rather than patched with a local scorer.

A store that cannot represent “I do not know when this became true” cannot be asked “what was true then?”

D-IMPL-331 lets a λc row declare which of an observation's two instants it decays against, and refuse by name when the declared one is absent. D-IMPL-332 stops the assembly layer destroying the uncertainty that makes the declaration mean anything: an unsupplied valid-time stays absent rather than being filled in with the ingestion instant. Neither half works alone — a declaration the layer beneath cannot honor is decoration, and an honest substrate nobody interrogates is unused.

After them, cp-09's F reads 0.970232. The λc table is live on the freshness term for the first time.
