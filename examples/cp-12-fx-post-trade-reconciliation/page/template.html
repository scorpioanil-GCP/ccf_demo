Rung 12 · Tier 3

Nothing about the size of a discrepancy says which kind it is

Three systems report a value for the same FX trade and they disagree. The conventional response is to call it a data-quality issue and reconcile toward one system as golden. Frequently none of them is wrong: the trading system booked at execution, the settlement system recorded post-affirmation, and the risk system revalued at a different market snapshot. All three are internally correct as of their own observation time.

Measured on this estate, the designed timing breaks span 4.08–8.00 pips and the designed genuine breaks span 4.01–7.93 pips. The ranges overlap almost completely. Magnitude is not a discriminator, and a reconciliation built on current state has thrown away the one thing that is.

The worked case, and the shape is the whole argument

TRD-W12-0002, USD/CAD, at amendment 0. Risk looks like the outlier — it recorded at 15:24:54, before either of the others. Corrected for its own −112-second skew it observed at 15:26:46, the same true instant as trading, and the two agree to the last digit. Settlement observed 33 minutes later. Nobody is wrong.

The unit of analysis

Sixty breaks, or a hundred and twenty

Three systems give three pairwise comparisons per trade. Choosing the pair as the unit is the obvious reading and it is wrong — when settlement disagrees with trading, it also disagrees with risk.

over the 60 break trades, at amendment 0:
  pairwise comparisons exceeding 0.5 pip   120
  underlying divergences                    60
  RATIO                                   2.00

Every one of the 60 partitions the same way — two systems agree and one does not, 60 of 60, with no other shape present. Counting pairs reports that single fact twice, exactly and systematically. A middle office told it has 120 breaks staffs for twice the work.

This is rung 10's lesson arriving from the other side: there, averaging destroyed a signal; here, pairwise expansion manufactures one. Both are the unit of analysis chosen before the phenomenon was understood. The payload exposes no field a consumer can count to reach 120 — pairwise variance lives beneath a verdict as an explanation of it, never beside it as a peer.

The honest half of the decomposition

not-evaluable is an answer and zero is a claim

The obvious implementation revalues each system at its corrected instant from the tick series, subtracts, and calls the remainder residual. At the 2-pip threshold it misclassifies 22 of 60.

The cause is structural. The tick series resolves to five minutes and the observation gaps are frequently smaller, so two instants inside one bucket revalue identically and the timing component reads zero. Reading that as the market did not move says something the evidence cannot support — the truth is the series cannot see whether it did.

And a tick from the previous trading day is worse than none

TRD-W12-0124's two compared instants are 31 hours apart across a weekend, and the nearest preceding tick is 20.0 hours old. Revaluing against it yields a residual of −72.49 pips on a raw variance of 5.15. The product returns not-evaluable, names the reason, and carries the staleness — because a number with no meaning is worse than no number.

And the converse mattered just as much. A gap of exactly zero is not the same fact: two identical instants leave no interval, so the timing component is zero by arithmetic rather than by resolution, and no tick series is consulted at all. The first implementation collapsed the two cases and refused 29 of 60 breaks — which turned out to bury precisely the 20 the estate designates genuine, 20 of 20. That is CP12-F-10, and it is the kind of defect only building the thing finds.

A correction the product makes, not a clock the fabric keeps

The corrected instant reaches nothing beneath this product

Each system carries a recorded clockOffsetSeconds — trading 0, settlement +47, risk −112. Correcting a recorded instant by it recovers the instant that system actually observed at, and that correction is the entire product.

It is also domain knowledge about three named systems, and it belongs to the reconciliation and to nothing beneath it. The corrected instant is not a valid-time, not an observation time and not a supersession.

So it is kept out of the fabric's temporal machinery entirely

No valid_from, no observed_at, no invalidation boundary, no Context Debt term. What the λc table ages against is the instant the source recorded, exactly as D-IMPL-331 declares — because a corrected instant on that path would make the SDK's freshness figure a function of this product's own alignment model.

FXR-G24 proves it through the real L2 path — boot, ingest, read the claims back out of L3 — and asserts it on settlement and risk, where the two instants genuinely differ. Never on trading, whose offset is 0 and where a fabric that had swallowed the correction whole would still look correct.

Two figures, one of them bound

A production SLA and a demonstration value are not the same contract

Two half-life figures appear in this product's specification and a reader must never be able to take them for one.

PRODUCTION FRESHNESS REFERENCE    1 hour     prose in the spec, BOUND NOWHERE
CP-12's ILLUSTRATIVE HALF-LIFE    48 hours   ccf_config.yaml, and every served F

measured at as_of = 2026-08-14, two real trades:
  1 hour     F 0.2500   vs   F 0.2508      spread 0.0008
  48 hours   F 0.5151   vs   F 0.8999      spread 0.3848

One hour is right for a live intraday reconciliation and it is what this product would run at in production. It cannot be demonstrated here: the trade window is eight days wide, so at one hour every observation has aged to nothing, F is carried almost entirely by the non-decaying fxr_trade class, and the term reads the same for a trade booked four days ago and one booked hours ago. It has stopped measuring its own subject.

48 hours is not a production recommendation. It is a demonstration value under CLAUDE.md §9, disclosed beside the value in ccf_config.yaml, at §B.5.0 of the specification, in FINDINGS.md, in the README and in the summary.

And a gate keeps the document and the deployment from drifting apart

FXR-G28 does not read the config and stop — that would assert the declaration and not the use. It serves the same product against a 1-hour Control Plane document and against the shipped 48-hour one, pins all four figures above, and requires the served F to follow the binding. Rebinding ccf_config.yaml to 3600 turns it red.

The evidence

Twenty-eight gates, and five were green on the first attempt

Every gate was sabotaged. Twenty-five went red on their own assertion. Three could not be falsified from this deployment at all — an SDK guard refuses the bad state before an artifact can exist — and are recorded unavailable, never folded into passed.

A gate asserted only where two answers coincide proves nothing about which one produced them

Five gates were green under their first sabotage and all five are recorded. Four were weak sabotages — they broke code the gate never reached. One was a weak test: FXR-G12 asserted the declared pip table and the pair label, and stayed green while Estate.pip returned 0.0001 for every pair, because it never exercised the lookup. A declaration nothing reads is documentation, not control. It now recomputes every served variance from the raw rates and requires the worked cases to exercise both scales.

The six trap gates carry an extra obligation the others do not: each builds the defective mechanism in the test, runs it on the same data, and asserts the two answers differ. That rule exists because two earlier rungs shipped trap gates that were green under their own sabotage, each having asserted only where the correct and defective mechanisms happened to agree.

What to change first

One file, and it is the most instructive edit in the tree

  • artifacts/pip-scale.yaml. Add a currency pair with no entry and ask for it: the product refuses rather than defaulting to 0.0001. USD/JPY prices to 0.01, and defaulting is how a JPY-quoted variance comes to read a hundred times too small and pass every threshold there is — on 135 of the 500 trades in this estate.
  • COMPARE_AT=latest on TRD-W12-0002, to watch a real break disappear.
  • NO_ALIGN_CLOCKS=1 on TRD-W12-0004. The genuine break becomes not-evaluable, because without the correction the two instants differ by 47 seconds — below what the series can resolve.
  • The λc row in ccf_config.yaml, set to the 1-hour production reference, to watch F stop discriminating between two real trades.

Honest limits

What this example is not

  • No settlement, netting, ISO 20022 or CLS-cutoff leg. The article's schema names a SettlementInstruction entity and settlesVia / netsWith relationships. Measured, both predicates are absent from this estate's vocabulary — not present with a count of zero. The payload reports the leg absent, because a predicate nothing asserts is not a predicate asserting nothing.
  • No per-trade Part 43 record. CFTC Part 43 exists here as a distribution profile — 44 rows summarising 30,501 real DTCC observations. The trades are patterned on it. It is provenance and calibration, never an evidence leg.
  • Only two break classes, where the article specifies four. The estate labels timing and genuine. amendment-lag and revaluation-basis have no labels, and the product does not invent the two it cannot evidence.
  • The product over-reports genuine, and it was not tuned. Of 60 designated breaks, 14 computed timing and all 14 are designated timing — no false timing. But 37 computed genuine against 20 designated. The tick series and the system values were engineered by independent generators, so revaluation cannot reproduce a designed move over a long interval: the disagreement grows from ~2 pips at 400 s to 13.73 pips at 10,849 s. break_record is a designated sample and not the truth, so the evidence stands and the designation is carried beside the computed verdict rather than a cutoff being tuned until they agreed.
  • No three-way disagreement anywhere. Across all 500 trades at 0.01 pip: 440 all-agree, 60 exactly-two-agree, 0 three-way. In every break the two agreeing systems match to the last digit. That limb of FXR-G08 is recorded not-required, and the absence is asserted by the executing test rather than assumed.
  • Notionals are banded, never exact. Part 43 caps large notionals for anonymity. Every variance this product reports is in pips — a property of the rate and not of the size — and nothing reconstructs a number from a band.

Illustrative calibration. This example runs against a point-in-time demonstration estate rather than continuously refreshed production data. Freshness half-lives and related thresholds are calibrated to make the governed behavior observable in that snapshot. They demonstrate how the SDK applies these controls and should not be interpreted as recommended production coverage-policy thresholds.
