Orientation

Where one store stops being enough

Rungs 00 through 03 each read one store and answer a lookup. A corporate family cannot be either. Who owns whom lives in an ownership graph; what each company legally is lives in a reference table; and neither store holds the answer, because the answer is the join.

So this rung opens both on every ask, and the split is visible in the payload rather than in the architecture. Under the demonstration anchor, 2 of 22 members carry a reference record and 20 do not — and all 22 are served. A build that quietly returned only the two that resolve would hand back a smaller, tidier, wrong family that balanced perfectly.

What the failure actually is

An entity-master query returns every company it has a record for. What it cannot return is the companies it has no record for. An exposure roll-up drawn from it finds the parent and one subsidiary, sums cleanly, and is wrong about the shape of the group by a factor of eleven. A member with no identifier is still a member.

A walk, and the depth inside the seal

max_depth is the parameter this rung exists for, and three properties ride on it. Above the declared ceiling it is refused by name and never clamped; absent, it takes the declared default read from the Control Plane rather than invented at serve time; and two depths are two content addresses.

The dangerous version is not a wrong depth — it is a silent one. A build that clamped 8 to 6 would return a payload that looks entirely healthy, seals cleanly, and answers a question nobody asked.

Ontology, load-bearing in three places

Served — every member carries its FIBO class chain, so a consuming agent needs no second lookup. Projecting — the perspective conditions on FIBO class, not on a hand-written role map, which is why credit-officer details 22 members and kyc-analyst details 0 against the same family, and why the two seal differently. Validating — declared gates refuse an untyped node and an off-vocabulary predicate.

Struck, not quietly dropped

A fourth role — types constrain the walk — was struck from this rung on two independently measured grounds: no identifier-bearing anchor in this estate reaches a class-diverse node within the demonstrated depth, so a gate over it would go green having proven nothing; and the SDK's traversal family types the walk on edges only, so the stop has no expression to build against. Nothing on this page claims a type-constrained walk.

The product reproduces byte-identical against a quiescent ledger for that run. Refusals

Four ways this product declines, and each is correct

A depth above the ceiling is refused, never clamped. The request says 8, a clamping build walks 6, and the payload looks fine while the seal greens. That is the whole failure mode this rung is most exposed to, which is why the refusal names the bound rather than adjusting to it.

An as-of date is refused, never honored quietly. Reconstructing the family as it stood on a past date needs a temporal axis this tier does not have. The dangerous outcome is not the refusal — it is accepting the parameter and ignoring it, which answers a question about today while the caller believes they asked about last year.

An unresolvable anchor is a 404, never an empty family. An empty payload reads as this company has no subsidiaries, which is a claim about the world rather than about the request.

And the served jurisdiction vocabulary is closed in both directions. A family serving a code the document does not declare is refused; so is a document declaring a code no member serves. Most systems check only the first. A declared rule that fires on nothing looks identical to a working one, right up until the day it should have caught something.

Sixteen gates, and every one made to fail

A gate that has never been observed red is not evidence that it runs. Each of the sixteen was sabotaged under its own gate id and the observed result recorded as data in artifacts/gates.yaml — a sabotage that turns something red proves only that the suite noticed.

None of the sixteen passed its first sabotage, and that is not the same as saying the risk was absent. Several were rewritten before they were ever run, because the design pass measured that the obvious version could not discriminate. A gate asserting only “two depths are two content addresses” — the criterion's own wording — passes its own sabotage, because the applied depth is echoed into the payload and the payload is what is sealed. The roster limb exists because of that measurement.

A check that reads a token present in both the compliant and the violating case is measuring vocabulary, not content.

Scope

What this example is not

It is not a credit opinion. The estate is public reference and filing data used to exercise mechanism, together with planted synthetic ownership circles — the circular structures are constructed rather than observed.

It is not a directed descendant tree. The walk is an undirected neighborhood: it follows a declared edge in both directions, so depth here is hops through adjacency rather than generations of ownership. At the demonstrated depth the two shapes agree; at depth 2 they do not, and the specification says so rather than letting a reader assume otherwise.

It is not a complete group. The demonstration anchor was chosen because one anchor exercises everything this rung teaches — a family deep enough that depth changes the answer, the only cycle reachable from an identifier-bearing anchor anywhere in the estate, the two-store split on a subtree small enough to read, and the same company entered twice. No company in this estate carries a complete reference footprint.

And eleven gaps were raised here and none was closed. Among them: the graph adapter floors the read at a minimum transaction rather than pinning it, which is exactly why the reproducibility claim above is bounded to a quiescent ledger for that run; the egress builder cannot withhold a product resource, so a genuinely tool-only door is not expressible and the resource must refuse instead; and no node-class admission filter exists in the traversal family, which is half of why the fourth ontology role is struck. An executor surfaces gaps; it does not close them — no file under src/ or runtime/ was touched by this phase.
