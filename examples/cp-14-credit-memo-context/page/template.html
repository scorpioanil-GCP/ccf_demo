Rung 14 · Tier 5

Both outcomes are the product

At the memo's governed tolerance — 0.25, the v2 policy's default at the decision instant — three of the five producers withhold: the spread, the exposure roll-up and the covenant chain each measured more Context Debt than this memo will act on. The family and the collateral are admitted. The composite refuses on its required slots, with all five rows persisted and every producer's own reason retained.

At the labeled diagnostic tolerance — 1.0, a value nobody would act on — all five serve, all five are admitted under provider-controls, and the memo is served: D_c 1.0, fail_closed.

Nothing was tuned, and the served memo is not fit to act on

Inherited debt is the per-factor minimum. The exposure child's balances are days old against a four-hour half-life, so its freshness is zero and the memo's is too; the covenant child can check two citations in ten chain nodes, so completeness is 0.2. A memo that averaged would read as moderately stale. This one reads as what its inputs are.

Determining a question is not answering it

The covenant question names the obligor's own evidence nodes

The covenant child takes the evidence nodes it may read. Which nodes are this memo's is a fact about the obligor's own facility documents: the roster's (source_accession, source_document) joined to the ledger's ex:PassageAnchor nodes by ex:documentId. The mapping reads exactly that — two reads — and stops at the anchor: no chain, no citation, no cross-reference. The child answered exactly those sources.

The seam this exposed

The road hands the question mapping the requirement and the effective request and nothing else. A mapping that must read to determine a question has no seam for the deployment's resolved sources, so the mapping is an object the spine binds to opened.sources after opening — the settings the SDK already resolved, never resolved a second time. Six lines; recorded as friction (CP14-F-34), not worked around.

Composition, proven

Each section names the address it was composed from

Every memo section carries child_content_address, and that address equals the one the admission row retained — five for five. The memo's lineage is the set-union of its own operators with every child's; its expiry is its stalest child's.

A controlled variant, identified as such: the collateral child asked without haircuts, every other input fixed. Its section's coverage ratio and address move; the memo's address moves; the other four sections and their addresses do not.

With every producer process exited, replay resolves the five retained contracts from the consumer's own store, hands them to the memo's plan factory, re-executes the recorded operator path and reproduces c115f378…. The governed refusal reconstructs along its recorded no-read path with the two admitted addresses intact.

The evidence

Twelve gates executed, four sabotaged by hand

The roster declares exactly the ids the completion executes against the five real producers. Four mutations were run by hand and restored: a section composed from a constant, a child-owned read in the consumer's leg, the SDK's child resolver imported into the consumer, and the covenant question asked with no sources — each turned its gate red and was recorded with what went red beside it.

And seventeen gates are named as unexercised

The specification declares twenty-nine. The seventeen that name the memo's own unbuilt logic — template sections, evidence references, peer benchmarks, the policy-version tolerance, the diagnostic comparison — are listed in FINDINGS.md and claimed by nothing.

What to change first

A registration, not a line of code

  • producers: in ccf_config.yaml — point a slot at another producer of the same class. The lane floor and the identity are the only things the consumer holds it to; two slots were re-pointed at successors during this rung and the mapping did not change.
  • DEBT_TOLERANCE=--debt-tolerance=1.0 — the diagnostic. Watch three ineligible rows become admitted and the memo serve fail_closed.
  • MemoQuestions in deployment.py — ask a slot a different question; the SDK admits or refuses the answer the same way.

Honest limits

What this example is not

  • Not a served memo at the governed tolerance. No producer's balances on this estate are fresh enough; the serving path is reached only at the labeled diagnostic, and it serves fail_closed.
  • Not the memo's own assembly. Template sections, evidence references, peer benchmarks, the relationship summary and the policy-version tolerance default remain unbuilt and are named as such.
  • No producer replay reference on a refused row (D-IMPL-342): the consumer retains the producer's reason, never its record's address.
  • The exposure slot is an escape hatch. Its producer is a frozen specimen behind a binding adapter over the SDK's standard door; the aggregation stays where it was, and the paved road's coverage limitation stands.
  • The replay environment is hand-assembled in the test — the fourth copy of the same forty lines across the paved trees.
