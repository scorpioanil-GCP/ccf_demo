## The findings

Each is executable at `tests/examples/test_s1_01_governed_resolver_adoption.py` as an
`xfail(strict=True)` whose reason carries the finding id, so the roster fails today because the
seam is absent and fails **loudly** — `XPASS` under `strict` — the day a seam lands without its
finding being closed. Verified not vacuous: each of the five tests fails at its own assertion,
and adding `seeds=request_seeds(request)` to the resolver turns `24.10-F-07` into
`XPASS(strict)` while the other four stay `xfailed` (sabotage applied and reverted;
`src/ccf/products/resolver.py` carries no executable change).

### `24.10-F-06` — a class using one of the whitepaper's four operator categories cannot be served

`GovernedResolver.__call__` calls `build_operator_registry` with no
`similarity_index_version`. That builder **requires** it exactly when a similarity artifact is
present and refuses otherwise (`D-IMPL-38`: *"an unversioned index is one that cannot be
replayed against"*). There is no field on `GovernedResolver`, no argument on
`build_governed_resolver`, and the registry is built inside `__call__` — so a deployment has
nowhere to supply it. Measured unbridged, against a real fabric:

```
ValueError: `parameters` carries a similarity artifact but no similarity_index_version
was supplied … (D-IMPL-38)   [src/ccf/deployment/operators.py:354]
```

**This stops adoption at step 4 of the composition's own nine**, before Π runs. Similarity is
one of whitepaper §7.4's four canonical categories, so this is not an exotic wiring.

### `24.10-F-07` — the request does not seed the traversal, and `DI-15.5-07` is reproduced

`GovernedResolver.__call__` executes Π with no `seeds`. `ccf.replay.engine:790` replays with
`seeds=request_seeds(path.request)`. Two consequences, the second worse than the first:

1. Two questions about two different companies compile to the **same** graph query and pin
   identically — measured above, and exactly the repudiation gap `D-IMPL-111` limb (a) closed
   on the hand-built path. An auditor asking *"which question did this decision answer?"*
   cannot tell Acme from Beta from the record alone.
2. Every product served through this composition whose plan walks is **replay-divergent by
   construction**, because serve-time and replay-time seed by different rules —
   `request_seeds`' own docstring names this as the failure it exists to prevent.

This one is an SDK correctness defect independent of `s1-01`.

### `24.10-F-08` — three Track-B product members have no seam

`obligation_in_force`, `source_refresh_recency` and `debt_evidence` are set by the hand-built
spine and by nothing in `GovernedResolver`'s candidate construction. All three are **inside the
product's content address** (`D-IMPL-112` gaps 2 and 3, `D-IMPL-117`, `D-IMPL-118`), so they
cannot be attached afterwards. The measured consequence is the Q3 obligation row above.

`debt_evidence` matched only because `s1-01` declares none by ruling (`D-IMPL-119` limb 3(b));
a deployment that populated it would meet the same wall.

### `24.10-F-09` — the decision instant is collapsed into the graph boundary

`expires_at`, the gate instant and `recorded_at` all derive from `request.asked_at`. `s1-01`
declares `decision_instant` as a **required** argument on its request contract and runs the TTL
from it, five minutes after the boundary the graph is read at. Under the resolver nothing reads
it: the TTL runs from the wrong instant and the record is stamped at the wrong one.

### `24.10-F-10` — the SDK's canonical worked example was dark for three phases

Every assertion in `tests/examples/test_s1_01_output.py` — 33 markers, 36 test outcomes — has
carried `xfail(strict=True)` since `24.7` on one reason, which named its own expiry:
*"un-quarantine and rerun unchanged after D-GATE-24.7"*. That gate closed; `24.8` and `24.9`
went by; nothing scheduled the un-quarantine, and a strict xfail reports green. The cause was
one call site and one required keyword-only argument — the third clause of `D-IMPL-261`'s own
test for when an example may change, *"the SDK surface it legitimately consumes was
deliberately changed"*. Repaired at this plan: **39 passed, 0 xfailed, 0 skipped**, with no
assertion weakened (the whole diff is 132 deleted decorator lines and one docstring paragraph).

Classified as **procedural**, not an SDK gap. It is recorded because it is the reason the `R-03`
measurement nearly had no before-state to compare against: a comparison against a program that
does not run is not a comparison.

## Observed and deliberately not numbered

The design-E2 operator-set binder (`refuse_operator_set_divergence`, `D-IMPL-86`) has no place
on the canonical path: `GovernedResolver` takes `required` from the class declaration and hands
it to `plan_factory`, so the projection's own `projection-requirements.yaml` is never consulted
and the two authored documents are never held against each other. Whether a deployment's
`plan_factory` can make that comparison itself is a **structural reading of two signatures and
was not measured live**, so it is recorded here as an observation rather than promoted into the
findings roster.
