# 15.6-06 — debt points at what it summarises

Plan 15.5-11 put Context Debt on the wire. `D-IMPL-112` then recorded what a
reader still could not do with it: a KYC analyst shown `D_c=0.221 /
forced_llm / F=0.803` cannot act on that alone, and the first of the three
things missing was **which specific observations contributed the decay**. Plan
15.6-05 gave `Observation` the identity a pointer could name. This plan gives
debt the pointer.

`D-IMPL-117` fixes what the SDK owes here and what it does not: *"the SDK owes
the **structure and the API to populate and extract** each, and owes **none of
the content**"*. So the shape ships; which observations are worth pointing at
stays the context product creator's judgement.

Two rulings shape what an adopter sees, and they pull in different directions.

- **`D-IMPL-118` (`qa-toplevel`)** placed the carrier as a **new top-level
  field on `ContextProductContract`**, a sibling of `debt` rather than a member
  of `DebtAssessment`. That was **not** the cheapest option and the ruling says
  so: `qa-nested` was measured cheapest — address moves, *"rebuilt bytes are
  byte-identical to the delivered bytes"*, **0** rehydrator implementations
  changing — and was declined, *"forgo[ing] that free ride deliberately, buying
  the field its own identity on the vessel rather than making it a rider on
  debt."*
- **`D-IMPL-119` (`qb2-scoped`, clarified in four limbs)** found evidence
  pointers **entitlement-sensitive**, found the shipped assembly path **not
  principal-scoped**, and put closing that gap out of scope for this phase.
  Under its limb 3 this plan had to choose between opaque handles and no
  pointers at all. **It emits no pointers** — see below.

## The content address moves, and that is the ruled change

`ContextProductContract` gained a hashed field, so **every content address
recorded before this plan differs from the one the same product produces now.**
`D-IMPL-117` authorized that in advance — *"surface impact is accepted … the
third product-content-address move [is an] authorized cost, not [a] risk to be
designed around"* — so it is a ruled cost, not a defect.

Unlike 15.5-11's move, this one is **not** reconstructible for free.
`ProjectionOutput` carries exactly `(plan_id, payload)`, so a `(record,
projection)` rehydrator can produce a top-level vessel field only if the record
carries it. `D-IMPL-118` therefore obliges **both** halves, and both landed in
one commit: a matching `debt_evidence` member on `ContentGenerationPath`, and
the read-back in every shipped `rehydrate_product` — the Protocol contract in
`src/ccf/replay/engine.py` plus all seven concrete implementations across
`tests/replay/test_engine_rehydration.py`,
`tests/replay/test_query_sequence_pin.py`, `perf/suites/replay/conftest.py`,
`examples/06-decision-replay/run.py` and
`examples/external/00-hello-context/run.py`.

**That the second half is load-bearing is a run result, not an inference.**
Deleting the single read-back line from
`tests/replay/test_engine_rehydration.py::_Environment.rehydrate_product` turns
**8 of that module's 30 cases** red with `ReplayDivergence` — including the
`debt` and `request` landings that predate this plan. The measurement is
recorded in a comment at the line so a later reader does not have to re-run it
to believe it.

The claim is proven on the real serve path rather than asserted:
`tests/replay/test_engine_rehydration.py::test_what_the_debt_pointed_at_replays_byte_exactly`
serves a pointer-bearing product, ingests a later envelope so the graph has
moved on four months, replays, and recomputes **both** addresses — the product
against the one the record names, and the record against the one the store
resolved it from. Its two adversarial twins show the failure modes are
detected rather than tolerated:
`test_a_rehydration_that_drops_the_evidence_is_divergence`, and — not implied
by it — `test_a_rehydration_that_swaps_one_pointer_is_divergence`, which rules
out an implementation treating the carrier as a count rather than as bytes
inside the address.

**What an adopter with recorded addresses must do.** Records written before
this plan replay unchanged: their products carried no pointers, their
dimension 5 carries the `NO_DEBT_EVIDENCE` sentinel, and the two agree —
asserted by `test_a_record_written_before_the_evidence_seam_still_replays`.
Addresses recorded *outside* the replay record — in a fixture, a transcript, an
external system's index — must be re-recorded for products assembled after this
plan.

**Exactly one committed artifact in this repository pins an affected address,
and it was regenerated by running the example rather than by editing a hash:**

| Artifact | Value pinned | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `examples/08-e2e-demo/artifacts/expected_output.txt` | replay-record address (elided to a 16-hex prefix) | `374f9f4dd68056fb…` | `06e454f49cc89712…` |

The re-derived census in plan 15.6-01's summary is what that count comes from,
not memory: 19 files hold address literals across the swept roots, 124
full-length and 2 elided, and **1** moves. Every site the census classifies as
*does not move* was confirmed by running the full suite rather than assumed,
and none was regenerated.

**Two census findings worth recording, because both are places a reader would
otherwise infer the wrong mechanism.**

1. The `skill-set in force` hash on the line below the one that moved
   (`aafa91413bcd8b21…`) **did not move**, and the census predicted it would.
   The prediction was correct for `qa-nested`, where `DebtAssessment` itself
   reshapes and everything carrying one moves with it. Under the ruled
   `qa-toplevel` shape the assessment is **byte-identical**, so only the record
   address — which carries `ContentGenerationPath`, which gained the member —
   moves. The census's count of one file holds; its mechanism does not.
2. The product content address moves everywhere and is pinned as a literal in
   **no** committed artifact in this tree. `expected_output.txt` prints the
   *replay-record* address, not the product's — a distinction the census
   surfaced and this plan confirmed by regeneration.

## The evidence pointers are not populated, and that is a ruling

`D-IMPL-119` limb 3 gives this plan exactly two paths. It takes the second,
quoted verbatim: **"(b) omit evidence pointers entirely until a later phase
adds principal scoping to the assembly path."**

Why (b) and not (a), stated so a reader does not have to reconstruct it. Limb 1
makes the safety property **the SDK's**, not the adopter's — the ruling declined
`qb2-opaque` precisely because that *"moves a real disclosure question onto
every adopter, and the SDK cannot then say the surface is safe by
construction"*. Limb 4 then refuses a handle that is merely *encoded* rather
than genuinely non-revealing: *"an obfuscated `entity_uri` is an interpretable
pointer with an extra step."* Limb 2 puts principal scoping of the assembly path
out of scope for this phase. The only inputs in hand at the shipped population
site are the assembled `EntityClaim`s, so every handle derivable there is either
interpretable — refused by limb 4 — or has had its referent discarded, which is
a pointer that points at nothing wearing the costume of one that does. The named
sentinel says the same thing without the costume.

The decision is written where an adopter will read it, not only here:
`examples/s1-01-hello-context-product/deployment.py::KycScenario.debt_evidence`
carries the reasoning and the quoted clause, and the construction site in that
example's `spine.py` names the limb beside the field.

**What this does not assert.** That scoped derivation is unwanted — the ruling
forecloses that reading itself: *"it does not license a later plan inferring,
from limb 2, that scoped derivation is unwanted; limb 1 says the opposite."*
The carrier ships with its replay seam, so the phase that closes the scoping gap
populates one method body and changes no surface.

**Why nothing here varies by principal, recorded as an interaction rather than
a new question.** `D-IMPL-118` puts the carrier inside the content address.
Principal-dependent contents there would give two differently-entitled
principals different addresses for the same assembly — the coupling `D-IMPL-105`
dissolved when it kept debt pure over `(factors, profile)`. Neither an omitted
pointer nor an opaque handle varies by principal, which is what keeps that from
arising.

## No `ccf.hitl` re-export moved, and the plan expected four

Plan 15.6-06's own text budgeted for four consequential `ccf.hitl.*` entries,
on 15.5-11's precedent: `EscalationHandOff.trigger` may be a `DebtAssessment`,
so when the assessment reshapes, the hand-off's canonical bytes move with it and
each spelling needs its own entry reading *"consequential, not deliberate"*.

**That budget was written for the placement `D-IMPL-118` declined.** Under
`qa-toplevel` the assessment gains no field and is byte-identical, so nothing
carrying one moves. A generated-manifest diff of the current tree against the
committed manifest shows **zero** `ccf.hitl` movement, and claiming four
consequential entries would have been a false claim in a migration note — the
one thing a curated log cannot afford. The four names still appear in the
frozen-baseline diff's `reshaped` partition, where 15.5-11's fragment already
claims them for the reshape that did happen.

## ccf.halflife.assessment.DebtEvidencePointer

- Surface: ccf.halflife.assessment.DebtEvidencePointer
- Public surface: true
- Before: absent — debt reached a consumer as a scalar, three factors and a set
  of scorer pins, with no way to say what any of it summarised
- After: a `CCFContract` with one field, `handle: str` — an opaque identifier
  for the thing pointed at, in the `ccf.skills.catalog.SkillRequirements` voice:
  *opaque to the SDK; declared by the deployment*. The SDK validates structure
  (type, uniqueness, canonical order) and validates nothing about meaning; it
  ships no entity-URI type, no claim-id type, no vocabulary and no default. A
  `float` is refused at construction by the module's shared `_refuse_float`
  rather than coerced
- Change type: added
- Why: `D-IMPL-112` named *which observations contributed the decay* as the
  first thing a reader of a debt figure cannot get at, and `D-IMPL-117` scoped
  the SDK's obligation to the carrying structure alone. A single opaque field
  is the whole structure that obligation supports; anything richer would be the
  vocabulary that ruling assigns away from the SDK
- Migration: none — new name, nothing referenced it. **Read
  `D-IMPL-119` before populating one:** until the assembly path is
  principal-scoped, a handle a reading principal can interpret is refused by
  that entry, whatever the deployment's own judgement

## ccf.halflife.assessment.DebtEvidence

- Surface: ccf.halflife.assessment.DebtEvidence
- Public surface: true
- Before: absent
- After: the annotated carrier type — a `tuple[DebtEvidencePointer, ...]`
  validated to be **handle-unique** and **canonically ordered**. A repeated
  handle is refused at construction with a message saying why two pointers to
  one thing is an incoherent record rather than a stricter one
- Change type: added
- Why: an alias rather than a field-by-field re-declaration because the carrier
  lands in **two** places — the product vessel and replay dimension 5 — and one
  validated shape is what keeps those from drifting apart. A sorted tuple rather
  than a `frozenset`, deliberately and against the `SkillRequirements`
  precedent: the value is inside a content address, so it must be
  order-insensitive in its bytes (which sorting gives, and
  `tests/halflife/test_debt.py::test_declaration_order_does_not_move_the_bytes`
  asserts), but a set would additionally collapse a repeated handle **silently**
  — and the repetition is exactly the incoherence worth naming out loud. This is
  `DebtAssessment.scorers`' own resolution, applied for its own reason
- Migration: none — new name

## ccf.halflife.assessment.NO_DEBT_EVIDENCE

- Surface: ccf.halflife.assessment.NO_DEBT_EVIDENCE
- Public surface: true
- Before: absent
- After: the empty tuple, as a named module-level constant whose docstring
  states the positive claim *this assessment was computed without evidence
  pointers*
- Change type: added
- Why: `D-IMPL-38`, and the reason this fabric's own
  `docs/surface-changes/15.5-11-debt-delivery.md` gives for

- Migration: none — it is the default on both fields that carry the carrier, so
  every construction site written before this plan asserts exactly what it
  always asserted

## ccf.products.contract.ContextProductContract

- Surface: ccf.products.contract.ContextProductContract
- Public surface: true
- Before: ten fields, ending at 15.5-11's `debt` — which said how much to trust
  the product's contents and nothing about what that judgement rested on
- After: one further field, `debt_evidence: DebtEvidence`, defaulted to
  `NO_DEBT_EVIDENCE`. A **sibling** of `debt`, not a member of it
- Change type: reshaped
- Why: `D-IMPL-118`, which chose `qa-toplevel` over the cheaper nested placement
  and recorded the reason — the field buys *"its own identity on the vessel
  rather than [being] a rider on debt"*. On the vessel rather than beside it for
  15.5-11's door reason, unchanged: all three shipped delivery paths serialize
  `servable.product` and none reads `ServedProduct.replay_ref`, so a placement
  beside the vessel would reach an in-process caller and no wire consumer at all
- Migration: **nothing is required to keep constructing products** — the
  sentinel default keeps every existing site saying what it always said — and
  **something is required if you recorded addresses outside the replay record**;
  see "The content address moves" above. A custom `ReplayEnvironment` **must**
  be updated regardless of whether you populate the field; see
  `ccf.replay.engine.ReplayEnvironment` below

## ccf.replay.record.ContentGenerationPath

- Surface: ccf.replay.record.ContentGenerationPath
- Public surface: true
- Before: eight fields, ending at 15.5-13's `request`
- After: one further field, `debt_evidence: DebtEvidence`, defaulted to
  `NO_DEBT_EVIDENCE`
- Change type: reshaped
- Why: this member is **the price of the ruled placement**, not a symmetry.
  Because the carrier is top-level on the vessel rather than nested inside the
  assessment, it does not ride `debt` back through replay; `ProjectionOutput`
  carries exactly `(plan_id, payload)`, so without this field no implementation
  of the `(record, projection)` signature can produce the value and every replay
  of every product raises `ReplayDivergence`. Dimension 5 and not a sixth
  dimension: `INV-CH7-80` holds, asserted by field enumeration in
  `test_the_evidence_lands_on_dimension_five_and_mints_no_sixth`. Beside the
  `debt` it explains, for that field's own reason — the value a rehydrator reads
  in order to reproduce the address sits next to the address it must reproduce
- Migration: none for construction. `NO_DEBT_EVIDENCE` is the positive
  assertion *this decision pointed at nothing*, so a record written before this
  seam parses and verifies unchanged

## ccf.replay.record.build_replay_record

- Surface: ccf.replay.record.build_replay_record
- Public surface: true
- Change type: behavior
- Before: the record's dimension 5 said nothing about what a debt pointed at
- After: **the same signature.** Dimension 5's `debt_evidence` is *derived from
  the `product` argument the builder was already handed* — no new parameter
- Why: derivation rather than threading is what keeps every
  `build_replay_record` consumer byte-for-byte unchanged, and it makes a record
  pinning evidence its product does not carry **unrepresentable** rather than
  merely discouraged. It is exactly the rule 15.5-11 applied to `debt`: a fact
  already present is read, never re-passed
- Migration: **none.** No call site changes. A caller that wants evidence on the
  record puts it on the product

## ccf.replay.engine.ReplayEnvironment

- Surface: ccf.replay.engine.ReplayEnvironment
- Public surface: true
- Change type: behavior
- Before: a conforming `rehydrate_product(*, record, projection)` had to carry
  `record.content_generation_path.debt` onto the product it returned (15.5-11)
- After: **the same signature** — which is why this is `behavior` rather than
  `reshaped`. What changed is the **contract**: it must now *also* carry
  `record.content_generation_path.debt_evidence`
- Why: `ContextProductContract.debt_evidence` is inside the hashed body, and it
  is **not** reachable through `debt` — the ruled placement made it a sibling of
  the assessment, not a member. `canonical_bytes` walks a model field-by-field
  over `type(value).model_fields`, so an implementation that omits it returns a
  product `ccf.replay.engine::replay`'s address check rejects with
  `ReplayDivergence`
- Migration: **required for any adopter with a custom `ReplayEnvironment`, even
  if you never populate the field, and it is one line.** Add
  `debt_evidence=record.content_generation_path.debt_evidence` to the
  `ContextProductContract(...)` your `rehydrate_product` returns. All seven
  implementations across six files in this repository were updated in the same
  commit (`src/ccf/replay/engine.py`, `tests/replay/test_engine_rehydration.py`,
  `tests/replay/test_query_sequence_pin.py`, `perf/suites/replay/conftest.py`,
  `examples/06-decision-replay/run.py`,
  `examples/external/00-hello-context/run.py`). **Do not re-derive the pointers
  at replay** — they are a deployment's judgement about a particular assembly,
  not a function of the record, so a rehydrator that rebuilt them would be
  returning a different decision wearing this one's identifier. An adopter who
  omits the line does not get a silent wrong answer; they get
  `ReplayDivergence`, which is the failure mode this fabric prefers
