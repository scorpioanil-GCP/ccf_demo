# D-IMPL-309 — the store head leaves the sealed answer

> **On the filename.** The `24.11-` prefix is the assembler's ordering key,
> not a claim about which phase this is. `D-IMPL-309` was ruled on the
> `example-modernization` branch after `D-GATE-24.11` closed, under the
> modernization pass `D-IMPL-305` chartered; the pass carries `MOD-NN` finding
> ids rather than a phase number, and `tools/assemble_surface_changes.py`
> globs `[0-9]*-*.md`, so a fragment named for its decision alone would be
> invisible to the assembler and to the drift gate that shares its reading.
> The `24.6-D-IMPL-NNN-` fragments are the precedent for the shape.

`MOD-05` measured two runs of one governed question — same subject, same
declared instants, same policy — whose served products were byte-identical
but for the substrate's transaction-time head, `74342` against `74409`. The
head sat inside `ProductLineage`, `ProductLineage` sits inside the content
address, and a ledger advancing because of activity unrelated to the question
therefore minted a new product identity. The ladder's central claim is *same
question → same answer*.

Dave's ruling: **"Logical determination identity is addressed. Physical store
furniture is recorded."** The head is execution provenance. It is recorded
authoritatively on replay dimension 1 (`ContextFabricSnapshot`), derived there
from the execution rather than from the product, compared at replay, and
reached through the product's replay reference. It is removed from the three
sealed models that carried it.

## Why removed rather than excluded from the hash

The first implementation added a fourth `ccf_hash_transparent` posture —
*ambient*: never rendered, at any value — and marked the three fields with it.
It was backed out on measurement, and the measurement is the reason this entry
exists at all:

```
original pin : substrate='fluree' pin='74342'
roundtripped : substrate='none'   pin='no-store-snapshot-pin'
```

`canonical_bytes` is at once the address input, the persisted representation
and the wire representation. A field omitted from it is therefore not
*excluded from identity* — it is **erased**, and read back as its sentinel.
For `store_snapshot_pin` that sentinel is the positive assertion *this
substrate exposes no native head*, so every product crossing the door would
have arrived stating something false about its own evidence; on the two
required, defaultless sites the round trip failed validation outright. A
`ccf_hash_transparent: true` field never had this problem because its omitted
value re-validates to exactly the value it had — omission is
information-preserving by construction, which is what "silence equals
yesterday" means.

The alternative — hashing an identity subset while serializing everything —
was rejected: it would give a product two addresses and break
`content_address = sha256(canonical_bytes(product))`, which is worth more than
keeping a store-head field on the vessel.

## What did NOT change

- **`ContextFabricSnapshot.store_snapshot_pin` is untouched**, including its
  `ccf_hash_transparent: true` mark. A replay record is about one execution,
  and two executions that read different heads are two executions. This is
  where the evidence lives and it is unchanged.
- **`ExecutionResult.store_snapshot_pin` is untouched.** The engine still
  reads the head once before its first query and carries it; what changed is
  only that the resolver no longer copies it onto the vessel.
- **`replay()`'s pin comparison is untouched and still fires.**
  `reproduced_snapshot` is built by `snapshot_of_execution` from the
  re-execution, never from the rehydrated product, so removing the vessel
  field cannot reach it. Proven by
  `tests/replay/test_engine_rehydration.py::test_a_rehydration_that_moves_the_pin_is_divergence`,
  whose vessel seam is withdrawn by ruling and whose dimension-1 seam is
  unchanged.
- **A *declared* snapshot is a different thing.** A pin the caller asks for is
  a semantic input to the determination and is addressed like any other
  declared context. This ruling reaches only a head an adapter merely
  observed.

## ccf.products.contract.ProductLineage

- Surface: ccf.products.contract.ProductLineage
- Public surface: true
- Change type: reshaped
- Before: four fields — `operators_invoked`, `policy_bundle_versions`,
  `graph_snapshot_at`, and `store_snapshot_pin: StoreSnapshotPin =
  NO_STORE_SNAPSHOT_PIN` added by `24.5-03` under `24.5-L-02`
- After: the three fields the Phase-15 baseline froze. The head is not here;
  it is on replay dimension 1
- Why: `D-IMPL-309`, on `MOD-05` — an ambient ledger head inside the address
  made the same governed question seal to two addresses
- Migration: a deployment that passed `store_snapshot_pin=` to `ProductLineage`
  is refused by name (`extra=forbid`) rather than having the value dropped,
  and reads the head from the replay record instead. Deployments that seal
  whole-vessel content addresses re-seal them once — and get address stability
  across unrelated ledger movement in exchange. This returns the signature to
  the frozen baseline's, so the name leaves the diff entirely; the round trip
  is `24.5-03` out and this entry back

## ccf.products.contract.ComputedEmptyFinding

- Surface: ccf.products.contract.ComputedEmptyFinding
- Public surface: true
- Change type: reshaped
- Before: five fields, including a required `store_snapshot_pin:
  StoreSnapshotPin` so an examiner could re-issue the same search against the
  same state
- After: four — `operators_returned_empty`, `operators_invoked`, `plan_id`,
  `emptiness`. `ComputedEmptyFinding.of()` no longer reads
  `execution.store_snapshot_pin`
- Why: `D-IMPL-309` — two identical searches under two ambient heads are one
  finding, not two; the state searched is recorded on dimension 1, which the
  product's replay reference resolves
- Migration: the keyword is refused by name; read the head from the record

## ccf.products.refusal.RefusalEvidence

- Surface: ccf.products.refusal.RefusalEvidence
- Public surface: true
- Change type: reshaped
- Before: `operators_invoked`, `plan_id`, a required `store_snapshot_pin`, and
  the two optional members. The pin was deliberately defaultless so that "a
  pin defaulted here would let a caller build evidence that quietly claims a
  substrate exposed no head when in fact the caller forgot to say"
- After: the pin is gone. That reasoning was right and is why the field could
  not simply be given a default — it was answered by moving the field rather
  than by defaulting it, and the guarantee now lives where the head is
  recorded per execution
- Why: `D-IMPL-309` — a refusal's canonical bytes are its address, its stored
  form and its wire form, and a head sealed there made two identical searches
  two refusals
- Migration: the keyword is refused by name; the shell's `replay_reference`
  resolves the record that carries the head
