# Launch bar

**Behavioral baseline: `dc7e8177`** (`main`, post-`D-GATE-341`). `D-IMPL-304`, `D-IMPL-342`
and `D-IMPL-341` are closed; nothing here reopens them. This page decides what remains
*necessary* for launch. It builds nothing and runs nothing.

## The release objective

> Developers can publish, acquire, compose, and reconstruct governed Context Products through
> supported SDK interfaces with reasonable effort. Completing the full credit-memo application
> is not required.

Three distinctions govern every row below. **Optional example enrichment** is not an **SDK
guarantee still needing proof**. **Missing test coverage** is not **demonstrated incorrect
behavior**. And **a completed repair phase** is not **overall release readiness**: three phases
closed cleanly on `dc7e8177`, and that establishes the baseline's behavior, not the release's.
No item is marked deferrable merely because no current example triggers it; each is judged
against whether the supported contract above permits the condition.

## What Dave decides

1. **Which road is supported for producers with a post-traversal aggregation** — both roads, or hold for a paved seam (`CP14-F-33`).
2. **Whether replay reconstruction ships as a documented recipe or a supported seam** (`CP06C-F-01`).
3. **Whether 341-1 and 341-2 ship as stated limitations or earn controls first.**
4. **Whether the governed nine of CP-14's unclaimed gates are covered by their SDK-level controls for launch purposes, or must be exercised through CP-14.**
5. **The disposition rule for standing reds** — which are carried with reasons and which block the tag.

## The final qualification step

None of the above is a tag. The tag follows, in this order: **a fixed release candidate** (one SHA, no
further behavioral commits); **the full applicable test run** against it, legs run directly and the
estate suites against `.76`; and **explicit disposition of every failure and of every required check
that could not execute** — by node id, with a reason, none inferred from an earlier record. A green
scoped package on `dc7e8177` is the baseline for that step, not a substitute for it.
