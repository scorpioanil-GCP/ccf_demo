# Peering and wire Protocols

Peering is the cross-boundary surface: Context Products exchanged between processes or between
organizations. The Context Product Contract travels intact across it, and an inbound product is
not admissible until the consuming Control Plane has verified the policy bindings it carries.
Identity and signing are pluggable because a deployment's PKI is its own.
