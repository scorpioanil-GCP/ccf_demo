**One-liner.** cp-04 answers over MCP through a tool that takes the caller's own
`lei` and `max_depth`, while the product resource beside it is registered,
enumerable, and refuses by name — and the two callers seal one request to one
content address, `205ae065…`, from a single standalone assembler neither of them
owns.

## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 1 — the plan's citation form is the one `D-IMPL-114` forbids]

Task 1's action asks the module docstring to cite `egress.py` **by line**. The
unanchored-citation ratchet has a ceiling of **0** and `D-IMPL-114` rules
`path.py::Symbol`. The docstrings cite
`runtime/ccf_runtime/mcp/egress.py::build_egress_server`,
`::ProductNotServable` and `::PRODUCT_URI_TEMPLATE`, all of which resolve. The
ratchet stayed at **0**, counted rather than inferred from a passing test. Same
finding plan `24.4-08` recorded as its deviation 5.

### 2. [Rule 3 — one door cannot be driven twice] `StreamableHTTPSessionManager` refuses a second run

The first draft used a module-scoped door fixture and drove each surface in its
own lifespan. Measured against `mcp==1.28.1`: `.run()` raises
*"can only be called once per instance"*, and `FastMCP` holds one manager per
server, so four of the module's tests errored.

**Fix:** every surface of ONE door is driven inside ONE lifespan, which is the
shape `tests/crossproc/test_mcp_egress.py::_both_surfaces_in_process` already
records and is strictly better than the alternative — a module that rebuilt the
door between surfaces would be asserting each thing about a different server.

### 3. [Rule 3 — the client's own view of the ASGI app] `.invalid` is refused by a Host guard

The first driver addressed the door at the `.invalid` authority the deployment
advertises. `FastMCP` installs a transport-security `Host` guard and answered
**`421 Misdirected Request`** to every call — which arrives as
`McpServerUnreachable` and reads exactly like a door refusing. The client
addresses `http://127.0.0.1:8124` (the SDK's own constant for this), and the
shipped door's advertised `resource_server_url` stays a `.invalid` name.
Recorded because a silent `421` would have made the whole module green while
measuring nothing.

### 4. [Rule 2 — a mitigation with no check] two threat-register entries had no assertion

`T-24.4-27` and `T-24.4-28` carry `mitigate` in the plan's own register and
nothing in the plan's task list asserted either. Both are correctness
requirements under Rule 2, so both got a test; `T-24.4-28`'s needed the
non-default role to be observable at all, and saying so is half of what the test
is worth.

### 5. [Rule 2 — the collaborator wiring is restated, and a comment would not hold it]

`server/compose.py` restates the three collaborators `transport.py` builds — the
scenario-capturing `read`, the `claims` closure, the per-run scratch root —
rather than sharing them. Sharing would have meant the MCP door importing the
`ProductClient` transport it does not use, and passing a perspective the shipped
adapter takes from the deployment rather than from a door. The duplication is
held by the two-caller equivalence comparison rather than by a comment, and both
docstrings say so.
