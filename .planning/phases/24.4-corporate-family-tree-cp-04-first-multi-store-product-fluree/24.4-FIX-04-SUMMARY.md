## What the ruling asked for, and what was built

`D-IMPL-234` names three limbs. All three are discharged, and none of them was
mine to decide.

**Limb (1) — the binder is not called.** `produce.py::_project` still does not
call `refuse_operator_set_divergence`, and was never edited. Verified:
`git diff --quiet -- examples/cp-04-corporate-family-tree/produce.py` returns
clean.

**Limb (2) — the detector learns the premise by POSITIVE CONDITIONS.** The new
route is
`tests/products/test_gate_two_call_shape.py::_supplied_by_the_product_class`,
whose docstring names its conditions and cites `D-IMPL-234`. It reads Dave's
wording — *"the plan/binding names the one projection and `required` is
supplied rather than authored twice"* — as three facts, each one a fact a
**two-document deployment would not exhibit**:

| # | Condition | Read from | A two-document deployment instead… |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | registers **no** `ccf.projection-requirements` | the artifact tree, via `_registers_requirements` (checked by the caller, `_one_document`) | registers one — that IS the binder's premise |
| 2 | the one authored side **exists**: the reserved `product_class` section carries a non-empty operator set | `ccf_config.yaml`, via the new `_config_declares_operator_set` | authors its set in the requirements artifact |
| 3 | **every** plan the body constructs reads `required=` from that declaration's own attribute — at least one, none from elsewhere | the AST, via `_constructed_plan_sources` | builds its plan from the requirements document |

Condition 3 is the "supplied, not authored twice" limb, and it is what separates
the exemption from `D-IMPL-137` **row C**: `required=tuple(scenario.operator_parameters)`
is a second set authored where the Control Plane cannot see it, and it stays red.

**Why the detector had to be taught rather than the list appended to** — and
this is the substantive finding of the plan. `cp-00` and `s1-00` author one
document and read it twice *in the same body*, so `_class_claim` and
`_plan_sources` both land. cp-04 projects in `produce.py::_project` and
assembles its `ProductLineage` in a **sibling function**, so the in-body claim
is absent — measured: `_class_claim` returned `None`, and `_one_document`'s
fail-closed default refused it. **That default was correct and was not
relaxed.** The new route supplies the missing evidence positively; it is an
extra way to answer `True`, never a loosened way to avoid answering `False`.

**Limb (3) — the roster.** `RULED_ONE_DOCUMENT_DEPLOYMENTS` went from two
members to three, adding `examples/cp-04-corporate-family-tree` with a
`D-IMPL-234` citation in the comment beside it, the way the existing members
cite theirs. **No other name was added.**
