**Four bundles, each INLINED OVER STDIN.** Two reasons, both measured on this
host and both recorded in this project's standing memory:

1. **A single argv string caps at `MAX_ARG_STRLEN` (128KB).** Every bundle

   produced **0 bytes** — which reads exactly like a clean, empty review. `stdin`
   has no such cap.

   0 having read nothing.** Inlining the subject makes a silent sandbox failure
   unable to empty the review: if the source is in the prompt, the reviewer
   either quotes it or visibly does not.

**Each reviewer was smoke-tested with a planted marker before any real payload**,
so a reviewer that was not reading at all was caught before a bundle was spent
on it.
