code-level constant in the test module and appears in **no comment anywhere in the
example tree** — including the artifacts, which is the subject of a Rule-1 fix below.

The specification's own five mentions are in Markdown **prose**, which the scan
correctly does not treat as a comment, and there is a control asserting exactly that.

### Rule 3 — a blocking issue, and my first fix for it was wrong

**What happened.** Creating `examples/cp-04-corporate-family-tree/artifacts/` turned
`tests/examples/test_artifact_schema_headers.py::test_every_covered_artifact_directory_sits_under_an_example_root`

**The first fix (`1d4f0d7`) was wrong.** A bare, refusing `run.py` satisfied the
bijection and broke five tests in `tests/examples/test_spine_separation.py`.
`is_pre_build` releases a Gate-1 tree from the spine checks **only while it carries
`spec/` and ZERO `.py` files at any depth**, and its docstring says the narrowness is
deliberate — *"one module anywhere puts the tree back in scope"* — because a rule that
skipped what it could not parse would let a shipped example lose its spine silently. The
bare `run.py` did exactly the thing that narrowness exists to make impossible.

**The repository has already ruled this, at rung 03, and Dave ruled it.** Commit
`4cf3372` records it: two example states are recognised and no third — authored
specification, and built example. Writing `artifacts/` requires a `run.py`; writing any
`.py` puts the tree in spine scope. *"Weakening a gate so an increment fits is the
failure; a stub honest about being one is not."*

**The fix (`7f682b5`) follows that precedent rather than re-deciding it.** `produce.py`
opens the seven canonical regions in order, balanced and SILENT, every region raising
`NotImplementedError` naming its step and the plan that fills it. `run.py` loads the five

### Judgment calls, recorded because they departed from the plan's literal wording

**`seed_entity_classes` declares the anchor's RESOLUTION class, not a FIBO class name.**
The plan says to declare "the FIBO classes the walk STARTS FROM". `entity_class` is the
vocabulary the deployment's `resolution` section mints, and it is what
`src/ccf/operators/categories.py:426` matches the frontier against — **a FIBO class name
there would match no entity record and seed an EMPTY frontier**, which reads as a
bounded walk that legitimately returned nothing. The artifact declares `family_anchor`
and says all of this in place, including that the FIBO chain is SERVED as `type_chain`
and is not an admission filter.

**Two entity classes rather than one**, for a related mechanical reason: the frontier is
*every* entity of a seed class, so one class over all 748 family nodes would seed the
walk from all of them and `depth_limit` would bound nothing.

**`ttl.yaml`'s two figures are equal.** The plan asks for the TTL and its ceiling "as two
figures". They are two FIELDS, so the gate does not compare a ceiling against itself, and
their values are both 86400 because the specification gives one number — inventing a
wider ceiling would be minting a domain value a build had chosen.
