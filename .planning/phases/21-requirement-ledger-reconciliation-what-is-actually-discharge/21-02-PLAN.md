The vocabulary is exactly six words (D-09): `Complete`, `Complete as ruled`,
`Complete over the ruled scope`, `Partial`, `Not built`, `Orphaned`.

The checkbox-channel twin carries the LONG form with the `path::symbol` evidence, appended to the
existing bullet after an em dash, and the box is ticked `[x]` if and only if the table verdict is
in the Complete family. The model is `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md:149` (`DCD-10`).

**D-03 is the bar:** a row may read `Complete` — or a 15.6-vocabulary variant — only with a
citation the lint can resolve. A citation the lint cannot resolve is not evidence, and a verdict
resting on one is a claim, not a finding.

Citation form (D-06, D-17): full repo-relative paths only. `path.py::symbol` wherever the
evidence is Python — which for this lane is every row that has evidence at all. A bare basename
is REFUSED by `tools/docs_tests/test_requirement_ledger.py::refuse_unresolvable`, and so is a
path that does not exist as written.
