<objective>
Land everything the audit needs to be *guarded while it happens*, before a single verdict is
written: the pinned diff base, the untouched-tree assertions, the shared strict parser, and the
two standing checks with their numbered sabotage controls.

Purpose: `Makefile:138` says `ci-core` "MUST stay green from Wave 1 onward". The properties that
are true of a *partially filled* ledger — citation form, channel agreement, vocabulary
membership, one-verdict-per-cell — land here so that a mid-phase `make ci-core` goes red on the
first bad verdict rather than at close. The completeness count is deliberately NOT here; it is
red until the last row lands and belongs to plan `21-06`.

Output: a full-history checkout on the CI `docs` job, `21-BASE-COMMIT.txt`, the ten `RECON-`
rows minted into `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`, `tools/docs_tests/test_requirement_ledger.py`, and
three appended `docs/DECISIONS.md` entries.

**The phase's own ten requirement rows are minted HERE, in wave 1, not at the gate.** `RECON-01`
through `RECON-10` enter both ledger channels as `Pending` before a single audit verdict is
written, following the `CLNT-23`..`32` precedent from Phase 20. Two reasons. First, the count:
the table is 133 rows at the pinned base and 143 from this plan forward. Per `D-21` **no standing
check in this phase carries either number** — what is asserted is that the two channels agree
id-for-id on whatever the ledger contains — but `PHASE_OWN_ROSTER` is a FROZEN roster of ten named
ids taken here in wave 1, and a row minted later would sit outside it and outside every check that
reads it. Second, and
decisively: rows written after Dave's approval, inside the gate checkpoint, would never be seen
by the close-time cross-AI panel, never by the single-agent review, and never by Dave, whose
approval was granted against a ledger that did not contain them. That is the exact ledger-debt
pattern this phase was commissioned to end, reproduced one level up. The 54-row working set is a
FROZEN roster taken by the strict parse at the pinned base; the ten `RECON-` rows are this
phase's own and are NOT members of it. Their verdicts are written in plan `21-06`, before the
panel.

Two standing constraints this plan establishes for the whole phase. **D-08**: the D-06 refusal
carries a NAMED sabotage control — a row given a deliberately false or ambiguous citation is
observably RED as a standing test, never as a probe recorded in a SUMMARY (`D-IMPL-170`); a phase
about evidence whose own evidence-check cannot fail would be this project's defect class one level
up, again. **D-16**: all work happens on branch `phase-21`; `main` is the approved-state branch
(CLAUDE.md §2.2) and the merge happens at the `D-GATE-21` entry, not before. Do not create,
switch or rename branches.
