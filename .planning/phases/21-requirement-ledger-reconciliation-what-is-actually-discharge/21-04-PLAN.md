The test is NOT "was the plan executed." It is **"does the obligation this row states have a
satisfied referent in the tree as it stands today?"**

| Side | Test | Verdict | Where the evidence lives |
|------|------|---------|--------------------------|
| **A — re-derived and satisfied** | the obligation's substance was carried forward and shipped by a later phase against the surface that now exists | `Complete` / `Complete as ruled` / `Complete over the ruled scope` | the later phase's tree and SUMMARYs; the `deferred-items.md` chains |
| **B — still owed, referent exists** | the obligation is coherent against today's surface and simply has not been done | `Not built` — and it goes on Dave's work list | the NAMED absence: the file that is not there, the test that does not exist, the directory that was to be retired and is not |
| **C — orphaned** | the obligation is *about* the rejected surface; satisfying it would mean rebuilding something the project deliberately walked away from | `Orphaned` — with the specific absence named | `RETIRED.md`'s "Why the plans cannot simply be resumed"; the abandoned plan text as history; `docs/retired/` |

A row can be split across sides and must then say so — `Partial` exists for exactly this, and
`DCD-02` is the in-tree model.
