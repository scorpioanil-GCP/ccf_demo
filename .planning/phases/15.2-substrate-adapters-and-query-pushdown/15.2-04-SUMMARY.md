## Decisions Made

- **Flattened families over nested contracts:** the policy family is three all-or-none fields and the temporal family one field directly on GraphQuery/QueryFragment (traverse stays a nested `TraversalSpec` as the plan names it). Nested policy/temporal classes would have added public names beyond the planned four; the whole-family rule is enforced by a shared `model_validator` instead, matching GQ-02's "the contract MUST refuse it at construction".
- **QueryFragment enforces at-most-one-family at construction:** GQ-06 defines a fragment as at most one family's contribution; validating at the boundary (CLAUDE.md §11) means plan compilation in 15.2-07 never sees a malformed fragment. Field set was within Claude's discretion per CONTEXT.md.
- **`SourceAttestation.peer` carries no default:** explicit `peer=None` is an assertion, per the "a default is a value nobody chose" discipline the mirrored contracts state.
- **Inline Literals rather than named mode aliases** for SourceAttestation, exactly as the plan's action text writes them — named aliases would be additional public surface names.
