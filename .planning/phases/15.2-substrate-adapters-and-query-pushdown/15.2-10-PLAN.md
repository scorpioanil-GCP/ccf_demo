Build the tier-2 read path (D-08): a ProductSource that mounts an admitted
Context Product behind the same GraphReader Protocol every substrate answers.
The §6.6 recursive-enrichment case becomes reachable at read time, with the
admission governance reused unchanged — zero edits to ccf.peering.admission.

model_validate against the existing record contracts; the view it answers is
ephemeral, owned by the execution; single-writer is not implicated because
nothing here can write. allowed_entities-bearing queries are answered correctly
for free — the shared fallback's set-intersection is the implementation, which
is exactly the D-IMPL-87 resolution's point about the fallback tier.

coverage, surface fragment.

       GraphReader Protocol:

         raise; cite the clause anchor in the query() docstring, including

         the mounted records — the fallback's re-applied as-of predicate
