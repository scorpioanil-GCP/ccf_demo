# Phase 15.2 — §3.1 Phase-Close Self-Review and Triage

This is the CLAUDE.md §3.1 step-1 self-review, run **before** the package is put
in front of Dave, because "a review report is a precondition of requesting
approval, not a substitute for it."

## Why this is a separate file from `15.2-REVIEWS.md`

`15.2-REVIEWS.md` is the **planning-time** cross-AI review, dated 2026-08-07,
of the *void twelve-plan set* drawn before the D-IMPL-87 resolution. The
standard `/gsd-review` workflow writes to that exact path. Running it as-is
would have overwritten a historical record with a review of different artifacts
at a different time — so the close review lands here instead and the planning
record is preserved unedited. Recorded as a deviation in `15.2-14-SUMMARY.md`.

The other adaptation: `/gsd-review` reviews `PLAN.md` files. A §3.1 *close*
review is against **shipped deliverables**, so the prompt carried the normative
prose in full, the phase diff statistics, the requirement texts, the thirteen
SUMMARYs and the deferred items, and asked each reviewer for BLOCKING /
ADVISORY findings with the specific unsupported claim named.

## Triage

### BLOCKING — fixed in-phase before the checkpoint

**B1 — SUBQ-06's requirement text stated the design D-IMPL-87 rejected.**
*Raised by:* Cursor (blocker 2). *Status:* **FIXED**, commit `9b932fb` lineage,
text corrected in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`.

The minted text said post-in-process segments carry survivors "as seeds +
`allowed_entities`". The D-IMPL-87 resolution says the opposite in its own
words — "seeds say where walks start, not which nodes they may cross" — and the
shipped engine passes `seeds=None, allowed_entities=survivors`
(`src/ccf/projection/engine.py:527-528`, pinned by
`tests/projection/test_segmentation.py::test_the_second_query_carries_the_survivors_as_allowed_entities`).
Plan 15.2-07 noticed the discrepancy and recorded it as a plan-language reading
rather than correcting the row.

This is blocking and not cosmetic: a **Complete** requirement whose text states
a rejected design is a repudiation defect. Promoting every survivor to a seed
would make every survivor a walk start (`GQ-10`), widening the pushed answer
past the in-process semantics and re-opening the exact entity-resurrection
defect the mini-cycle was convened to close. A later reader would take the row
as the ruling. Text now matches the ruling and the code; no code changed.

**B2 — Neo4j pushdown was not distinguished from silent degrade.**
*Raised by:* Cursor (blocker 3), echoed as an advisory by Codex (A2).
*Status:* **FIXED** — new test, green against a live server.

Cursor's framing is exactly right and worth preserving verbatim in the record:
*"Byte-identity to the fallback is a conformance bar, not a pushdown bar. Under
D-04, a broken `_translate_query` that always returns `None` still matches the
oracle."* `GQ-34` makes that explicit — the degraded posture is byte-identical
**by design**.

Measured: `grep -c translated_answers` → `test_sql_pushdown.py` 6,
`test_mongo_backend.py` 2, **`test_neo4j_backend.py` 0**. The neo4j leg's whole
recorded evidence was `6 passed` on the differential corpus, which a
never-translating adapter also passes. For the phase whose goal is substrate
pushdown, with Neo4j named in the roadmap as *"the strongest pushdown test"*,
that is a claim stronger than its evidence.

Fixed by `tests/graph/test_neo4j_backend.py::test_cypher_traversal_is_pushed_not_silently_degraded`
— `translated_answers == 4 / fallback_answers == 0` across depth-0, depth-1, the
out-then-in depth-2 path, and the resurrection shape, with `memory` as the
differential control. Green against Neo4j Kernel 5.26.29: `1 passed`. All five
adapters now carry a push proof, not merely a conformance proof.

### BLOCKING — escalated, cannot be fixed without a decision Dave has not made

**B4 — the pushed SQL validity predicate diverges from the fallback on an
offset-authored claim timestamp (`GQ-31` has a reachable counterexample).**
*Raised independently by:* Codex (blocker 1) and Gemini (blockers 1 and 3);
recorded in-phase by plan 15.2-09 as DI-15.2-03. *Status:* **ESCALATED as
`D-IMPL-90` (OPEN)** — the named gating decision of the D-GATE-15.2 package.

Both reviewers sharpened the finding beyond how the deferred item had it:
`GQ-31` is **byte-identity**, not "no over-disclosure", so a known-diverging
shape is a counterexample to the phase's central conformance clause regardless
of which direction it errs in. That reading is accepted.

The audit **measured** the corner rather than restating it, and the measurement
corrects a false assumption shared by the deferred item and the reviewer. One
`EntityClaim` with `valid_from = 2026-03-01T08:00:00+05:00`:

| Lane | Rendering | Normalized? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `model_dump(mode="json")` — what `_writer.py` persists into `claim_json` | `2026-03-01T08:00:00+05:00` | **no** |
| `canonical_bytes(claim)` — what `content_hash` and every answer use | `2026-03-01T03:00:00.000000Z` | **yes** |

Normalizing the persisted payload would therefore **not move any content hash**:
claim identity is `content_hash(_entity_content_key(claim))`, which passes the
`datetime` value through `canonical_bytes` and is already UTC-normalized. The
identity lane and the payload lane are independent. That removes the
replay-compatibility objection that made the tidiest option look unavailable.

Not fixed here, per §3.1's own rule — every available option is a persisted-format
change, a two-dialect SQL rework outside this plan's scope, or a normative-prose
concession, and none is an executor's call at a close-out gate. Options,
consequences, and risk posture are in the `D-IMPL-90` entry.

## What the reviewers checked and found sound

Recorded so Dave can see what the review actually covered, rather than trusting
a clean bill:

- The prose authority chain: `docs/GRAPH_QUERY_SEMANTICS.md` at 1.0.1, `GQ-16`
  scoped to constrained answers, amendment history under `GQ-35`, zero
  occurrences of "implementation-defined", DI-15.2-01 closed for the right
  reason (Codex, Cursor).
- The `allowed_entities` model is sound against entity resurrection: `None` /
  empty / populated are three distinct facts; seeds keep one meaning; traversal
  cannot cross outside the survivor set; relationships need both endpoints
  in-set (all three reviewers, independently).
- `QueryFragment` structurally excluding `seeds` and `allowed_entities` — an
  operator cannot smuggle segment-boundary data (Codex).
- Executed query hashes recorded from execution rather than plan prediction,
  with ArtifactStore bytes as the audit mechanism (Codex, Cursor).
- Bytes-before-append pin ordering, so a published pin always resolves
  (Gemini).
- Single-writer: no `*GraphWriter` / `*GraphTxn` outside `ccf.graph._writer`,
  the Protocol-only exception in `protocols.py`, the confinement test, and the
  graphstore fixture now parametrized over all five backends (Cursor, Gemini).
- `as_of()` ≡ the degenerate `query()`, with zero call-site churn (Cursor,
  Gemini).
- Survivor cap is `None`-defaulted on engine, manifest and record — mechanism
  shipped, value not baked (Cursor).
- Tier-2 `ProductSource`: mounts only an `AdmittedProduct`, typed mount
  refusal, nothing enters L3 (Codex, Cursor).
- Attestation accepted modes as unordered sets, refusal plus gate path, dim-1

- CI wiring carries all three service containers and their marked steps

- The do-not-disturb seams: `peering/admission.py` and `deployment/binder.py`
