<objective>
**Track B — gap 1, second limb.** `DebtAssessment` gains the carrier that lets
debt point at the observations whose decay it summarises, and the API to populate
and extract it.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-112` recorded that a KYC analyst would not act on
`D_c=0.221 / forced_llm / F=0.803` alone, and named the first of three things
missing: **which specific observations contributed the decay.** Plan `15.6-05`
gave `Observation` the identity a pointer can name. This plan gives debt the
pointer.

Output: an optional carrier on `DebtAssessment`, the `assess_debt` parameter that
populates it, the replay quartet that proves it survives replay, and one fragment
covering `DebtAssessment` and every type whose canonical bytes move with it.

**The product content address moves here, and that is the ruled change**
(`D-IMPL-117`: *"surface impact is accepted... the third product-content-address
move is an authorized cost, not a risk to design around"*). Do not design around
it. Do regenerate exactly what pins it, and count what that is rather than
assuming — plan `15.6-01` re-derived the census and its figure, not
`15.6-RESEARCH.md`'s, is the one to work from.

**The free ride, and why the shape matters.** `DebtAssessment` is already carried
on **both** `ContextProductContract.debt` and `ContentGenerationPath.debt`, so
anything nested inside it inherits replay reconstructibility for nothing — every
rehydrator's existing one-line `debt=record.content_generation_path.debt` carries
a reshaped assessment verbatim. A new **top-level** field on the vessel does not:
`ProjectionOutput` has exactly two fields, and a top-level addition costs seven
`rehydrate_product` implementations across six files. `D-IMPL-118` ruled which
shape; implement the ruling, and if it chose the top-level or declared shape,
budget the seven implementations explicitly in task 1's summary.

**The entitlement ruling lands in this plan, and nowhere else.** `D-IMPL-119` —
the Wave-2 answer to *"does an evidence pointer leak entitlement?"* — has exactly
one implementing site, and it is task 2's population of the carrier at the s1-01
assembly. Its three candidate answers produce three different populations at that
call site, so an executor who does not read the ruling implements one of them by
accident. A ruling with no implementing task is a ruling that gets defaulted,
which is the failure the Wave-2 checkpoint exists to prevent.

**What the SDK owes and what it does not** (`D-IMPL-117`): a way for debt to
point at what it summarises — and **nothing about which observations matter**.
The pointer contents are opaque identifiers the deployment declares, in the
shipped `src/ccf/skills/catalog.py::SkillRequirements` shape. No entity-URI type,
no claim-id type, no vocabulary, no default.
</objective>
