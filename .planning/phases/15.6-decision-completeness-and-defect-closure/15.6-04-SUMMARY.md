document's § Q-A row 2 measures it: the token is **absent** from the canonical
bytes of both inputs; `ProjectionOutput` carries exactly `(plan_id, payload)`;

`84799e67…`, which is neither the delivered address `e358be39…` nor the baseline

finding verbatim (*"There is no perspective-scoped view to derive a narrower
