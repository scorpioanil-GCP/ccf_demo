<the_shape_this_plan_specifies>
`D-IMPL-124` rules Shape B and leaves three things inside it genuinely open.
This plan **specifies all three**, with reasons. **Nothing here is deferred to
a further ruling, and an executor must not raise one for these three.** The
Wave-2 rulings are made.

**1. REPLACE, not parallel.** The ruling offers *"replace (or parallel)"*. This
plan replaces. Reason: the ruling's own operative sentence is *"no `source_ref`
strings leave the vessel"* and *"the inference channel is **closed**"*. A
parallel shape leaves the per-source map on the vessel, which leaves the oracle
open — that is Shape A wearing a second field. Replacing also avoids two vessel
fields describing one fact, which would double the replay obligation and give a
future reader two places to look for one answer.

**2. TWO instants, named `oldest_source_refresh_at` and
`newest_source_refresh_at`.** Both of Dave's examples are two-member pairs, so
two members is inside the ruling either way. Two rather than one because a
single instant cannot express the span: with sources on different cadences, a
lone "newest" hides the stalest and a lone "oldest" hides the freshest, and
`D-IMPL-112` gap 2's whole complaint is that a reader could not tell how old the
underlying filings were.

`oldest`/`newest` rather than Dave's alternative `stalest`/`freshest` spelling,
and the reason is the phase's own discipline rather than taste: *stale* and
*fresh* are **judgement words**. A field named `sources_stalest_at` asserts that
the SDK has a notion of staleness, which `D-IMPL-117` says it owes nothing of
(*"no staleness threshold"*) and which `D-IMPL-101` deferred as salience.
*Oldest* and *newest* describe the ordering of two declared instants and assert
nothing about whether either is acceptable. Record this reasoning in the
docstring; it is the sentence that stops a later contributor from "improving"
the names.

**3. NO count, NO cardinality, NO residue of the set.** Do **not** add a
`source_count`, a `sources_summarized` figure, or anything else that says how
many systems the deployment reads. A count is source enumeration in aggregate —
it tells a reader how many systems exist even though it names none — and it is
not freshness, so it is outside what the field exists for. This is an explicit
prohibition, not an omission.

**The carrier, concretely.**

```
class SourceRefreshWindow(CCFContract):
    oldest_source_refresh_at: datetime   # tz-aware; naive refused at construction
    newest_source_refresh_at: datetime   # tz-aware; naive refused at construction
```

- Both instants reuse the existing `_the_instant_names_its_zone` discipline —
  a naive instant is **refused**, not coerced (`D-IMPL-38`), because the value
  is inside the delivered product's content address.
- **One coherence validator is permitted and required:**
  `oldest_source_refresh_at <= newest_source_refresh_at`, refused otherwise with
  a message in `_refreshes_are_one_per_source_in_canonical_order`'s voice — a
  window whose oldest end is after its newest end describes two facts claiming
  to be one, which is an incoherent record rather than a stricter one. Equality
  is legal (one source, or several that published together).
  **This is not the forbidden comparison.** The forbidden comparisons are
  against `now`, against a threshold and against a cadence — those draw a
  conclusion. This one checks that a single declared fact is internally
  coherent, exactly as the duplicate-reference refusal it replaces did.
- Ordering validators are **gone with the collection**: a two-field contract has
  no declaration order, so `_refreshes_are_one_per_source_in_canonical_order` is
  deleted rather than adapted, and the order-insensitivity test it backed is
  replaced by a test asserting the field set is exactly two.

**The alias and the sentinel keep their names.**

- `SourceRefreshRecency` is re-pointed from
  `Annotated[tuple[SourceRefresh, ...], …]` to `SourceRefreshWindow | None`. It
  keeps its name for `DebtEvidence`'s recorded reason — the carrier lands in
  **two** places, the vessel and replay dimension 5, and one named shape is what
  keeps those two from drifting apart.
- `NO_SOURCE_REFRESH_RECENCY` keeps its name and its docstring's positive claim,
  and its **value moves from `()` to `None`.** State the reason in the
  docstring, because this is a deliberate departure from the empty-tuple
  sentinel `15.6-07` chose and a reviewer must not read it as drift: `()` is the
  empty case of a *collection*, and a window is not a collection — there is no
  empty window that does not require inventing an instant, and an invented
  instant is the silent default `D-IMPL-38` forbids. The same-vessel precedent
  is exact: `ContextProductContract.debt` and `.request` are both optional
  *structures* whose `None` is documented as a positive assertion in the
  `NO_HITL_ACTIONS` voice. What the name buys is that every construction site
  and every default still **spells the claim** rather than leaving a bare
  `None`.

**Both field names stay `source_refresh_recency`** — on
`ContextProductContract` and on `ContentGenerationPath`. Reason: the field is
still source-refresh recency, merely summarized; and keeping the spelling means
the seven `rehydrate_product` read-back lines are byte-identical, which removes
seven opportunities to introduce an error while changing nothing about whether
the seam is load-bearing. **The seam must still be re-measured** — the fixture
changes shape, so the recorded necessity counts are stale.

**What is deleted.** `SourceRefresh`, `_refreshes_are_one_per_source_in_canonical_order`,
and both names' re-export spellings in `ccf.halflife.debt`. Remove them from
both `__all__` lists. A class kept "for compatibility" would leave a
`source_ref`-bearing type on the public surface that an adopter could still put
in a payload, which defeats the ruling by leaving the shape available.
</the_shape_this_plan_specifies>
