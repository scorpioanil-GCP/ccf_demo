<objective>
**Track B — gap 2.** A deployment can record **when the underlying source
filings were last refreshed**, as a fact distinct from the freshness the fabric
computed.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-112` recorded a KYC analyst's second missing item in Dave's own
framing — computed freshness of `F=0.803` says how much the fabric's own decay
curve has eaten, and says nothing about when the upstream registry last
published. `15.6-RESEARCH.md` § B3 verified that **nothing in the tree models
it**: `Observation.observed_at` is *"when the fabric learned the observation"*,
`LineageEntry.observed_at` is the same clock, `FreshnessScorer` computes the
derived number rather than the underlying fact, `HalfLifeEntry` carries a rate
rather than an instant, `SourceAttestation`'s own docstring says *"temporal
correctness is deliberately not an attestation dimension"*, and `FreshnessSLA` is
an expectation rather than an observation.

Output: an optional slot, populated by the s1-01 deployment, surviving replay,
with its own fragment and its own address regeneration.

**What the SDK owes and what it does not** (`D-IMPL-117`): *"a slot for
source-refresh recency, owing no definition of a source or a cadence."*
Concretely — **no `SourceType` enum, no refresh-interval field, no staleness
threshold, no default, and no comparison of a recency against anything.** The
source reference is an opaque string the deployment declares. The SDK stores an
instant and a reference and draws no conclusion from either.

**`LineageEntry` is a blocked slot and the plan must not try it.** Two
independent locks: `D-IMPL-58` ruled `LineageEntry` is not extended to carry
federated axes; and
`tests/peering/test_admission_record.py::test_claims_py_is_unchanged_so_reading_a_cannot_creep_back_in`
pins `sha256(src/ccf/contracts/claims.py)` for the **whole file** and separately
asserts `LineageEntry`'s exact field set. That test's own docstring says the
correct response to needing a change there is *"revisited under a new `D-IMPL`
entry rather than under this test."* The failure mode to avoid is an executor
editing the pinned constant to make a test pass — which is the retouching-the-
evidence defect the phase's own spike discipline exists to prevent.

**Recency is not salience.** `D-IMPL-101` deferred salience to a later phase.
This plan records an instant and a reference; it ranks nothing, weights nothing
and scores nothing. If a task starts to look like it is deciding which sources
matter more, it has drifted into salience and must stop.

**The product content address moves again.** This is the second of up to three
moves inside this phase, each authorized by `D-IMPL-117` and each regenerating
its own artifacts. It is serialized behind plan `15.6-06` for exactly that
reason: three concurrent address moves would be three merge conflicts on one
hashed contract.
</objective>
