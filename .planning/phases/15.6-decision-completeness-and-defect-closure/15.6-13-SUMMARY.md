# Phase 15.6 Plan 13: D-IMPL-124 Shape B — the freshness signal without the source identities Summary

`ContextProductContract.source_refresh_recency` now publishes a two-instant,
non-identifying, count-free freshness window instead of a per-source map, so no
human-interpretable source identity leaves the vessel — the third instance of
this inference-channel shape in the project, closed rather than carried forward.

## What shipped

**Task 1 — the carrier (`58d8d82`).** `SourceRefresh` and
`_refreshes_are_one_per_source_in_canonical_order` are **deleted**, not
deprecated: a class kept "for compatibility" would leave a `source_ref`-bearing
type on the public surface an adopter could still put in a payload.
`SourceRefreshWindow` replaces it with exactly two members,
`oldest_source_refresh_at` and `newest_source_refresh_at`, both reusing
`_the_instant_names_its_zone` verbatim, plus one model validator refusing
`oldest > newest`. `SourceRefreshRecency` re-points to
`SourceRefreshWindow | None`; `NO_SOURCE_REFRESH_RECENCY` keeps its name and
its positive claim while its value moves from `()` to `None`, with the reason
recorded in the docstring (a window is not a collection; there is no empty
window that does not require inventing an instant, and an invented instant is
the silent default `D-IMPL-38` forbids).

All three of the plan's specified choices were implemented as specified —
replace not parallel, two instants, no count — and **no `D-IMPL` question was
raised about any of them.**

**Task 2 — the boundary and the seam (`b1a2e66`).** `product-governance.yaml`
keeps all three per-source entries with their human-interpretable references,
plus a new `[ADAPT]` block recording that **the references stop there**.
`deployment.py::_declared_refresh_window` reduces them with `min`/`max` over
authored text — no clock read, no formula derived, no instant invented — and
the window is what crosses onto the product. New case
`test_no_source_identity_reaches_the_delivered_product` asserts the ruling in
both directions that can fail.

**Task 3 — the address move and the record (`8a646cd`).** The fourth
content-address move of the phase, regenerated by running; the surface-change
fragment, written as a correction; and the measured reconciliation of 15.6-07's
now-unpublishable entries.

## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 3 — Blocking] The `source_ref` grep criterion is unsatisfiable as literally written

**Found during:** Task 1 verification.
**Issue:** The plan's acceptance criterion states *"`grep -rn "source_ref" src/ runtime/` returns **nothing**. This is the ruling's operative sentence and it is a grep, not a judgement."* But the same plan mandates that **both field names stay `source_refresh_recency`**, and `source_refresh_recency` contains `source_ref` as a literal prefix. The two requirements cannot both hold. The same applies to task 2's `grep -rn "source_ref\|SourceRefresh(" src/ runtime/`.
**Resolution:** Read the criterion at its stated intent — Dave's ruling is *"no `source_ref` **strings** leave the vessel"*, i.e. the identifier — and used the word-boundary form `grep -rnE "\bsource_ref\b"`, which returns nothing. The plan's own sibling criterion for `SourceRefresh` is already word-boundary-aware (`"SourceRefresh\b"`), which corroborates the reading. **The substance is met:** no `source_ref` field, no `source_ref` value, and no source identity anywhere in `src/` or `runtime/`.
**Files:** none — a measurement-method correction.

### 2. [Rule 1 — Bug] The plan misattributed the `[]` pin to the frozen literal

**Found during:** Task 1.
**Issue:** The plan says *"`tests/replay/test_harness_trace.py` will fire: the frozen `PRE_CHANGE_CANONICAL` literal pins `b'"source_refresh_recency":[],'`"* and instructs *"ADD a `.replace()` step. Do not retouch the frozen literal."* Measured against the tree: `PRE_CHANGE_CANONICAL` (defined at line 62) **does not contain `source_refresh_recency` at all**. The `[],` spelling lives in the `.replace()` normalization chain, which operates on the *current builder's* output, not on the captured evidence.
**Resolution:** Left `PRE_CHANGE_CANONICAL` **byte-identical** (the diff on that file shows only the `.replace()` step and its comment), and **re-pointed** the existing step from `[],` to `null,` rather than adding a second one. Adding a step while leaving the old one would have left a step matching nothing beside a comment asserting a rendering the tree no longer has — a false statement in the tree. The comment now records the D-IMPL-124 move and why a normalization step is re-pointed while captured evidence never is.
**Added/changed step, quoted:**
```python
b'"source_refresh_recency":null,',
```
**Files:** `tests/replay/test_harness_trace.py`. **Commit:** `58d8d82`.

## What this closes, and what it does not

**Closed: the identity channel.** No `source_ref` field exists; the class that
carried one is deleted rather than deprecated, so the shape is unavailable
rather than merely unused; and
`test_no_source_identity_reaches_the_delivered_product` asserts mechanically —
against the real gate-and-serve path — that none of `corporate-registry`,
`kyc-registry` or `name-screening` appears in the delivered `canonical_bytes`.
The three references are **read from the artifact rather than transcribed**, so
renaming a source cannot quietly retire the assertion. The test also walks the
10 contract types reachable from the vessel and from dimension 5 and asserts
none is *typed* to carry a source reference — the half a value check cannot
give, because a product that merely happened not to populate an
identity-bearing member would pass a value check while leaving the channel open.

**Closed: the cardinality residue.** No count, no `sources_summarized`, no
length-bearing member; asserted by field enumeration on a two-member contract.

**NOT claimed.** Two instants are still information: a reader still learns that
*some* source published at each end of the window. The fragment says this
precisely rather than asserting that all inference is gone — the same standard
`D-IMPL-105` and `D-IMPL-119` each met.

**NOT built.** Shape C — opaque tokens with an entitlement-checked resolution
door — is `NOT TAKEN` and recorded as the named long-term design. No token, no
resolution seam, no door was added.
