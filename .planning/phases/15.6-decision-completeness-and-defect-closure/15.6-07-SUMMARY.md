# Phase 15.6 Plan 07: Source-Refresh Recency Summary

**A deployment can now record when each upstream source last published as a
top-level fact on the delivered product, distinct from the freshness the fabric
computed — and the SDK stores the instant, reads it back through replay, and
draws no conclusion from it whatsoever.**

## What shipped

### The slot, `qa-toplevel` (`D-IMPL-118`)

`ContextProductContract` gained `source_refresh_recency: SourceRefreshRecency`,
defaulted to `NO_SOURCE_REFRESH_RECENCY` — **a sibling of `debt`, not a member
of `DebtAssessment` and not a member of `DebtFactors`**. The ruling clause
implemented, quoted by entry number:

> **`D-IMPL-118`:** *"These four rows are properties of the placement _shape_
> applied to a member of `ContextProductContract`, not of any one gap's field.
> They therefore price all three Track B vessel-carried members identically."*

and its consequence, also quoted:

> *"An executor of any of the three plans that ships the vessel field without
> the record-side member and the seven rehydrator edits has not implemented
> this ruling."*

The element is `SourceRefresh`, a `CCFContract` carrying **exactly two**
members: `source_ref: str`, opaque in the `SkillRequirements` voice, and
`refreshed_at: datetime`, timezone-aware. The collection is a **sorted tuple**
rather than a `frozenset`, following `debt_evidence`'s resolution for its
reason — the value is inside a content address and must be order-insensitive in
its bytes, and a set would collapse or preserve a contradiction rather than
refusing it.

**What is absent on purpose, and asserted as absent:** no `SourceType`, no
`Literal` of source names, no cadence or refresh-interval field, no staleness
threshold, no default of any kind, and **no comparison** of a refresh instant
against `now`, a threshold, or an `Observation.observed_at`. That last is
asserted rather than promised —
`tests/halflife/test_debt.py::test_the_recency_is_carried_and_never_consumed`
scans every module under `src/ccf/` and requires `refreshed_at` to appear in
exactly one of them, plus asserts `datetime.now` and `utcnow` appear in neither.
A diff read of the added `src/` lines confirms it independently: the only
comparison in the new code is `value.tzinfo is None`, the zone guard.

### The six replay cases (`3528238`)

Added to the existing tripwire module rather than a new file, following the

   `DebtFactors`. The second exclusion is this field's own: the `qa-nested`

   `product_content_address` **and** `content_hash(record)` against the store
   address, recomputed rather than trusted. The moved graph is pointed for this
   field in particular: the fabric's decay has advanced and the recorded

4. `test_a_rehydration_that_drops_the_recency_is_divergence` and — not implied
   by it — `test_a_rehydration_that_moves_one_refresh_instant_is_divergence`,
   which rules out an implementation treating the slot as *which* sources were
   read rather than *when* each last published.
5. `test_a_record_written_before_the_recency_seam_still_replays` — the
   sentinel's compatibility promise.

### The population: declared by a deployment, not derived by the SDK

`examples/s1-01-hello-context-product` populates the slot from **three entries
authored in `artifacts/product-governance.yaml`**, one per source system id
that `artifacts/events.yaml` already stamps on its envelopes, each instant
naming its zone. The artifact carries the reasoning where an adopter reads it:
deriving a refresh instant from an envelope's `observed_time` would collapse it
back into the fact it exists to be distinct from, and computing it from the
decision instant would make it a restatement of `now`. No formula was invented
in example code and no clock was read.

It is read as a scenario **field** rather than as a `GraphView`-taking method,
unlike the `debt` and `debt_evidence` beside it — recorded as a deliberate
asymmetry, not an inconsistency. Those two are *measured* against what the
operators left standing; this one is *declared*, because a refresh instant is
not a property of the assembled view at all. Handing it a view it would not
read would suggest otherwise.

## Findings

**1. The census's mechanism finding from 15.6-06 is confirmed a second time.**
The `skill-set in force` hash on the line directly below the one that moved
(`aafa91413bcd8b21…`) **did not move** again, for the same reason: under
`qa-toplevel` `DebtAssessment` is byte-identical, so only the record address —
carrying `ContentGenerationPath`, which gained the member — moves. Recorded as
a confirmation rather than as a new finding.

**2. `s1-01`'s committed output did not move, and it is the population site.**
`tests/examples/test_s1_01_output.py` is green with no regeneration. That
example's transcript prints the answer and its provenance rather than a product
content address, so a hashed-field addition is invisible to it. Recorded
because the opposite is the natural assumption for the one example that
actually carries populated values.

**3. A possible entitlement question about recency, named and NOT ruled.** The
execution prompt was explicit that `D-IMPL-119` does not govern this plan and
that a refresh timestamp is not an evidence pointer, and that limb 3/4 must not
be applied by analogy — so it was not, and the slot ships populated. Naming the
adjacent question rather than ruling it: a refresh instant for a source a
principal is not entitled to consult is, in principle, an oracle of the same
family `D-IMPL-105` and `D-IMPL-119` each closed — a reader could learn that a
source *exists* and *when it last published* without being entitled to its
contents. **Nothing shipped here varies by principal**, so the coupling
`D-IMPL-119` recorded against `D-IMPL-118` does not arise: the same assembly
produces one content address for every principal. But the source *set* a
deployment declares is not currently scoped by anything, and the shipped
assembly path is still not principal-scoped (`D-IMPL-119` limb 2). **This is a
new question, not a limb of an existing entry, and it is not ruled here.**

**4. `docs/DECISIONS.md` needs no correction.** `D-IMPL-118`, `D-IMPL-119`,
`D-IMPL-120`, `D-IMPL-117`, `D-IMPL-101` and `D-IMPL-100` were all read before
implementation and none was found wrong or stale against the tree.

## Deviations from Plan

### `[Ruling wins]` The `<files>` manifests were short, and the extra edits were required

The plan's task-1 `<files>` list names only `src/ccf/halflife/assessment.py`
and `tests/halflife/test_debt.py`. Under `qa-toplevel` seven further files
**must** be edited — `src/ccf/products/contract.py`, `src/ccf/replay/record.py`,
`src/ccf/replay/engine.py`, and five of the six rehydrator files — and the
plan's own action prose authorizes exactly that (*"If the ruling chose anything
other than nesting inside `DebtAssessment`, enumerate in the summary the seven
`rehydrate_product` implementations across six files that must change, and
change them"*). All were edited; the seven are enumerated above.

`src/ccf/halflife/debt.py` was also edited, outside every manifest, to
re-export the three new names. Not doing so would have made the module split —
which exists only to break an import cycle — an adopter's problem, since
`assess_debt` lives in `debt.py` and that is the spelling a deployment already
imports from.

`examples/s1-01-hello-context-product/spine.py` and
`artifacts/product-governance.yaml` were edited outside task 2's manifest, for
the same reason: the plan says to *"declare it in a scenario artifact"* and to
populate the slot, and the vessel is constructed in `spine.py`.

### `[Ruling wins]` `assess_debt` gained no parameter

The plan's task-1 action anticipates the possibility that the slot nests inside
`DebtAssessment`. It does not, so `assess_debt` gains nothing and stays pure
over `(factors, profile)`.
`tests/halflife/test_debt.py::test_assess_debt_is_unchanged_by_the_recency_slot`
asserts `debt` and `signal` are byte-for-byte what they were for the same
factors and the same profile, and that the assessment's field set is untouched.

### `[Ruling wins]` Task 2's acceptance criterion about `record.content_generation_path.debt`

Criterion 2 of task 2 says the recency should be *"reachable through
`record.content_generation_path.debt`"*. That was written for the nested shape
`D-IMPL-118` declined. Under the ruled shape it is reachable at
`record.content_generation_path.source_refresh_recency`, and the case asserts
that plus INV-CH7-80 by field enumeration — which is the criterion's actual
content (dimension 5, no sixth minted), satisfied at the correct address.

### `[Rule 1 — in scope]` Two field-enumeration guards fired and were updated

`tests/products/test_request_contract.py::test_no_content_address_moves_because_of_the_pin`
and
`tests/replay/test_harness_trace.py::test_every_pre_change_field_is_byte_for_byte_unchanged`
both failed on the new field — **which is what they are for**: a field landing
in the delivered bytes is a reviewable event. Both were updated to name
`source_refresh_recency` with the ruling behind it; the frozen
`PRE_CHANGE_CANONICAL` literal in the second was **not** retouched, only a new
sentinel-key `.replace()` step added, so the captured literal still stands as
evidence.
