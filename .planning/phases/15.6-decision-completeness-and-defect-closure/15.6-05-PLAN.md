<objective>
**Track B — gap 1, first limb.** `Observation` gains the identity that makes a
debt evidence pointer possible, and the one real assembler in the tree stops
throwing that identity away.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-99` ruled per-subject and per-claim debt attribution **NOT
MEASURABLE** because *"there is no structure in this tree to count a per-subject
cost against."* That was true of the **type** and was never true of the **call
site**. At `examples/s1-01-hello-context-product/deployment.py` the assembler
holds `record.claim.entity_uri` and `record.claim.lineage` in the loop variable
and discards both when it constructs the `Observation`. **Gap 1's first limb is
"stop discarding the join key," not "build one."**

Output: one field on `Observation`, one populated call site, and one
surface-change fragment.

**What the SDK owes and what it does not** (`D-IMPL-117`): an `Observation`
identity and a way for debt to point at what it summarises — and **nothing about
which observations matter**. The identity is an opaque string the deployment
chooses; `Observation.observation_type`'s own docstring already sets the voice
— *"Its vocabulary is user configuration; the SDK never enumerates it."* Copy
that sentence's discipline exactly. No `Literal`, no `StrEnum`, no default
identity scheme, no entity-URI type. `tests/invariants/test_no_obligation_vocabulary.py`
(plan `15.6-03`) scans `src/ccf/halflife/` and will fail on a closed vocabulary.

**The shape is Dave's, not the executor's.** `D-IMPL-118`'s Q-C ruling decides
required-versus-optional; `D-IMPL-118`'s Q-A ruling decides whether the identity
is a fixed field or declared content. Read the ruling first and implement it. If
the ruling was a hybrid, implement the hybrid as stated; do not normalise it to
the nearest option.

**This plan does not move the product content address.** `Observation` is an
*input* to debt computation, not a member of `ContextProductContract`. The
address moves in plan `15.6-06`, when `DebtAssessment` reshapes. Keeping the two
apart is why they are two plans.
</objective>
