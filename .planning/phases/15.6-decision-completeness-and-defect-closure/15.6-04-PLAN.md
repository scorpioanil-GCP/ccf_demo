<objective>
**Track B — the ruling gate.** Put the spike's measured findings to Dave and
record his rulings as `D-IMPL` entries. Everything in Waves 3 through 6 depends
on this.

Purpose: CLAUDE.md §15 is explicit — a whitepaper-silent design question is a
ruling, not an executor's default. *"Do not pick a default and hope."*
`D-IMPL-117` assigns the fixed-versus-declared measurement to the spike and the
**choice** to Dave, and the spike (`15.6-01`) deliberately recommends nothing.
This plan is where the choice is made.

**Two questions are mandatory, not one.**

1. **The fixed-versus-declared fork** (`D-IMPL-117`'s primary question). Are the
   three Track B carrying structures fixed fields on the vessel, or declared
   content in the shape the payload schema already uses?
2. **Does an evidence pointer leak entitlement?** — **new and unruled.**
   `D-IMPL-105` ruled the analogous question for *completeness*, and ruled it
   precisely because a caller could infer what they are not allowed to see. That
   argument transfers to evidence pointers and **no shipped ruling covers it.**
   Plan `15.6-01` task 2 measured it and offered no mitigation. Nobody below Dave
   may default it.

A third question rides along because it is Dave's by the same logic and would
otherwise force a second Track B checkpoint: **required or optional on
`Observation`.** `D-IMPL-97`'s *"every subclass is a place to forget"* argument
cuts toward required; `D-IMPL-38`'s no-default discipline makes optional
expressible without ambiguity. The cost split is 10 files against 13, with three
named artifacts breaking on the required shape. That is a governance trade, and
governance trades are Dave's.

Output: new `D-IMPL` entries in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, numbered from **118** (the
last shipped entry is `D-IMPL-117`; re-verify at this plan's base commit before
numbering).

**This plan writes no code and touches no `src/` file.** It converts measurement
into ruling.
</objective>
