## Findings worth carrying to the Wave-2 checkpoint

1. **The Q-E mechanism is not what the research says it is.** A 64-hex sweep
   finds **zero** literals in `examples/08-e2e-demo/artifacts/expected_output.txt`
   — the one file `15.6-RESEARCH.md` § B1 and `D-IMPL-116` both name as moving.
   The example prints its addresses **elided to a 16-hex prefix**, a form a
   full-length sweep is blind to; and what the file pins is the **replay-record
   address and the skill-set-in-force hash**, not the product content address.
   Both still move on a `DebtAssessment` reshape (the record carries
   `ContentGenerationPath`, which carries `debt`), so the count of one holds —
   but a planner budgeting for "regenerate the product address" would budget
   against the wrong mechanism. The spike sweeps both forms so the census cannot
   under-report again.

2. **Assumption A1 is confirmed by execution, not by docstring reading.** A
   reshaped `DebtAssessment` subclass survives assignment into the real
   `ContentGenerationPath.debt` field and is carried back onto the rebuilt
   product verbatim, reproducing the address exactly. The free ride is real.

3. **Q-B2 is a genuinely new question with a measured answer that is not
   reassuring in the direction one might assume.** The finding is not "the view
   is scoped, so a pointer is safe"; it is that **there is no perspective-scoped
   view at all** — the narrowing is `PolicyFilter`'s, keyed on
   deployment-declared parameters identically for every principal of the class.
   Whether that makes an evidence pointer safe or unsafe is Dave's ruling.

## Deviations from Plan

### Formulation decision (plan-sanctioned)

The plan's task-1 action states: *"If an executor finds a formulation of a
verdict that a later plan in this phase could falsify, that verdict is reported
instead, and the summary says which and why."* One formulation was considered
and kept as **asserted**, with the reasoning recorded here:

**`qa_toplevel`'s "not reconstructible" verdict.** The measurement constructs a
real `ContentGenerationPath` carrying no member that names the evidence token,
then observes that the token is absent from the canonical bytes of that record
and the projection. This is a property of the *constructed inputs*, not of the
shipped field list, so a later plan adding a defaulted member to
`ContentGenerationPath` does not falsify it — the spike does not populate the
new member, and the token stays absent. The one shape that would break it is a
new **required** member on `ContentGenerationPath`, which would break the
construction rather than the assertion, and which the tree's positive-sentinel
discipline (`NO_SCORER_PINS`, `NO_GRAPH_QUERIES`, `NO_PAYLOAD_SCHEMA`) makes
unlikely. Kept asserted because it is the single verdict that decides whether
placement 2 is viable at all, and reporting it would leave the ruling package's
load-bearing negative unguarded.

Symmetrically, `qa_nested`'s reconstructibility assertion depends on the shipped
`ContentGenerationPath.debt` field continuing to exist. Removing it would break
replay outright, so no plan in this phase is scoped to.

### Citation corrections made during execution

Two `path::Symbol` citations were wrong on first writing and were corrected
against the tree before the task-1 commit — `build_product_router` is actually
`build_router`, and `build_operator_registry` lives at
`src/ccf/deployment/operators.py`, not `src/ccf/deployment.py`. The door
enumeration now resolves its builder symbol by AST and asserts the resolution,
so that class of citation rot fails the suite rather than surviving in prose.
This is exactly the defect class the phase exists to close, demonstrating itself
inside the phase's own first plan.

### No auto-fixes were required

No Rule 1, 2 or 3 deviation fired. The plan forbids touching `src/`, `runtime/`
and `examples/`, and `git diff --name-only src/ runtime/ examples/` is empty for
all three commits.
