<objective>
**Track B — gap 3.** Each `DebtSignal` can carry a declared obligation, bound
through the Control Plane, resolved at replay to the binding **in force at
decision time**. The SDK ships the binding mechanism and **no obligation
vocabulary**.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-112` recorded the analyst's third missing item — *"what
operational obligation `forced_llm` actually imposes."* `15.6-RESEARCH.md` § B4
measured the state of play: `DebtSignal.FORCED_LLM` is produced at exactly one
site (`src/ccf/halflife/debt.py::assess_debt`) and **consumed nowhere**, while
`OK` and `FAIL_CLOSED` each have exactly one consumer and both actions are
whitepaper-grounded. That asymmetry is the gap, and it is also the strongest
evidence that the SDK is entitled to ship a *binding* mechanism: it already acts
on the other two signals.

Output: a binding field on `DebtProfile`, its resolution into the assessment, its
replay pinning, the Control Plane refusals, the regenerated published schema, and
one fragment.

**This is the single highest-risk item in Track B for the No-Hardcoding Rule.**
`D-IMPL-112` *quotes* three words an analyst used — re-pull, escalate,
accept-supervised-synthesis — and they read like a spec. They are not. Putting
any of them in `src/` is the CLAUDE.md §11 defect, and **every lint in this tree
would pass it**: `cinv01b-numeric-table` catches a numeric table;
`cinv01a-prompt-shape` scores prose length with `MIN_PROMPT_CHARS = 40`. A
three-member `StrEnum` of obligation names trips neither. Plan `15.6-03` shipped
`tests/invariants/test_no_obligation_vocabulary.py` in Wave 1 precisely so this
plan runs against an armed gate rather than a post-mortem.

**The line, stated so it cannot be crossed by accident.**

| The SDK owes | The SDK owes nothing of |
|---|---|
| A field on `DebtProfile` mapping each `DebtSignal` member to an **opaque** obligation reference | The reference's vocabulary — no obligation name appears in `src/` |
| Validation that the mapping's **keys** are `DebtSignal` members, a type the SDK owns | Validation that the **values** mean anything; structural only, `SkillRequirements`-style |
| Carrying the bound obligation into replay so a decision replays against the obligation in force **at the time** | Any default. An unbound signal is a positive assertion under a sentinel, never an implied "none" |
| Refusing a malformed mapping — wrong key type, duplicate, half-declared | Refusing an obligation string a reviewer dislikes |

**Why `DebtProfile` is the home for the calibration half.** It already owns
`forced_llm_threshold` and `fail_closed_threshold` with **no defaults**
(`D-IMPL-38`); it is a `VersionedArtifact` with a published schema; and it is
already pinned per-agent as `halflife_debt_profile: VersionedRef`, which
`D-IMPL-97` named as *"axis 5... the home for a product's debt configuration
already exists, on the agent, declared and version-pinned."* Putting the
*binding* anywhere else splits one calibration across two artifacts.
`D-IMPL-118`'s Q-A fork does not reach it: a Control Plane artifact is not the
product vessel.

**The assessment half is a different question, and the fork does reach it.**
Task 2 adds a member to `src/ccf/halflife/assessment.py::DebtAssessment`, which
rides `ContextProductContract.debt` and is therefore **inside the product content
address** — the same vessel `D-IMPL-118` ruled for plan `15.6-06`'s evidence
carrier and plan `15.6-07`'s recency slot. ROADMAP scopes the fork as *"are these
three things fixed fields on the vessel, or declared content in the shape the
payload schema already uses?"* — three things, and this member is the third. So
task 2 **implements the Q-A branch**, exactly as `15.6-07` task 1 does, rather
than assuming a fixed member because the other two took that shape. An earlier
draft of this plan asserted the fork was inapplicable to all of gap 3; that claim
holds for the `DebtProfile` half only, and is scoped to that half here.
</objective>
