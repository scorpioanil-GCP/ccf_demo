<objective>
**Track A — `DI-15.5-05`, and the Track A scoping questions Wave 8 needs.**
Two shipped refusal functions have no caller on any shipped delivery door. Rule
it or wire it — and that is a decision, not an executor's reading.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-113` ruled the analogous *bind* case as *"an adopter obligation
the SDK documents, not a door the SDK enforces,"* and its reasoning is structural
rather than case-specific: *"the door never holds the registered class —
`ProductResolverProtocol` hands the deployment three strings plus a perspective
and a request, and the class is resolved behind that seam."* That is a fact about
the **seam**, so it plausibly transfers to `refuse_request`. But `D-IMPL-113` also
created an obligation the SDK has **not** discharged, in its own words:
*"**Documented** is not the same as **mentioned**: the adopter has to be told, **at
the seam**, that a resolver which does not call `refuse_bound_payload` serves an
unvalidated payload against a schema its class declared."*

And the recorded population is wrong. `15.6-RESEARCH.md` § A3 measured it:
`refuse_request` **has one caller** —
`examples/s1-01-hello-context-product/spine.py` — and the deferred-items entry
saying "zero callers" was written **nine hours after that call site landed, in
the same phase**. It was wrong when written, not stale by drift. The correct
statement is narrower than either record: *no `runtime/` door calls it; one
shipped example does* — and that example is `D-IMPL-113`'s own reasoning
demonstrated, not a counterexample to it.

Output: a ruling, the seam documentation the ruling obliges, a test that asserts
it, a corrected deferred-items table, and a corrected `CPS-03` requirement row.

**This plan also carries the two Track A scoping questions Wave 8 and Wave 9
cannot start without.** Batching them here rather than opening a third checkpoint
is deliberate: `15.6-RESEARCH.md` warns that stacking ruling checkpoints in
flight complicates the gate package, and both questions are Track A's.
