<objective>
**Track B.** Measure what each candidate shape for the three Track B carrying
structures actually costs, so Dave rules on numbers rather than on projections.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-117` assigns the fixed-vs-declared fork to an in-phase spike
and the choice to Dave. `15.6-RESEARCH.md` § B2 carries a recommendation
explicitly labelled *"spike to confirm"*, and three of its assumptions (A1, A5,
A7) were reasoned rather than executed. A ruling package built on projections is
the defect this project has been bitten by before. This plan discharges the
assumptions before the ruling, not after it.

Output: `docs/DEBT_COMPLETENESS_RULING_EVIDENCE.md` — one measured figure per
open question, in a MEASURED COST column — plus the spike module that produced
the measurements, kept in-tree so a later reader can re-run them.

**This plan recommends nothing.** It costs the options Dave already has in front
of him and adds nothing new to decide. No option is ranked, preferred or
defaulted, and the acceptance criteria grep for that.

**Forbidden trees: `src/`, `runtime/`, `examples/`.** The spike measures; it does
not change. A source edit here would pre-empt the ruling, and the acceptance
criteria assert `git diff --name-only src/ runtime/ examples/` is empty.

**The spike lives inside the always-on suite, and that constrains what it may
assert.** `pyproject.toml` sets `testpaths = ["tests"]`, so `tests/spikes/` runs
under `make ci-core`, which must stay green from Wave 1 onward. Plans `15.6-05`,
`15.6-06`, `15.6-07` and `15.6-08` each move the very `Observation`,
`DebtAssessment` and `DebtProfile` shapes this module measures, and plans
`15.6-10` and `15.6-11` move the manifest and citation counts. A module
asserting any of those would go red on *correct* work by a later plan in its own
phase, and the only available repair would be to edit the evidence. Editing the
evidence to keep a gate green is the opposite of evidence. The separation of
**REPORTED** from **ASSERTED** is therefore load-bearing, not stylistic.

**Retiring owner: plan `15.6-12`, task 1. No other plan owns it.** The module is
a measurement instrument with a phase-length life. `15.6-12`'s phase-close sweep
either retires it or re-points it at the post-ruling tree, and accounts for the
choice in the gate package.
