## Three things this wave learned that the next close should not re-pay for

**1 · A silent-empty-review arrived through a new door.** The first nine invocations
returned `rc=126` in 0 seconds with 0-byte outputs — `Argument list too long`. Linux caps
a single argv string at 128 KB regardless of the 2 MB `ARG_MAX`. **Read through a pipe, or
not read at all, that is indistinguishable from three reviewers finding nothing.** Fixed
by feeding every payload on stdin, each smoke-tested first.

**2 · A resolved citation is not a correct finding.** All three reviewers passed the
read-proof (9/10, 10/10, 10/10 against controls of 0/2). gemini resolved **10/10** and
still inverted the sense of the text it cited, twice. The read-proof establishes that a
reviewer LOOKED; it does not establish that it UNDERSTOOD. Five findings were rejected
and are recorded as rejected.

**3 · The most valuable result of the depth pass OVERTURNED a panel finding.** Cursor
reported that the criterion-2 gate could stay green while every `sdk_mechanism` in the
recipe was fiction. The mechanism was right — the recipe test reads that field **zero**
times. The conclusion was wrong: `tools/lints/citations.py` scans `.yaml` and gates them.
**Settled by sabotage, not by reading**: a fabricated symbol moved the lint from rc=0 to
rc=1 naming file, line and symbol, while the test stayed 9 passed. **Acting on the finding
would have added a redundant assertion.**
