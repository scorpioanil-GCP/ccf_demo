**§3.2 capability decomposition** — per the Standard.

**§3.3 correlation key and single-writer discipline** — the correlation key this product
resolves on, and the CCF single-writer statement (CLAUDE.md §10): only L2 holds a write
handle to L3, so this product's rows enter the graph through the assembly worker and
through nothing else.

**THE THREE TRAPS, each stated with the binding chosen and what it costs.** Take the
bindings from `24.1-DATA-PROBE.md`; do not re-derive them and do not choose differently:

1. `report_period` is a spread of 46 distinct periods, `31-DEC-2014` → `31-MAR-2026`,
   because one filing window carries amended filings restating historical periods.
   **A query that does not pin the period silently mixes a decade of positions into one
   answer.** State the pinned period and where the pin is applied.
2. One issuer LEI fans out across many CUSIPs — one LEI in the estate reaches 52.
   Aggregating a holder's position across all CUSIPs for an LEI without a band filter
   over-counts. State the band binding and the CUSIP treatment.
3. `issuer_match_band = 'weak'` is 22.5% of rows and is a **deliberate** imperfection
   (`ccf_dataprep/docs/known-issues.md` §8.15). A weak band is a completeness signal,
   not a defect to filter away silently. **If the product filters to `strong`, say what
   that filter costs in rows and say that the cost is a completeness fact §E's debt
   model must account for.**

**CRITERION 9 — the educational statement, and it goes where a reader MEETS A FIGURE,
not in a footer.** Criterion 9's illustrative clause reads *"real LEIs, invented
behaviour"*. For THIS product that is only half right and the spec must be accurate
rather than echo it: the LEIs are real, the managers are real, the positions are real
SEC filings, and both tables this product reads are 100% `provenance = 'public'`
(22,476/22,476 and 156/156, measured). What a reader must be told instead, in three
statements placed beside the figures they qualify:

  (a) this is **one quarterly archive**, not a live feed;
  (b) **22.5% of issuer matches are `weak` by design**, not by defect;
  (c) the **estate around these two tables** carries synthetic and engineered rows
      (`synthetic — engineered` 30,365, `synthetic — internal` 13,840, `derived` 20)
      even though this product's two tables do not — and `ccf_v2.engineered_entity`
      carries no `lei` column at all, so no engineered entity can collide with an issuer
      LEI by construction.

Do NOT write "invented behaviour" about this data. It would be inaccurate in the other
direction, and presenting real SEC filings as invented is the same class of error as
presenting invented data as real. **Record this divergence from criterion 9's wording in
the plan's SUMMARY as a finding for Dave** — the criterion is discharged in substance
(no number reads as a fact about a real company without the reader being told what the
dataset is) and the clause that does not fit is named rather than quietly satisfied.
