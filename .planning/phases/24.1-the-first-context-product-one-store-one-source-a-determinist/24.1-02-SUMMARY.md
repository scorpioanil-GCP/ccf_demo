## TWO INSTRUMENTS OF MY OWN THAT WERE WRONG, AND WHAT THEY WOULD HAVE COST

Both were caught by a control, which is the only reason they are in this table
and not in the document.

### 1. A lint invocation that reported green having checked nothing

`python -m tools.lints.citations` exits **0** with a broken citation and a
fabricated symbol both sitting in the tree. I ran it, saw `EXIT=0`, and would
have recorded the file as lint-clean on that reading. The control — seeding
`_probe.md` with `src/ccf/products/gates.py::no_such_symbol_at_all` — returned
**0 as well**, which is what exposed it. The real gate is
`python -m tools.lints --all`; under the same seed it exits **1** and names
both defects by rule. **A module that is importable is not necessarily a
gate**, and this is the phase's own signature defect arriving inside the
phase's own verification.

### 2. A criteria control that deleted the wrong criterion 12

My positive control for "a deleted criterion is named by number" removed the
first line in `.planning/ROADMAP.md` starting `  12. ` — and **six** lines in
that file start that way, one per phase that has a twelfth criterion. It
deleted another phase's, Phase 24.1's block was untouched, and the detector
correctly reported *no change*. Read at face value that is a control that
"failed" and would have discredited a working instrument. Re-aimed by scoping
the deletion to the text after `### Phase 24.1:`, it fires: openers **18**,
`missing = [12]`. **Same class as Wave 1's tautological control** — a control
that does not act on the subject is not a control.

## The three tasks

### Task 1 — front matter and §A

All ten §0.4 elements above §A. Assembly Mode **1** with its one-sentence
rationale (`D-14`), tier 1, cost band ×1, lifecycle **draft**, build-order note
recording that `NN` is build order and `CP-SPEC-000` is the spec id and the two
are deliberately different numberings (`D-18`/`D-19`).

The four-question build test is answered **No · Yes · Yes · Partial** and
admitted on that stated basis. Inflating it to four yeses would have been the
easy write; the Standard asks for the qualified answer in writing so a reviewer
can challenge the admission, and `CP-SPEC-001` sets the same precedent.

§2.3's non-applicability is written as one line under its own heading rather
than omitted, per §0.3.

**Two front-matter fields carry `[TBC]` rather than an invented name:** the
approved domain list, and the accountable-owner triad. Naming a bank function
as the owner of a teaching artifact would be the §0.2 defect — *"a spec with
invented system names is not buildable"*.

§A names the decision (position concentration on one issuer), the role
(covering analyst), the trigger (a quarterly filing window), the counterfactual
from the **closed taxonomy** (`temporally-incoherent`, with `context-starved`
second), and writes the counterfactual as a runnable acceptance test.

**One honesty point inside that test.** Its period half asserts a *condition*,
not a prediction: 1,989 rows sit outside the pinned period estate-wide, but
whether the anchor itself carries out-of-pin rows was never measured, so the
test says so rather than implying a measurement nobody took. Its CUSIP half is
anchor-specific and measured (5 off-security managers, 10 rows of over-count).

### Task 2 — §B, nine elements

All nine written; none omitted; each of the Standard's "common error" traps
named where this spec could have fallen into it.

**The TTL, and the consequence that is owed to later waves.**
`ttl_anchor: from_entity_freshness` anchored on the reported quarter end, per
`D-22`, with the distinction written out: *"there should be a timestamp and
there isn't"* is a data defect, *"the dataset reports per period and the spec
anchors on that period"* is a normal declaration, **and this product is the
second sentence**. Duration declared at **365 days** — the amendment window,
because what makes this answer wrong is a restatement of *that* period, not the
arrival of the next one.

**Two structural consequences, both new to this wave and both binding on
Waves 4–6:**

1. `expires_at` = `2026-03-31` + 365 days = **`2027-03-31`**.
2. Because the anchor is the reported quarter and not the request instant,
   **the product's servability decays with the archive's age.** Gate 3 requires
   `now < expires_at`, so once the pinned period is more than 365 days old the
   product is **refused** — the gate working, not a defect. The class's
   `MAX_TTL` artifact must therefore declare **≥ 31,536,000 seconds** or the
   same gate refuses at the *upper* bound instead. **The example has a shelf
   life, and it is written down rather than discovered in 2027.**

**Lineage** is written as a member-by-member mapping onto the SDK surface, with
two honest entries the plan asked for and one it did not:

- `context_debt` and `payload_hash` are both present. `payload_hash` is named
  as a **derived content address** (`src/ccf/contracts/canonical.py::content_hash`,
  recorded as `product_content_address`) rather than claimed as a field on the
  contract type, because it is not one.
- The two absences — passage anchors and `model_record` — keep their rows and
  say why, rather than being dropped.

**§B.4 declares exactly one caller parameter** (`D-12`), required, **no
default**, refused as data by `src/ccf/products/request.py::refuse_request`
before the graph is touched. Percent-of-class is recorded as **not derivable**
with the measurement behind it (`%Shares%` → `[]` against a `%Revenue%` control
returning four tags).

### Task 3 — §C, and criterion 9

Element table with **one system of record**, the counter-intuitive `lei` column
named as the **issuer's** in prose, and the two `derived` rows separated by
**who** derives them: the resolved security is this assembly's and appears in
§D; the issuer-match band is the *source system's* and no §D step will be
invented to satisfy the Standard's rule about derived elements.

Capability decomposition yields one likely partial (group-and-rank inside a
read) with a stated interim posture and a version string — `v1.0.0-partial` —
that says it is partial.

Correlation keys at two grains, with **measured** crosswalk coverage (48 of 48;
control 0 of 48), and the single-writer statement split into two claims that
are genuinely different: the source estate's, and CCF's.

**The three traps, each with its binding and cost:**

| Trap | Binding | Cost recorded |
|---|---|---|
| 46-period spread | pin `31-MAR-2026` **at the L1 read** | 1,989 rows outside the pin are not served — other quarters' answers |
| CUSIP fan-out | **RESOLVE** from `(lei, period)` to the dominant CUSIP; **no pinned literal** (`D-39`/`D-40`/`D-41`) | an extra §D step, accepted; the 5 off-security managers are not served, which is the point |
| 22.5% `weak` | **no band filter**; the band travels with the row | the alternative costs **115 managers (6.6%)** and **$2.77bn (2.4%)** and removes **zero** wrong-security rows |

The spec states explicitly that the "fuzzy name match admitted the off-security
rows" framing is **measured false** — all five are `strong` at 1.0000 — so a
later reader cannot re-inherit it from the research file.

**Criterion 9 is discharged as `D-34` ruled it, and no finding is raised.** All
four `D-35` statements appear **beside every figure cluster** — the banner, §A,
§B and three times in §C — not in a footer. `invented behaviour` appears
nowhere, and the spec says so deliberately: these rows are public filings, and
being inaccurate in the cautious direction is the same class of error as being
inaccurate in the confident one.

## What I am handing forward, not deciding

1. **The example has an expiry.** After `2027-03-31` the declared TTL puts
   `expires_at` in the past and gate 3 refuses. Waves 4–6 inherit it; whether
   the answer is a longer duration, a moving pin, or an accepted end-of-life is
   not this wave's call. **Flagged now rather than found by a red CI run.**
2. **Two Standard requirements this SDK does not meet**, recorded in §B.4 and
   **not closed** (`D-04`): `debt_tolerance` is Control Plane state
   (`src/ccf/controlplane/artifacts.py::DebtProfile`) rather than a caller
   parameter — which is the *better* design on the merits, since a caller who
   can move its own fail-closed band can decide to be served context the
   deployment ruled too stale; and an unsupported parameter is refused as
   `undeclared-parameter` rather than under the Standard's
   `not_supported_at_tier` name. Wave 9 owns the gap register and the drift
   check (`D-05`); these are inputs to it, not verdicts.
3. **No ontology IRI binding contract exists in this deployment.** Six of the
   twelve `[TBC]` markers are IRIs. `cmns-org:LegalEntity` is bound because the
   source estate's own record binds it; nothing else could be bound without
   inventing a term, and the Standard rates an invented binding worse than a
   marked gap.
