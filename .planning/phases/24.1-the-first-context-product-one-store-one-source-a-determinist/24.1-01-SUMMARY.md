## A PREMISE IN THE PHASE'S OWN RESEARCH THAT DOES NOT HOLD

`24.1-RESEARCH.md` §A.5 item 2 describes the off-security CUSIPs under the
anchor as *"admitted by a fuzzy issuer-name match."* **Measured: all five are
band `strong` at `issuer_match_score = 1.0000`, and the `weak` rows sit
entirely on the correct security `032095101`.**

Consequence, and it changes what a later plan would have built: **filtering to
`strong` drops 115 real holders (6.6% of managers, $2.77bn of position) and
removes ZERO wrong-security rows.** The band filter and the CUSIP fan-out are
two different problems and `strong` is the remedy for neither. Hence the two
rulings above. Reported here rather than patched into the research file.

Separately reconciled, so nobody reads a contradiction: the research's
`1,647 strong / 201 weak` is the **all-period** figure; this session's
`1,633 / 115` is the **pinned-period** figure. Different bound, both correct —
which is itself the argument for pinning.

## A control of my own that I threw away

My first control for "managers only off the dominant CUSIP" was the same query
with `cusip <> 'ZZZZZZZZZ'`. It returned **5** — the same number as the figure.
That is **tautological**, not passing: any manager not on the dominant CUSIP is
by definition on another. Replaced with the reciprocal set difference, which
returns **1,742**. Recorded in `24.1-DATA-PROBE.md` §6.5 rather than deleted,
because it is the same defect class this wave exists to catch, arriving inside
the wave's own probe.

## Findings beyond what the criteria say

1. **Criterion 17's `265` count is not a deletion detector.** Under
   `rm <tracked file>` the count stayed **265** (the index is unchanged); under
   `git mv` it stayed **265** (it is a rename). The count, the `D` filter and
   the `R` filter each miss what the other two catch — the criterion names all
   three, and now it is measured why.
2. **A criterion count is not a bound check either.** Under the WRONG block
   bound (`**Plans:**` instead of `**Requirements:**`) the opener count was
   still **19**; only the continuation count (3 spurious) and the sha caught it.
