### 2.2 THE INSTRUMENT FINDING — a control that agreed while the reading disagreed

**This wave's own criteria check produced a FALSE STOP, and the positive control
beside it did not catch it. Recorded rather than smoothed over, because it is the
`D-65` class arriving inside the machinery `D-65` was written to police.**

What happened, in order:

1. This task first **re-implemented** the detector from `24.1-BASE.md`'s prose
   description rather than running the bound instrument from `24.1-01-PLAN.md`.
   The re-implementation ended its extraction with `.strip("\n")`.
2. The canonical capture is
   `re.search(r'(?s)beside the count\.\n\n(.*?)\n\*\*Requirements:\*\*', sec)`,
   whose group ends `'…roof the chunk list IS the whole recipe\n'` — **one
   trailing newline** the re-implementation removed.
3. The re-implementation reported sha **`c198659e…`**, *"MATCH = False"*, and
   `exit=1` — a **STOP** on a block that had not changed by a single byte.

**The control that should have caught it could not.** `24.1-BASE.md` records
control B — criterion 12 deleted — at sha `bf1aee1b…3ef35`, and the
re-implementation reproduced `bf1aee1bd69ac23919f86c9e2c452a6ee6950c93e031f86ae2d8d08b7453ef35`
**exactly**. That agreement read as proof the extraction matched. It was not:

| Block | live sha | control-B sha |
| --- | --- | --- |
| canonical (trailing `\n` kept) | `15d3ef66…c9300f` | `bf1aee1b…3ef35` |
| stripped (trailing `\n` removed) | `c198659e…81172` | `bf1aee1b…3ef35` |

Control B is built as `"\n".join(block.splitlines())`, and `splitlines()`
discards a trailing newline. **The control is blind to the exact degree of freedom
the live sha turns on**, so it returns the same value under both normalizations
and cannot distinguish a correct reader from an incorrect one. It agreed with
Wave 1 while the measurement disagreed, and its agreement was evidence of nothing.

**The lesson, stated as a rule:** a positive control has to be sensitive to the
same degree of freedom the measurement turns on. `D-65` names *"positive control
on the reader"*; this adds that a control which normalizes away the measured
property is a control in name only. Filed as a Wave-10 finding in §6.

**Both of Wave 1's controls were nonetheless re-run this session and both fired**
(under the stripped normalization, which is the one the controls are insensitive
to — so they demonstrate the detector's *classification*, which is what they were
built for):

| Control | Expected | Observed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — criterion 7 re-wrapped mid-sentence** | openers STAY 19, continuations RISE by one | openers **19**, continuations **0 → 1**, fragment named: `'lives in definition artifacts, asserted by the No-Hardcoding lint (`CLAUDE.md` §'`, sha moves |
| **B — criterion 12 deleted** | openers DROP to 18, gap check NAMES 12 | openers **18**, `missing vs 1..19 = [12]`, contiguity `False`, sha moves to `bf1aee1b…3ef35` |

Control A is what proves the count alone would not have caught a silent re-wrap:
under a re-wrap the **opener count stays 19**.
