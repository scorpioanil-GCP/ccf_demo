Write a throwaway detector (a shell pipeline or a short Python script under
`/tmp`, NOT committed to the repo — no new tracked file) that:

1. Extracts the criteria block from `.planning/ROADMAP.md`'s `### Phase 24.1:` section:
   the lines between the paragraph ending *"…required beside the count."* and the next
   bolded field line (`**Plans:**` today, `**Requirements:**` if the plan list is ever
   removed). **Bound it on the paragraph's closing sentence, not on a field name that a
   later edit can insert ahead of** — planning itself inserted `**Plans:**` into that gap
   on 2026-08-25, which is the exact drift this bound exists to survive.
   Measured at planning time: **19 openers, 3 continuation lines**, sha256
   `a965b8cbbf1700e08a6d04b1b937a5a246e35b9e5d4734fd185510f74bcdaa20`. Re-derive it; do
   not copy that value forward without re-running the detector.
2. Classifies each non-blank physical line as either a CRITERION OPENER (matches
   `^\s{2}[0-9]+\. `) or a CONTINUATION (anything else). Reports both counts.
3. Asserts the opener count is **19** and that the opener numbers are exactly 1..19 with
   no gap and no repeat.
4. Records the sha256 of the extracted block verbatim.

POSITIVE CONTROLS, both required and both recorded:

   populations rather than counting lines.
 - Feed it a copy with criterion 12 deleted. The opener count must drop to 18 AND the
   gap check must name 12 as missing.

Write into `24.1-BASE.md`: the opener count, the continuation count, the sha256, and
both control results. **This sha256 is what every later plan re-checks against.** No
plan in this phase may author, edit, renumber or re-wrap a criterion line; the sha256
is how that is proven rather than promised.
