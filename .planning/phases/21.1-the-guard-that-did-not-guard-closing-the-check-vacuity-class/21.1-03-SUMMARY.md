## `_REPO_INDEX_CACHE` is restored by the control, not left thinned

`_REPO_INDEX_CACHE` is process-lifetime state. A control that left junk in it would poison every test running after it — this phase's own defect class arriving through the control rather than through the check. `CONTROL 22`:

- saves the real mapping before touching anything;
- clears the cache around each subject so `_index()` recomputes;
- restores in a **`finally`** that undoes the monkeypatch, clears the cache a second time and puts the saved contents back; and
- **asserts the restoration worked** — `_index()` is called once more after the `finally` and required to equal what it returned before the control ran.

That last assertion is what makes the restoration a checked property rather than a claim. The whole module runs in one process and is green (65 passed) with `CONTROL 1` and the positive citation control both passing.
