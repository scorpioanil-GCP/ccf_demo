## The `guard` kind is eighteen entries, not two, and that was the plan's one real gap

The plan defines five kinds and then says `CONTROL 32` and `CONTROL 33` take `guard`. Worked through the actual control set, **nineteen controls fit none of the five** — `CONTROL 1`–`4b`, `6`, `7`, `9`, `10`, `11`, `14`, `15`, `17`, `19`, `23`, `29`, `32`, `33`. Each of them holds a checker that **returns its findings rather than raising a limb**: `channel_disagreements`, `vocabulary_offenders`, `doubled_verdicts`, `unverdicted`, `uncited_verdicts`, `roster_rows_with_two_verdicts`, `refuse_unresolvable`, `citations_in_status_cells`.

There is no bucket for them, and criterion 13 quantifies over **every** control. Two readings were available:

| Reading | Consequence |
|---|---|
| `guard` is exactly `CONTROL 32`/`33` | nineteen controls have no kind; `CONTROL 34` cannot be written without a seventh, unnamed disposition, and seventeen of the module's controls stay aim-unproven |
| `guard` is *"the control holds one of the module's own guard mechanisms"* — the plan's own words | every control has a kind, and the same inverted obligation the plan already specifies for `CONTROL 32`/`33` extends to all eighteen unchanged |

The second is taken, and it is the plan's literal definition rather than a widening of it. The block that argues `CONTROL 32`/`33` are `guard` argues they are *"ordinary controls over two ordinary in-module guards"* — which is exactly what the other sixteen are.

**The cost is real and it was paid:** eighteen saboteurs, one per entry, each carried in the registry entry as data. **The benefit is that `CONTROL 36b` proves twenty-one aim pairs rather than three**, and no control in this module is registered-but-unproven except the two `pathspec` aims that `21.1-08` owns by design.

**Why the fourth is unreachable, proven rather than asserted.** `twin == line` requires `line[m.start(1):m.end(1)] == flipped`, and `flipped` is `"x"` unless `m.group(1).lower() == "x"`, in which case it is `" "`. Both cases are contradictions: `group(1) == "x"` forces `flipped == " "`, and `group(1) == " "` forces `flipped == "x"`. No tick in the class `[ xX]` reaches it, and **no substitution of `CHECKBOX_LINE` by a real regex reaches it either**, because the contradiction is in the flip and not in the pattern.

So it is reached by substituting a **pattern object** whose match reports a group VALUE the flip reads as "not a tick" and a group SPAN that already holds an `x` — so the twin rewrites the character to itself. That is a control fixture in this module's established idiom (`CONTROL 22` substitutes `repo_index`, `CONTROL 29` substitutes `_index`), **not a reshaping of the shim**, which Dave rejected as *"new mechanism in a prove-existing-mechanism wave"*.
