The obvious duplicate check is `len(rows) == len(set(ids))`. **Do not write it.** A self-comparison
is not a carried count and would survive `D-21` on a technicality, but it fails the module's own
idiom and criterion 9's spirit: it reports a SHORTFALL rather than a ROW, and a shortfall is not
something a reader can act on. `unverdicted` at `:1675` says so in its own docstring.

Write it the way the module writes everything else: build the multiset of ids, and refuse naming
EVERY id that appears more than once. The message shape research measured is "duplicate requirement
ids in the traceability table: ['HARD-02']".
