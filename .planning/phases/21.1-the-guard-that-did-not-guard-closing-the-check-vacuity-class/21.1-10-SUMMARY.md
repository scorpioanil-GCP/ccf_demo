# 21.1-10 — the §3.1 close

## One-liner

The close panel found the phase's own defect class four more times, in the machinery
built to close it; all four are fixed in-phase, and `D-GATE-21.1` is approved.

## What ran, in §3.1's order

Execution → cross-AI panel on the **shipped tree** → additive single-agent review →
every finding verified against the tree → blocking findings fixed in-phase → present
to Dave → ruling. Nine commits, `d478d06`..`e3c6c6d`, plus `fd08b9e`.

## The panel

`D-IMPL-178`'s payload: the committed tree, hand-assembled, **553 KB / 8,272 lines
inlined** — the whole check module line-numbered, the whole §Traceability table at its
real file line numbers, `21-DOCS-16-SPLIT.md` and `21-FINDINGS.md`. **Left out and
named:** this phase's ten PLAN and nine SUMMARY documents, which are the
pre-execution record.

Source inlined rather than pointed at by path because on this host `codex` runs under
`bwrap` and has exited 0 having read nothing when the sandbox failed to start. It
emitted that warning three times on this run and the review was unaffected — the
mechanism working, observed.

The panel was **briefed on the known-and-handled list and told not to re-derive it**,
and asked instead what is *adjacent* to each. That instruction produced the blocker.

**gemini, codex and cursor all REVIEWED**; `claude` skipped for §3.1 independence.
22 findings, every citation resolved against the tree individually rather than
sampled. **Five rejected**, recorded as rejections with their refuting evidence — and
one of those was *caused by the executor's own brief*, which paraphrased
`D-IMPL-123`'s ceiling scope loosely; that cause is named rather than left as the
reviewer's error.

## The single-agent review

Additive, never a substitute. It read the module in full, established its own baseline
(**94 passed in 4.23s**) and reproduced every finding **in-process** against that green
tree. Nine findings. Its negative results are recorded too — no leak path around
`CONTROL 32`/`33`, no silent `derived_base` degradation under rebase / squash /

## Three things that happened during the fixes, recorded because they are the evidence

**1. A close fix regenerated the phase's defect class, and the machinery caught it in
seconds.** Adding `_index.thinned_index` made `CONTROL 22` — which asserted
`"index" in str(failure)`, a substring **both** limbs satisfy — go GREEN with the limb
it names neutered. `CONTROL 35` reported it by name. That is the `CONTROL 5` defect
wave 5 fixed, reproduced by the executor and caught by the instrument this phase
built. `CONTROL 22` now asserts the limb id.

**2. A red for the wrong reason nearly passed for a caught defect, inside the
verification of a finding about exactly that.** The first `fabric_diff` sabotage
reddened the suite — via an `IndexError` on the empty-paths branch with a limb
neutered, not `CONTROL 18` catching anything. Taken at face value, the blocking
finding would have been recorded **REJECTED**. The empty-safe sabotage is the one in
the record.

**3. The `D-21` sweep caught wave 10's own constant.** `PHASE_OWN_PHASES` was added
without being rostered in `FIXED_SETS`, and the sweep built in wave 9 named it. The
guard held against the wave that came after it.
