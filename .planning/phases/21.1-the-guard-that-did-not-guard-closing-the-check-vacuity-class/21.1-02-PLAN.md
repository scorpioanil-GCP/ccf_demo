<objective>
Close `RR-2`: two readers disagree about what a cell says and neither refuses. `verdict_token` strips
bold and reads the LEADING alphabetic run; `doubled_verdicts` reads only BOLDED runs. A cell reading
`Complete — later **Partial** (…)` is read as `Complete` by the first, yields one bolded verdict to
the second, and passes EIGHT gates with ZERO refusals — measured on `HARD-02`'s real cell with its
citations left intact, so the mutation is attributable to the verdict alone. A working-set row that
visibly reads `**Partial**` is reported `Complete` by the entire module.

Purpose: the ledger's two verdict readers are the ledger's own definition of what a row claims. When
they can disagree in silence, every downstream verdict is a coin toss the module never sees.

Output: one predicate that returns the SET of verdicts a cell asserts, consumed by both
`doubled_verdicts` and `roster_rows_with_two_verdicts`, and `CONTROL 19` handing the shipped function
that exact shape.
</objective>

<the_trap>
**Two obvious alternative rules are traps and both were measured. Do not choose either.**

- *"Any vocabulary word appearing anywhere in the cell"* flags **13 of the 143 real rows** —
  `EXAM-02`, `DOCS-14`, `DOCS-15`, `DOCS-18`, `CPS-03`, `DCD-02`, `DCD-09`, `DCD-10`, `CLNT-05`,
  `RECON-01`, `RECON-02`, `RECON-04` and one more. Several of those Dave has personally ruled on.
  `D-09` fixed the vocabulary at six words precisely to stop this.
- *"The leading verdict must be bolded"* flags **69 of the 143** — a bare leading verdict is a
  long-standing convention across `HARD-04`, the `PERF-`, `DEMO-`, `ERGO-`, `SUBQ-` and `CPS-` lanes,
  `CLNT-23`..`32` and `DOCS-22`.

**The rule that works, measured at 0 of 143:** the set `{verdict_token(cell)} ∪ {bolded runs of the
cell that are in VERDICT_VOCABULARY}` must have at most one member.

Supporting measurements, which are what make that rule safe rather than lucky: 74 rows bold their
leading verdict and 69 do not; ZERO bare-lead rows carry any bolded vocabulary word; ZERO rows carry
a bolded vocabulary word different from their leading token. **Consequence: this fix requires NO
ledger edit and surfaces no hidden bad row.** Criterion 11's exception clause is unused on this axis.
</the_trap>

<residual_to_state_not_design_around>
The union rule does NOT catch probe shape `D5` — `Complete — later Partial (…)` with BOTH verdicts
unbolded. `verdict_token` reads `Complete`, there is no bolded vocabulary word, and the disagreement
is invisible to every reader including this fix.

Criterion 3 names *"an unbolded leading verdict and a later **bolded** different verdict"* and
criterion 4 asks that the two readers cannot disagree without one refusing; the union rule satisfies
both AS WRITTEN. `D5` is a WIDER shape than the criteria name. **Name it in the SUMMARY as a known
residual — `R-α` — with the measurement. Do not silently claim it is closed, and do not reach for a
stronger predicate: every stronger predicate measured flags rows Dave has ruled on.**
</residual_to_state_not_design_around>
