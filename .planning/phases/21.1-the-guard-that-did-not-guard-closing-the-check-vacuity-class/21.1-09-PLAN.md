<the_ledger_rules_that_bite_here>
Four properties of this module constrain how a verdict may be written. All four are measured.

1. **A verdict other than `Not built` or `Orphaned` must cite evidence in its status cell**, or
   `uncited_verdicts` refuses the row — and once `PHASE_OWN_ROSTER` includes the six, that gate reads
   them.
2. **A citation must be a full repo-relative existing path.** A bare basename is refused, a directory
   is refused, and a `::symbol` on a NON-Python path is refused — the lint's symbol pattern is
   hard-anchored to `.py`. Where the evidence is a markdown document (`VAC-03`), use an anchored line
   citation, which is the shape `D-IMPL-172` settled.
3. **An anchored line citation is weaker than it looks.** Phase 21 found one of its own rulings citing
   a line that had moved 38 lines, and found a sabotage report green because a neighbouring entry
   cross-cited the target. Re-resolve every line citation AT WRITE TIME, read the line you are citing,
   and say in the SUMMARY that you did.
4. **Flipping a verdict means flipping its checkbox.** `channel_disagreements` compares the leading
   verdict's membership in the Complete family against the tick. Both channels move together or the
   module refuses.
</the_ledger_rules_that_bite_here>
