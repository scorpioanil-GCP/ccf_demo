# Phase 12 Plan 01: Deterministic Synthetic Banking Data Store Summary

Deterministic 3-source synthetic banking substrate (1160 accounts, 3600 txns, 905 wires, 1205 KYC entries, one planted 5-member circular-payment ring) generated by a pure interpreter of scenario.yaml, with checksums pinned in the artifact and enforced by pytest.

## What Was Built

- **scenario.yaml** carries every domain value: seed 20260812, fixed epoch, three sources (`core_banking`, `wires`, `kyc_registry`) with full table/column vocabulary, a 5-member fraud ring (M1→M2→M3→M4→M5→M1 wire edges) whose per-source identifiers never string-match, all noise lexicons/counts, and the `expected` pin block (row counts, per-source sha256 checksums, ring signature, cycle members).
- **generator.py** interprets the artifact with zero vocabulary in code: sources selected by role (`ledger`/`transfer_log`/`registry`), tables by role (`entities`/`movements`), columns by role keys. Five documented RNG stream offsets; ring rows written verbatim from the artifact and shuffled among noise; parameterized SQL only; infeasible configs raise ValueError.
- **test_demo_generator.py** (4 tests, ~0.8s): regenerate-twice equality, expected-block pin comparison, ring presence + signature + single-cycle closure, plain-sqlite3 row counts.
