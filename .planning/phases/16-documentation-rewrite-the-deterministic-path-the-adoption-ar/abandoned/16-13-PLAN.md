<objective>
Publish Section 1 — the deterministic path — and close UAT gap G-01's framing
defect **by construction**, not by editing the pages that carried it.

Purpose: `D-12` is explicit that Section 1 being deterministic and agent-free is
the structural fix, so the agent-first mental model the UAT condemned *cannot
survive the new structure*. Three specific defects retire here: the Concepts
data-context page opening on *"how does an agent get at first-party records"*,
the buried line-133 acknowledgement that the SDK ships no agents, and the
**absence of any how-to for deterministic Context Product assembly**.

The six axes of `G(E, R, T, P, Π, L)` are genuinely new content: `grep "six
axes"` over `public/` returns three hits and all but one are about the six
*specialization axes of an agent*. This is the page a beginner needs first and
the tree has never had.

**Nav discipline.** `mkdocs.yml`'s `nav:` enumerates every path explicitly with
no globs, and under `validation.nav.omitted_files: warn` plus `--strict` a page
absent from nav fails the build and a nav entry whose file was deleted fails the
build. That is why each page-publishing plan owns nav in its own wave.

Output: three new pages, one rewritten pillar page, two re-cut reference pages,
and nav.
</objective>
