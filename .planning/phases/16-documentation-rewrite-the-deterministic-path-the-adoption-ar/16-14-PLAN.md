<objective>
Write the argument the published surface has never made: **why CCF, and what
breaks in what teams build instead.**

Purpose: UAT gap G-01's second half measured the entire published tree and found
**zero** hits for "why not just", "instead of building", "rather than building"
and "compared to". No page in this project has ever made a comparative-rationale
argument, which `16-PATTERNS.md` records as a genuine `NO ANALOG` — the *form*
can be borrowed from `public/concepts/invariants.md`, but the *genre* is new.

`D-10` fixes the content: name what teams build instead — RAG over a vector
store, hand-rolled context assembly, a semantic layer plus prompt templates — and
state what each lacks: no replay, no policy binding at assembly, no lineage to a
passage, no staleness measure. The worked demonstration is the UBO question,
*"Who ultimately owns Acme?"*, which requires traversing an ownership chain,
aggregating percentages across hops, and filtering by jurisdiction-specific
thresholds. A vector store over chunked documents structurally cannot answer it,
because the answer lives in the **relationships**, not in any passage.

The page's force depends on plan 16-02: on the shipped FIBO data the question
was answerable in one hop, and one hop is retrievable. That is why the data
extension was a requirement-bearing early-wave deliverable and why this page can
only be written now.

`D-10` also records the accepted cost: the docs now owe a defence of these
comparisons as the alternatives evolve. Say so on the page rather than in the
plan.

Output: one page, one standing gate that is the exact inverse of G-01's
measurement, the cross-link plan 16-13 deferred, and a human read.
</objective>
