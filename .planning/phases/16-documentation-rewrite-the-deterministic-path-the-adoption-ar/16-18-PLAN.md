<objective>
The terminal retirement: replace `tutorial/adopters-guide.md`, retire the last
seven examples, and prove there is no partial state.

Purpose: `tutorial/adopters-guide.md` is the phase's single hardest scheduling
constraint — 447 lines, **35 include directives, six of the ten examples**
(`00-quickstart`, `01-agent-basics`, `02-skilling`, `05-workflow-hitl`,
`06-decision-replay`, `external/00-hello-context`). It cannot be moved
incrementally, which is why RESEARCH R-1's mitigation was to leave it untouched
until the new section tutorials existed as new files in a new nav position and
then delete it in one commit. That commit is this plan.

`tutorial/demo-walkthrough.md` is the same shape at smaller scale: 14 includes
from `examples/08-e2e-demo` plus 6 prose-only references. It carries **DEMO-03**,
whose re-homing target is `public/tutorial/hello-ccf.md` per plan 16-01's
requirement ledger.

Everything this plan deletes has already had its dependent pages re-cut by plans
16-13, 16-15, 16-16 and 16-17. What remains is the adopters guide, the demo
walkthrough, and the mechanical proof that nothing else points at a retiring
path.

**And what the plan review found: `public/` was never the whole surface.** The
§ A.2 retirement inventory swept `examples/`, `tests/examples/` and `public/`
only, while `[tool.pytest] testpaths = ["tests"]` puts every gate under
`tests/` in `make ci`'s always-on path. Four gates outside the swept trees
hard-point at what this plan deletes: the named artifact-directory set and its
permanently-deferred entry (`tests/examples/test_artifact_schema_headers.py`),
four headroom-free non-vacuity floors
(`tests/products/test_gate_two_call_shape.py`,
`tests/products/test_required_operators_guard.py`), the CLI schema-export
fixture (`tests/cli/test_schema_export.py`), and the publish-scan clean-page
tuple (`tools/publish_scan_tests/test_publish_scan.py`). Plan 16-04's
`tools/retirement_sweep.py` (plan 16-21) measured them repo-wide and assigned them here;
this plan closes them **in the same commits as the deletions that break them**.

Output: two pages replaced, seven examples and their test modules gone, the
Makefile down to five examples, four out-of-tree gates repointed, four
non-vacuity floors re-counted with the removal recorded in git, final nav, and
every standing gate green.
</objective>
