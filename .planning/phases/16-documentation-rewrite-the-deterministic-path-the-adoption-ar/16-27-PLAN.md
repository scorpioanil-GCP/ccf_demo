<objective>
Discharge `D-IMPL-135`: `ProductDiscovery` gains a fourth axis, `audiences:
frozenset[str]`, on the same footing as `domain`, `intents` and `capabilities`
— opaque, deployment-declared, uninterpreted — and the **full surface
obligation** that a change to a shipped §6.3 element carries is discharged in
the same plan rather than deferred to a phase-close sweep.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-136`'s `product_class.discovery` section (design spec §5.1)
advertises `audiences: [analyst, investment_banker]`. There is nowhere for that
to land today. Dave's question that produced the ruling was whether
`Perspective` was the placeholder for advertising who a product serves; it is
not — `ccf.projection.plan::Perspective` is the role-conditioning tuple Π
interprets under, derived at a door from the caller's grant. Conditioning a view
and advertising an audience are different things, and the ruling keeps them
apart by giving the advertisement its own axis.

This plan is deliberately **first and alone**. It is a surface change to a
shipped element; `D-IMPL-135` says in terms that folding it into the manifest
bookkeeping ruling would leave *"a later reader tracing why `ProductDiscovery`
moved"* with nothing.

Output: one field, one restated constant, one test module, one surface
fragment, a regenerated `docs/SURFACE-CHANGES.md`, a regenerated
`docs/schemas/ccf.product-class.json`, and four new requirement rows.
