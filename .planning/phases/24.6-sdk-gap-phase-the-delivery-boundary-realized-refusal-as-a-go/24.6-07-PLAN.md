Ruled wave 3 (refusal shell / replay), first plan. "Refusal is itself a decision and deserves the same
five-dimensional replay model. Do not create second-class replay." The builder takes a refusal with no
product, dimension 1 says positively when nothing was read, the shell carries its own replay
reference and the policy bindings, provenance and permitted evidence that made it, and the assembler's
identity and version enter dimension 4 for served and refused decisions alike.

Purpose: D-13, D-14, D-16 (`LC-01` = `D-IMPL-245` row (a)); the `D-IMPL-243` discipline on every new field.
Disclosure (D-17) is plan 08's — this plan records the INTERNAL shell in replay and gives the envelope
the slot the disclosed level lands in.

Output: the widened builder and record; the additive envelope; `DoorOutcome` rule 5 inverted; the
resolver minting on all three refusal branches; the re-derived replay and door tests.
