## Decisions Made

- **The floor lives on the request contract, not in `SkillArgumentProperty` and not in the constraints layer** (option b at the ruling; Dave: *"It is simply the parameter's valid domain"*). `argument_domains` is a seventh declaration key; a domain over an undeclared, magnitude-less or inert parameter, or a default outside its own domain, is refused at declaration.
- **`_has_magnitude` is a function, not a module-level string collection**, because the request-contract test module's constant scan guards against string-collection constants.
- **The maximum stays the technical capability.** Dave's three-part definition splits `minimum` (parameter validity) from `maximum` (backend capability); the domain mechanism carries only the floor for cp-04, and above 100 is still `constraint-refusal` / `bound-exceeded` under `D-IMPL-253` Q21 — recorded in `D-IMPL-259` as the applied reading, for confirmation.
- **The default stays 3.** Dave wrote "remains 6"; the tree has declared 3 since Gate 1 and 6 was the deleted ceiling. Flagged in `D-IMPL-259`, not silently applied either way.
