## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the touched module(s) by name (quick command), plus `make lint` when `src/` or `runtime/` changed.
- **After every plan wave:** the chunk that owns the wave in the foreground — A for waves 1–3 (products, agents, mcp, halflife, controlplane), B for any replay/projection change, D + A for wave 4 (http_api, authz + mcp), C for waves 2 and 6 (hw1, cp-04) with the estate legs for cp-04; `make typecheck`; the three `gate-tools` suites separately.
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** all four chunks, both store legs, `make lint` 0, `make typecheck`, `make product-gates`, `make docs-strict`; the five lock instruments reproduce (criterion 35).
- **Max feedback latency:** ~300 s (chunk D); per-task ≤ 60 s.
