## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the module(s) the task names in `<automated>`; `make lint` when `src/` or `runtime/` changed.
- **After every wave:** chunk A for waves 1–7 (`tests/products tests/agents tests/mcp tests/controlplane`), chunk B (`tests/replay`) from wave 6, chunk D (`tests/http_api tests/authz tests/client tests/contracts tests/deployment`) from wave 8, chunk C (`tests/examples`) for waves 6, 9–11; `make typecheck`; the three `gate-tools` suites separately.
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** all four chunks, `leg-postgres`, `leg-fluree`, `make product-gates`, `make docs-strict`; `RETIREMENT-COUPLINGS.md` regenerated LAST.
- **Max feedback latency:** 120 s per task. **Declared exceptions:** plan 13 Task 2 (the live legs and the seal run) and plan 15 Task 2 (the four chunks, the legs, the make gates) — their `<automated>` is the fast subset; the long runs are the ACTION, foreground, pasted with `rc=`.
- **Sequencing:** the resolver, gates, refusal, record and door modules are touched by plans 03→04→05→07→08→(09‖10) — one tree, in dependency order; plans 06‖07 and 09‖10 and 11‖12 are the only parallel pairs, each with disjoint `files_modified`.
