# Phase 24.6 Plan 10: the MCP door shells every governed determination Summary

Dave, `D-IMPL-253` §1, verbatim: **"Governed determination → governed response.
Technical/runtime failure → transport error."** And on why the standing resource
could not keep answering *not found*: **"not-found would destroy the distinction
and replayability."**

Three things landed at the L6 egress door. The **standing resource** got the
refusal channel it never had. **Both** entitlement paths moved onto the one
governed delivery-boundary step and now only *encode* what it returns. And
`ToolError` was narrowed to what it should always have meant — the fabric's own
failure — carrying an opaque id rather than a message a client would have to
string-parse.

## What was built

**Task 1 — the resource's channel, both paths governed, the builder's refusal**
(commit `71a94132`).

`door_refusal` gained a keyword-only `identity=`, used exactly when `asked` is
`None`. That one keyword is the whole reason the standing resource could not
have a refusal channel before: the function raised *"there is no question for
the refusal envelope to name"*, and a door that manufactured one would have been
reporting a question nobody asked. The door still invents nothing — it states
the two facts its own surface establishes, `parameters={}` and
`temporal_boundary=None`, and never a subject, an intent or a freshness figure.

The tool path emits its denial through **`door_answer`**, the one emission site,
rather than building a second `DoorOutcome`. That was not the plan's shape — the
interfaces block specifies `replay_ref=_replay_ref_of(decided)` — but
`_replay_ref_of` is private to `ccf.products.door`, and reaching for it from a
transport would put a second reconciliation of the envelope-to-outcome reference
translation in the tree, which is the exact thing that function's docstring says
it exists to prevent. Routing through `door_answer` needs only that the
`ProductRequest` be built before the entitlement call, which consults nothing.

**Task 3 — every door that entitles binds the seam** (commit `fbd4f29a`).

Eight sites measured at `1022ad59`, all eight bound, plus both `create_app`
twins under `tests/mcp`. cp-04's **shipped** door binds at *composition*, on
Dave's *"I would prefer the problem to be detected at construction/boot"*:
`compose()` now constructs one `ReplayRefusalRecorder` over the store
`produce.py::_serve` already writes and one `ControlPlaneProductEntitlementSource`
the recorder reads its disclosure posture through — one source, one clock, one
audit plane.

The wire expectations were re-derived, not weakened. Four scenarios asserted
`ERROR_* in detail`; they now assert the shell's kind, the withheld cause, and
the code's **absence** from the body, with the code's presence still asserted on
the entitlement audit event. The two-surface matrix split from two outcome
classes into three — *served*, *shelled*, *401* — because a governed denial is
now an answer on both surfaces and only authentication is still a transport
error.

**Task 2 — the D-27 control** (commit `5da83c3d`).

Seven consumer-observable channels, on both surfaces, at all three levels, with
both subjects produced by the real mechanism rather than hand-built.
