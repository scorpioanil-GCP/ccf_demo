## 1 — P-I: override attempts on a REFUSED request reach the replay record (criterion 10)

**Measured open.** `ConstraintRefusal`, `PolicyResolutionConflict` and `ProductRequestViolation` carried no
override member; `resolve_effective_request` verified the attempts (`verified_overrides`) before any bound was held,
then returned refusals that dropped them, and the record's dimension 2 carried `NO_REQUEST_RESOLUTION` — an examiner
replaying a refused request could not see that an override was asked for, or that it was refused as unentitled.
Dave: *"do not substitute corrected docstrings for missing replay evidence."*

**Fixed (`635e8d3a`).** `overrides: tuple[OverrideRecord, ...]` on all three refusal types (`src/ccf/products/constraints.py`,
`src/ccf/products/request.py`), hash-transparent at the empty tuple; `_with_overrides` in `constraints.py` attaches
the verified attempts at every refusal exit of `resolve_effective_request` — the conflict fold, `required-absent`,
each `_refuse_against` hit, the technical ceiling, and the validation verdict over the effective values. `envelope_for`
renders it as `fields["overrides"]` (`"[]"` when nothing was attempted, so field presence is one shape per kind); it
is withheld at `minimal` and crosses at `reasoned`/`full`; the record's internal envelope keeps it whole. Rosters ticked
(three entries); `policy_overrides`' docstring now states the carriage.

**Controls.** `tests/products/test_policy_constraints.py::test_a_refused_request_keeps_the_override_it_attempted`
(three exits, each carrying exactly the attempts handed in, `entitled=False, honored=False`; the empty tuple renders
without the key) and `tests/products/test_delivery_determinism.py::test_a_refused_request_records_the_override_it_attempted`

it at `minimal`). The four envelope field-set assertions in `test_refusal_envelope.py` now name the member.
