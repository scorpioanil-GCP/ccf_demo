# Phase 24.6 Plan 07: every refusal is a first-class replayable decision Summary

Dave, verbatim (`D-IMPL-253` Q13): **"Refusal is itself a decision and deserves
the same five-dimensional replay model. Do not create second-class replay."**
And its delivery-boundary corollary (`D-IMPL-256` (1)): **"No fulfilled Context
Product does not mean no Decision Replay."**

Before this row, three of the governed resolver's paths returned a refusal
shell and appended nothing — and `build_replay_record` could not have written
one had they tried: it required a `product` and read five dimension-5 values
off it. A question that produced no product produced no audit record at all.

Now it produces both, through **one** seam, and the shell it hands back knows
where its own record landed.

## What was built

**Task 1 — the shell's governance members and the record's refusal outcome**
(commit `10940b71`).

`RefusalEnvelope` went from seven fields to thirteen, additively: the policy
sources that governed the refusal, the address of its record, the permitted
evidence of the search behind it, the Authority layer's resolution, the
disclosure level applied, and — for a **delivery-boundary** denial —
`ResourceRequestIdentity`, the identity `D-IMPL-256` (1) requires. Every one is
hash-transparent at its sentinel, so a 24.5-shaped shell is byte-identical.

Two new contracts. `RefusalEvidence` is the **internal** form and applies no
filter of its own — a filter there would be a second place disclosure is
decided, which is how audit truth gets destroyed to achieve suppression
silence. `ResourceRequestIdentity` names two of the five questions Dave listed
and its docstring names where the other three are answered; it carries **no
subject and no principal**, which is why it is retained on the recorded shell
and on a disclosed one alike.

`build_replay_record` takes `execution` and `product` as optional and a
`refusal` outcome, under **five** guards at the top of the body rather than in
prose: both-or-neither outcomes, a product with no execution, a tenant stated
twice, a tenant stated not at all, and the two spellings of the refusal block
supplied together. Dimension 1 for a refusal that read nothing is
`ContextFabricSnapshot.unread` — a **named constructor**, because *"nothing was
read"* has exactly one spelling and a caller reaching for `build` with an empty
watermark would produce a different and false claim.

**Task 2 — the one minting seam, and the two rules it inverts** (commit
`45f7b26a`).

`RefusalRecorderProtocol` is named once and consumed by four callers that share
no shape: this module's three refusal branches, the governed
delivery-boundary step of plan 08, and cp-04's wrappers.
`ReplayRefusalRecorder` composes exactly two shipped things and invents
nothing — the widened builder, and `record_refusal` over the **same**
`ReplayWriter` the served path uses. That sameness is the substance of *"do not
create second-class replay"*: a second writer, or a second record shape, would
be exactly that.

`DoorOutcome`'s rule 5 was **inverted, not removed**. It said a refused outcome
carries no replay reference, *"nothing was delivered, so no replay append
authorized anything"* — true about delivery, and quietly denying what the
ruling asserts. It now requires agreement between the outcome's `replay_ref`
and its own envelope's. Still five rules; three assertions cover it, because a
validator that refused every refused outcome would satisfy the raise alone.

`Result.replay_ref` left `GUARDED_FIELDS` on the identical reasoning, and this
is the change most worth reading as a **correction**. Its membership rested on
a premise stated verbatim in the constant's own docstring — *"`replay_ref`
exists only after the replay record was written, which is strictly after all
three gates passed"* — whose second clause Q13 makes false. Guarding it would
now be refusing to report an audit trail that exists. The other five are
untouched and no guard was weakened.

determination are byte-identical whatever address the shell happened to hold.
Sabotage 3 is RED against it, and green after restore.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 2 — a shipped structural invariant] `ReplayRefusalRecorder.record`
takes an already-resolved `Perspective`, not the plan's `principal_id` and
`role`.** This is the deviation that matters most to the plans downstream, so
it is first.

The plan's interfaces block fixes the signature as
`record(envelope, *, principal_id: str, role: str | None, …)` and says *"the
three helpers agree on this signature"*. Written that way it is **RED by
construction** against
`tests/invariants/test_mcp_role_separation.py::test_no_principal_id_parameter_exists_below_the_door`,
which AST-walks `ccf/products`, `ccf/projection` and `ccf/replay` for a
`principal_id` **parameter** and admits exactly one enumerated exemption
(`entitlement.py::grant_for`). The invariant's substance — 15.5-RESEARCH.md
Pitfall 3 — is that entitlement is decided at the door and everything below
receives identity **already resolved**.

Measured before deciding: my two methods were named as offenders. The two ways
out were to add an exemption, or to take the thing the invariant names as
correct. Adding an exemption to a shipped structural invariant in order to
admit this row's change is the defect plan 03's deviation 1 refused, so the
seam takes a `Perspective`. A delivery boundary that denies before a role is
resolved passes `NO_ROLE_RESOLVED` explicitly — which is **better**, because it
is a positive claim the door makes rather than a `None` this seam would
interpret on the door's behalf.

**Downstream, concretely:** plans 08, 09, 10, 13 and 16 write
`perspective=Perspective(principal_id=…, role=… or NO_ROLE_RESOLVED)` rather
than the two halves. The failure mode if a plan uses the old spelling is an
immediate `TypeError`, never silent wrong behavior.

**2. `src/ccf/products/__init__.py` was NOT edited, though the plan directs it.**
The module's own docstring states: *"The package re-exports nothing,
deliberately: a second import path for the same symbol is a second surface the
drift manifest has to track."* Plans 02, 04 and 05 each recorded the same
measurement. `RefusalRecorderProtocol` and `ReplayRefusalRecorder` are exported
from `ccf.products.resolver.__all__`, which is where `GovernedResolver`,
`ServedProductResolverProtocol` and every other name on that seam lives, and
which is what `docs/surface/v0.2-surface.json` tracks.

wrong: passing both silently preferred the value and dropped the block, so a
