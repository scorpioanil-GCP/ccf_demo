<objective>
Close `D-59`: give a deployment's own operator a route into the registry the operator plan
actually reads, additively, without letting a caller declare its category.

Purpose: `src/ccf/deployment/operators.py::build_operator_registry` dispatches on artifact TYPE
through an `isinstance` chain and raises on anything else. The registry beneath it is already
open — `ccf.operators.registry.OperatorRegistry.register` takes no category argument and reads
category and parameter schema off the operator — **but the composition path in front of it is
not.** `examples/cp-00-institutional-holders/deployment.py::source_read` worked around it with
a private `OperatorRegistry`, which is the right LOCAL answer and is exactly why cp-00's recipe
witness is `payload_key_present` rather than `operator_in_lineage`.

Output: a defaulted keyword taking already-constructed operators; four gates; one fragment.
</objective>
