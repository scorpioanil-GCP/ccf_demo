## 7 · One finding NOT fixed, and why — for Dave

**A6 — the example's refusal ordering is the inverse of the SDK's.**
`examples/cp-00-institutional-holders/assembler.py::HoldersAssembler.assemble` returns the
governed refusal at line 194, **before** the tenant check at 196 and the role check at 204. The
SDK's `DeterministicAssembler` does the opposite, and this close added the gate that keeps it
that way (B3). So a caller asking a non-conforming question **of the wrong tenant** gets a
contract refusal from the example and an identity refusal from the SDK.

**It is not fixed here, deliberately.** The example learns its tenant from the spine's own
return value (`assembled.tenant_id`), which only exists after assembly, so correcting the order
means restructuring the example's data flow. `deployment.tenant_id()` reads it from config and
would make the check possible earlier — **that is a redesign of the example's seam, at close
time, under a criterion (16) that fences rewriting `cp-00`.** Recorded rather than performed.
