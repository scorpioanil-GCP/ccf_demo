Every wave verified the surface with `tests/test_surface_drift.py`. **It was green throughout.**
Two stricter instruments in `tests/*.py` were red — the very files whose omission

`tools/assemble_surface_changes.py::_collect_fields` builds **one field-dict per `##` section**,
and a second `- Surface:` bullet **overwrites the first**. All three of this phase's fragments

passed. **Only `test_every_diff_line_has_a_matching_public_log_entry` looked.** Fixed by giving
each entry its own heading: **9 of 9 parse.**

**(2) Five committed shapes were disturbed without registration.**
`test_no_committed_plain_class_or_function_shape_is_disturbed` is stricter than the drift gate:
a committed `class`/`function` shape may not move at all unless it is in
`_SHAPE_MOVED_BY_FRAGMENT`, where each entry *"must earn it: the fragment it names must exist
and must claim the name."* All five are additive, keyword-only, defaulted widenings — precisely
the case that allowance was written for, on the `build_egress_server` precedent — and each is
now registered with its reason and its fragment.
