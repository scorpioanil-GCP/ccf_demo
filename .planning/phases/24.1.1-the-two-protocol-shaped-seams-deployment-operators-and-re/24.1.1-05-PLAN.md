<objective>
Update every `.assemble(` call site to branch on the widened return type, and let cp-00's client
show the seam doing its job — without touching cp-00's spine.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-213` classified `D-60` **NOT additive** per `D-10` precisely because widening a
Protocol's return type makes every existing caller branch. Criterion 8 is that consequence,
made checkable. What it is NOT is a licence to touch the example's spine — criterion 10 fences
that, and criterion 16 fences the rest of `examples/`.

The minimal honest change to the client is a **DELETION**: today
`examples/cp-00-institutional-holders/run.py::main` catches `AssemblyRefused` and then reaches
around the exception into a `_Recorder` observer to find the typed `ProductRequestViolation` the
spine produced. Roughly twelve lines of except-plus-observer archaeology collapse to a
three-line `isinstance` branch, and the re-raise goes with them.

Output: every call site branching, a smaller client, an assembler that returns, and a spine
proven untouched.
</objective>
