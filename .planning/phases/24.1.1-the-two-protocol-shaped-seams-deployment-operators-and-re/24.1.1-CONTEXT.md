## 3 · `D-59` — the deployment-operator registration seam

**Subject:** `src/ccf/deployment/operators.py::build_operator_registry`.

**Current behaviour, read at `3a37999`.** The builder dispatches on artifact TYPE through
an `isinstance` chain over four canonical categories, and anything else raises:

    raise ValueError(
        f"manifest key {key!r} carries a"
        f" {type(parameter).__name__}, which names no canonical operator"
        " category; the builder does not guess which operator a"
        " deployment meant"
    )

The registry itself is **open** — `register(operator)` takes no category argument and reads
category and parameter schema off the operator. **The composition path in front of it is
not open.** A deployment's own operator therefore has no route into the operator plan's
registry; `cp-00` worked around it by registering into its own private `OperatorRegistry`
at the source read, which is the right LOCAL answer and is why step 1's recipe witness is
`payload_key_present` rather than `operator_in_lineage`.

**Constraints the seam must not break, and they are already in the module's docstring:**
registration must NOT re-introduce a caller-declared category — that would let a planner
order an operator into a position it does not belong in, the Policy-after-Similarity
failure `INV-CH7-26` forbids; `DuplicateOperatorKey` stays fatal and uncaught; and the
module holds **no operator key literal and no entity class literal** (`CINV-01`).

## 4 · `D-60` — assembler refusal as data

**Subject:** `src/ccf/agents/assembler.py::ContextAssemblerProtocol.assemble`.

It returns `AssemblyOutcome` and nothing else, so a conforming assembler that receives a
governed refusal can only **raise** — in a codebase whose stated posture is that a refusal
is **DATA**.

**The vocabulary to follow already exists; this phase converts, it does not invent:**

| Today | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| `src/ccf/agents/errors.py::AssemblyRefused` | a `ValueError` — the raise path |
| `src/ccf/products/request.py::ProductRequestViolation` | a `CCFContract` — the data path, and the posture to match |
| `src/ccf/agents/assembler.py::AssemblyOutcome` | a `CCFContract`, frozen and `extra=forbid` by inheritance |

Criterion 5 binds the new refusal to the `ProductRequestViolation` posture rather than a
second vocabulary.

## 6 · Two hazards named before planning, so they are not negotiated mid-phase

**Criterion 9 carries the real design risk.** If a refusal returned as data does not reach
the Decision Replay record *by construction*, the fix may want modules beyond the two named
in criterion 12 — and that is the fence moving. Dave's amendment is the control: **any such
module is ENUMERATED IN A `D-IMPL` ENTRY BEFORE ANY EDIT, and silent widening is RED.**
Criterion 12 then measures against the two named modules **plus** whatever that entry
names, per-member, with each member proven to name tracked files (the `VAC-01` guard,
because a misspelled pathspec diffs empty exactly like a clean one).

**Criterion 16 is the one most likely to drift under pressure.** The natural way to
pressure-test a *registration seam* is a second example that registers an operator, and
"small" will feel cramped. Dave drew that line at the gate: **new tests plus AT MOST ONE
`tests/` fixture; no second shipped example tree; no bulk retrofit (`D-07`); and `cp-00` is
NOT rewritten to register a deployment operator.** `cp-00`'s private-registry workaround
stays as it is — criterion 10 requires its spine untouched.
