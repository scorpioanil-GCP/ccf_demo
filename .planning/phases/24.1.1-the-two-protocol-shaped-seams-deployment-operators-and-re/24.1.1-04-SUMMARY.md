# Phase 24.1.1 Plan 04: A Refusal Has an Identity in the Record

## What shipped, and what it deliberately is not

Criterion 9 is discharged on **Reading B**: a named sub-structure on dimension 5, on the
`hitl_actions` pattern this module already carried four times. One sentinel, one field, one
builder keyword, one public minting function, one write-time refusal.

**Dimension 5 rather than dimension 2**, and the reason is in the docstring: dimension 5 is
*how the delivered content came to be* and a refusal is that path's terminus; a refusal is not
a policy binding.

## The control, in three legs, because they close different holes

| Leg | What it closes |
| --- | --- |
| **1 — derived, never claimed** | `mint_assembly_refusal` produces the block FROM the assembler's return value; the builder records it verbatim |
| **2 — a false claim is refused at write time** | handing the minter a refusal while asserting `NO_ASSEMBLY_REFUSAL` raises, on `FalseGrantClaimError`'s exact reasoning. The symmetric case — asserting a refusal over an outcome — is refused too |
| **3 — a fabricated block is refused** | the builder admits only the sentinel or a value that round-trips as a `ProductRequestViolation` |

honest move is to check the file before believing the suite.

## The hole this does NOT close — and criterion 9 is NOT reported as absolutely discharged

**A caller who never calls the minter, and hand-passes the sentinel while holding a refusal,
still produces a well-formed FALSE record.** Leg 3 stops a fabricated block; it does not stop a
truthful-looking silence, and Leg 2 reaches only callers that ask for the check.

Closing it needs the refusal **value** to travel into the builder rather than a pre-serialized
block — Reading A's shape, refused for this phase because it pulls `serve.py` and `resolver.py`
in. **Criterion 9 is discharged AS RULED and not absolutely.** Named in three places so it
cannot be lost: the builder's docstring, the surface fragment, and here.

Also unchanged and deliberate: **a refusal still terminates without its own replay record and
without a delivery.** This plan gives a refusal an identity *within a record that was written*;
it does not stand up a refusal serve path.
