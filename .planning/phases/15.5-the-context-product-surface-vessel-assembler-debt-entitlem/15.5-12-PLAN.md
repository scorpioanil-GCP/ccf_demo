<objective>
Implement `D-IMPL-31`'s mixing-refusal rule, ruled on 2026-07-25 and never
built, plus the two composition rules that go with it.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-97` rules that products may aggregate other products. Three
things must then be true, and one of them is already a standing ruling.
`D-IMPL-31` resolved that `expires_at = min(class MAX_TTL ceiling, derivation
over constituent signal validities)` and that *"product classes may declare
constituent-validity floors whose breach refuses assembly rather than emitting a
product fresher-labeled than its stalest load-bearing constituent."* The
whitepaper commits the same at `docs/CCF_Whitepaper_1-2.md:1483`: *"Temporal
Validation refuses to assemble a product that silently mixes the two"* — 90-day
financials with 15-minute blotter entries.

**This criterion is implementation of a standing ruling, not a new design
decision.** Do not re-open it.

**Q6 is this plan's ruling, and it is the reason this plan depends on
`15.5-05`.** Q6 asks how debt composes across an aggregate's constituents — and
whether it composes at all in this phase. Criterion 8 as written does not
mention debt composition, so option (d), *out of scope for 15.5*, may already be
the intended reading; but "may already be" is a guess, and no other plan in the
phase receives Q6's answer. Task 1 opens by reading the ruling and fixing this
plan's scope to it, the way plans `15.5-08` and `15.5-09` open against Q1 and
Q5.

Output: N-way lineage union, the TTL floor, the constituent-validity refusal,
the bind-time refusal of an unpromisable freshness SLA, and whatever debt
composition Q6 ruled — each with property laws chosen to be jointly falsifying.
