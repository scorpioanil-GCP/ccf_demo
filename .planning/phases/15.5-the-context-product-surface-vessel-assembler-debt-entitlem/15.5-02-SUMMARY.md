## Deviations from Plan

Three, all additive strengthenings within the plan's own stated behavior. No
Rule 4 architectural questions arose.

**1. [Rule 2 — missing critical functionality] `Element.absence_markers`**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The plan's pending branch asserts `element.field_name not in union`. A pending `field_name` is a *prediction*; land `freshness_sla` as `freshness_sla_seconds` and the prediction-keyed assertion walks past it, leaving must-have truth 3 ("an element that lands with its marker still on fails rather than passing") unmet under a rename.
- **Fix:** Each pending element declares substring markers (`freshness`, `discovery`, `address`) asserted absent from every field name in the union, after the exact-name assertion and still before the skip.
- **Files modified:** `tests/products/test_contract_elements.py`
- **Commit:** dbe9ec6

**2. [Rule 1 — bug in prior art] docstring audit resolved via `field_name`, not snake-cased element name**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The rescued draft derived the expected field from `element.name.lower().replace(" ", "_")`. That is correct for the four elements the docstring currently names but wrong the moment the docstring is rewritten: a claim that Policy Binding lives on `RegisteredProductClass` would resolve to `policy_binding`, which is not a field, producing a false failure against a true claim.
- **Fix:** Resolve through `element.field_name`, which the enumeration already declares and the injectivity guard already constrains.
- **Files modified:** `tests/products/test_contract_elements.py`
- **Commit:** dbe9ec6

**3. [Rule 3 — blocking] `.venv` symlink in the worktree**
- **Found during:** Task 1 verification
- **Issue:** The plan's verify commands are `.venv/bin/python …`, and the venv lives in the main checkout, not the worktree.
- **Fix:** `ln -sfn /home/parkave/ccf_code/.venv .venv` inside the worktree. `.venv` is `.gitignore`d (line 15), so nothing was committed. Import resolution verified to prefer the worktree's `src/` via `PYTHONPATH`.

## Surfaced, Not Papered Over

**The union mechanism is coarse at Policy Binding.** `contract.py`'s own
field-to-element table routes Policy Binding through
`lineage.policy_bundle_versions` — a *nested* field, which a top-level
`model_fields` union cannot reach. The enumeration therefore keys Policy
Binding to `accepted_policy_binding_modes`, the class-level top-level field
carrying the element's contract surface; keying it to `lineage` instead would
collapse two elements onto one field, which the injectivity guard rejects.

The residual is stated in the module docstring rather than hidden: the union
proves Policy Binding has a bearing field, it does **not** prove the nested
policy-bundle route still exists. Closing that would need `field_name` to
accept a dotted path, which diverges from this plan's stated `model_fields`
union mechanism and from its `key_links` contract. Recorded here as an
observation for 15.5-07 or a later plan, not silently absorbed.
