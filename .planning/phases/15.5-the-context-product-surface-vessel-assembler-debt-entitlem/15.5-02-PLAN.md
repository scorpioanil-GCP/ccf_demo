<objective>
Make the §6.3 contract an executable assertion instead of prose.

Purpose: three of nine committed elements went missing while every gate stayed
green, because every gate in this repo tests what the fabric *does* and none
tests what the delivered product *carries*. `15.5-CONTEXT.md` locks this as the
anti-recurrence requirement. The test is written **first**, before the elements
land, so that the fix is forced to satisfy an assertion rather than to satisfy a
review.

Output: `tests/products/test_contract_elements.py` — the nine-element
enumeration, green today with three declared deferrals, and structurally unable
to stay green once an element lands without the declaration being edited.

**The ratchet must fail in both directions**, and that is the one property this
plan is most likely to get wrong. A pending element that only *skips* leaves a
hole: land `freshness_sla` on the contract, leave its pending marker on, and the
suite stays green while criterion 2 is silently unmet. The pending branch
therefore asserts the field is **absent** from the union *before* it skips.
</objective>

Declare `Element` as a `NamedTuple` with `name`, `whitepaper_anchor`,

Note when writing the present six that `assembly_mode` is borne by
`RegisteredProductClass`, not by the instance — which is the whole reason the
enumeration runs over the union rather than over `ContextProductContract`
alone.

Enumerate against the **union** of `ContextProductContract.model_fields` and
`RegisteredProductClass.model_fields`, exactly as
`tests/peering/test_trust_policy.py:213` unions two model field sets. The union
is deliberate and the docstring must say why: `Q8` has not ruled whether
`freshness_sla` lands on the instance or the class, and the enumeration must be
correct under either ruling. It asserts that the element is **borne**, not
where.

Four tests:

1. `test_the_contract_has_nine_elements` — `len(NINE_ELEMENTS) == 9`, citing
   `D-IMPL-30` in the docstring and naming that the "eight" references,
   CLAUDE.md §5's included, are editorial drift the ruling authorizes treating
   as such.
2. `test_every_element_is_borne_by_a_named_field`, parametrized over

   assertion comes first and the skip is unreachable when the field has landed.
   The skip reason names the element, the plan that closes it, and the

   not removed.

   second direction. Assert the pending names equal the declared three. Between

4. `test_the_module_docstring_names_only_fields_that_exist` — read
   `src/ccf/products/contract.py`'s module docstring via

asserted the contract's own shape, and a docstring cannot fail when someone
