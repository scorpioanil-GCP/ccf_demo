<objective>
Widen the product resolver seam exactly once, so that both the authorized
`Perspective` and the typed request reach the assembler.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-108` (Dave, 2026-08-11). Two gaps turned out to be one.
`DI-15.5-03` recorded that all three exposed doors resolve and enforce an
entitled `Perspective` but do not forward it — so a door proves the *refusal*
leg while leaving unproven that the product it served was shaped under the
perspective it authorized. Separately, plan `15.5-08` declares the must-have
truth *"The request surface is validated where it crosses the door, not
downstream"* while naming no door file in `files_modified`. Both are blocked on
the same fact: `ProductResolverProtocol` and `ExperienceProductResolverProtocol`
are three-string seams (`tenant, product_class, version`) through which neither
value can pass.

Reshaping the seam twice would mean editing the same 24 files twice — the exact
cost `D-IMPL-98` declined for the request surface. This plan applies that
argument consistently and does it once.

Output: both Protocols widened, all three doors passing what they resolved, and
`DI-15.5-03` closed.
</objective>

<task number="1">
<name>Widen both Protocols, and make the widening refuse rather than default</name>
<action>
Widen `ProductResolverProtocol` (`runtime/ccf_runtime/mcp/egress.py:136`) and
`ExperienceProductResolverProtocol` (`runtime/ccf_runtime/mcp/experience.py:215`)
to carry the authorized `Perspective` and the typed request alongside the three
strings they already take.

The request type is whatever plan `15.5-08` shipped in `src/ccf/products/request.py`
— read that module and its SUMMARY before designing the signature. Do not invent
a second request shape.

**A default is the wrong tool here.** The whole point of `DI-15.5-03` is that a
resolver silently constructing its own `Perspective` is indistinguishable from a
resolver honouring the door's. A parameter that defaults to `None` recreates
exactly that ambiguity one layer down. Make the seam require the value.
</action>
