<objective>
Make the declared payload schema the discipline it is supposed to be: a payload
that violates it does not become a `ServableProduct`.

Purpose: under the vessel model the declared schema **is** the typing
discipline — there is no per-product subclass to carry it. `15.5-07` gave a
product class somewhere to declare its payload shape; without enforcement that
declaration is documentation. This plan enforces it at whichever layer Dave
ruled on Q5, using the additive-gate posture `GATE_SOURCE_ATTESTATION` already
proves, so the canonical three gates stay byte-untouched.

Output: enforcement, its refusal shape, and a measured serve-path cost.
</objective>
