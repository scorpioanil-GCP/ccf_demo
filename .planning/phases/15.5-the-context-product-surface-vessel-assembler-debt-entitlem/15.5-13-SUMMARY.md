# Phase 15.5 Plan 13: The Reference Example Assembles Summary

**`examples/s1-01-hello-context-product` now answers about any company at any
time: the subject and both instants are fields on a typed `ProductRequest`
validated at the door, the record pins graph questions that resolve to their own
bytes, and the delivered product carries a measured Context Debt that crosses a
CAR band six weeks later against the same graph.**

## What changed, and why it is the point of the phase

Dave's 2026-08-10 review asked what CCF buys over an MCP server in front of a
database. The example's answer was weak because the example was weak: it pinned
`subject_entity_ref`, `temporal_boundary` and `now` as literals — one company,
one frozen instant, one answer. It was a *configured* product, and a configured
product is an aggregate with extra ceremony.

Three questions now run on `make example-s1-01`, from one graph and one ingest:

| # | Question | Beneficial owners | D_c |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Acme, as of 1 Aug 2026 | Jane Doe 30.0%, John Smith 45.0% | 0.035 — `ok` |
| 2 | Beta Holdings, as of 1 Aug 2026 | Jane Doe 60.0% | 0.035 — `ok` |
| 3 | Acme, as of 15 Sep 2026 | Jane Doe 30.0%, John Smith 45.0%, **Marcus Vale 30.0%** | 0.221 — `forced_llm` |

1 vs 2 is a different company. 1 vs 3 is a different instant, and the share
transfer that was already in the graph on 1 August but not legally effective
until September makes a new beneficial owner appear — with nothing re-ingested.
`--subject` asks a fourth question that is in no artifact at all.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — the subject and both instants become arguments (`e4db82c`)

`ProductRequest` carries subject, intent, freshness and `asked_at`;
`decision_instant` is the class-declared parameter in `parameters`, and
`expires_at` derives from it exactly as it derived from the deleted
`decision.yaml`'s `now`. `spine.assemble_and_serve` gained one argument
(`request=`) and `read` gained one parameter; the 15.5-04 shape is otherwise
intact and `tests/examples/test_spine_separation.py` stays green.

`refuse_request` runs immediately after `read`, **before the graph is touched**
— address first, then the closed parameter world. `request-contract.yaml`
declares its own `schema_name`/`schema_version` rather than taking them from the
incoming request, because a contract that took its identity from what it was
validating would agree with everything by construction.

`ubo-question.yaml` lost exactly one key. The class and attribute vocabulary is
genuinely Control Plane governance and is byte-unchanged.

### Task 2 — pin the question, deliver the debt (`7e43b14`)

`serve()` now receives a `QueryPinWriter` and `execution.executed_queries`. This
is the first time the 15.2-08 path has run anywhere in the tree: the record was
already pinning two graph queries per run and nothing could resolve either.

Debt is **measured**, not declared. F is `FreshnessScorer` over every entity
claim the operators left standing, typed by resolved entity class and dated by
its own `observed_at`; C is the fraction of the declared required classes the
view holds; S is declared, and only F is pinned — `NO_SCORER_PINS` exists so
that an unpinned factor and a dropped pin are different artifacts.

Six new assertions, each classified against the module's own distinction:

| Assertion | Class |
| --- | --- |
| a different company gets a different answer | independent expectation |
| the same company at a later instant gets a different answer | independent expectation |
| every pinned query resolves to the bytes it names | SDK mechanism claim |
| a later instant pins a different question to the graph | independent expectation |
| the delivered product carries its debt | propagation check |
| the debt figure is measured and not constant | independent expectation |

### D-IMPL-111, limb (a) — the request's subject seeds the traversal (`20fff85`)

`ProjectionEngine.execute` gained `seeds: frozenset[str] | None = None`, threaded
to **every pushable segment that carries a traverse family and no other**.
`GQ-03` refuses seeds with no walk to start, so a non-walking segment is passed
`None`; where a survivor set is also in force the two compose under `GQ-17`.

**The rule is "every walking segment", not "the opening segment", and that was
found by measurement rather than by reading.** The first implementation seeded
only the opening segment on the argument `compile_segment_query` already makes
for why the engine promotes no survivor to a walk start. It changed **nothing** —
s1-01's plan segments as `[push: policy] [in-proc: similarity] [push: temporal,
traversal]`, so the traverse family is *post*-in-process. The refusal that
argument states is about promoting *survivors* — all of them — to walk starts,
which would begin the walk everywhere the previous segment left something
standing. The caller's anchor is one named entity, stated in the question.

`request_seeds` is the **one** derivation, in `ccf.products.request`, used by the
serving path and by `replay()`. Two derivations could drift, and a drift here
would replay a different question under the original decision's identifier.

Measured effect on the example: **the answers, the debt and the product content
addresses are byte-unchanged**; only the walking segment's pin moved, per
subject. Seeds widen the frontier (`GQ-11` unions them in) rather than narrowing
it, and the anchors were already reachable, so no view changed.

### D-IMPL-111, limb (b) — the question lands in the record (`956abed`)

`ContentGenerationPath.request: ProductRequest | None = None`, and
`build_replay_record(..., request=...)`. **0 forced repairs** across the 32 call
sites in 28 files, under the positive sentinel — the pattern this phase has now
used six times.

**A parameter, not a derivation, and that is the difference from debt.** Debt is
a property of the *product*, which was already a parameter, so D-IMPL-100 read it
off and made a record pinning a debt its product does not carry unrepresentable.
The question is a property of the **call**: the engine is handed a plan, a
perspective, a boundary and a seed set and has never seen it, and L5 importing
L6's request surface to carry one would invert the layering.

**Dimension 5, not dimension 4.** Dimension 4 answers *"for whom, with what
authority?"* (`docs/CCF_Whitepaper_1-2.md:3266`) and a request asserts none of
that — it has no role, principal or tenant field by construction. What it *does*
determine is the generation path, so it sits beside the `graph_query_sequence`
it explains.

**The two limbs are one mechanism.** Limb (a) makes the pins depend on the
question, which makes them unreproducible unless replay can re-derive the seeds;
limb (b) is what lets it. A record carrying limb (a)'s pins without limb (b)'s
question would diverge against itself on its first replay.
