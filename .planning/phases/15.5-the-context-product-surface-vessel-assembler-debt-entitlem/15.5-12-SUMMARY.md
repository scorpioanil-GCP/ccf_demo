# Phase 15.5 Plan 12: D-IMPL-31 Implemented, Fifteen Months After It Was Ruled

`D-IMPL-31` was ruled on 2026-07-25 and never built. An aggregate product's
lineage, its expiry, and whether it was legal to assemble at all were whatever
an adopter's assembly code happened to do. All three now have one module, and a
class that promises a freshness its constituents cannot offer is refused while
the fabric is being wired rather than discovered by the agent that consumed the
product.

## Q6's ruling, read before anything was written

**`D-IMPL-104` (Dave, 2026-08-11) — RESOLVED as to meaning, DEFERRED as to
implementation.** Read first, per the plan's own task-1 instruction, and this
plan's scope was fixed to it rather than inferred from criterion 8's silence.

- **The rule Dave selected:** an aggregate product's debt is the **per-factor
  maximum across its constituents** — worst factor wins, freshness against
  freshness, completeness against completeness, semantic consistency against
  semantic consistency. Recorded as *consistent with* `D-IMPL-31` rather than
  as a new axiom, because that ruling already composes validity by taking the
  most constraining constituent.
- **The branch this plan took: the deferral branch.** `D-IMPL-104` names this
  plan explicitly — plan `15.5-12` ships criterion 8's three named limbs,
  lineage union, the TTL floor and the SLA refusal, **without the debt limb**,
  and cites the entry for the meaning rather than inventing a rule at the
  moment it first needs one.
- **What that meant for tasks 1 and 3:** no `compose_debt` in
  `src/ccf/products/composition.py`, and **no debt-composition law** in
  `tests/properties/test_aggregate_composition_laws.py`. Under the plan's own
  out-of-scope branch, *"that sentence is the deliverable of this branch; a
  silent absence is not"* — so the omission is stated in three places with its
  citation: the module docstring, the property-suite docstring (which also
  records the law that belongs there when the surface arrives), and the package
  docstring. `test_no_debt_composition_ships_and_the_module_says_why` asserts
  both the absence of the symbol and the presence of the citation, so the
  statement cannot rot away silently.
- **No tension to record with `context_debt`.** Because nothing was built,
  nothing could reintroduce the weighted-additive form `debt.py:16-22` and
  `debt.py:289-300` foreclose. `D-IMPL-104` prices the future combinator at
  zero new weights for the same reason.

**`D-IMPL-105` (Q7)** was also read: debt is not per-principal, so nothing in
this plan acquired an entitlement dimension. Composition here is pure over
already-gated constituents and reads no principal, credential, or tenant value
(threat `T-15.5-58`, accepted in the plan's register, holds as written).

## What shipped

### Limb 1 — the N-way lineage union

`compose_lineage(lineages)` unions `operators_invoked` and
`policy_bundle_versions` across N constituents, so every constituent's source
signal stays resolvable from the composite (CLAUDE.md §7.2, D-14).

**It folds `ccf.peering.admission.union_lineage` rather than re-implementing
it.** A second union in the tree would be a second reading of what lineage
continuity means, and the shipped one is already property-tested. A source
assertion —
`test_compose_lineage_reuses_union_lineage_rather_than_re_unioning` — holds it
there by requiring the call and refusing any `operators_invoked |` or
`policy_bundle_versions |` expression in the module. That test caught the
deliberate weakening in the task-3 falsification run, which is a small piece of
evidence that it is live rather than decorative.

On exactly two constituents the result **agrees with `union_lineage` on the two
set axes**, so the pairwise case has one behaviour and not two.

### The N-way anchor — named as a choice, because it is one

`union_lineage`'s asymmetry is load-bearing: `graph_snapshot_at` is the
**producer's**, because a producer and a consumer are ordered. Its own
docstring records that order-independence is therefore *"a law about the two
set axes"* only. **An N-way composite of siblings has no producer**, so whose
anchor to keep is genuinely undefined by that function, and nothing in the
algebra decides it.

**This module takes the earliest constituent's anchor. That is a design choice,
not a derivation** — the most conservative anchor, by analogy with the TTL
floor, on the reasoning that a composite must not claim a snapshot fresher than
any constituent actually saw. It is stated as a choice in the module docstring
with that reason, it has its own property law (deliberately separate from the
order-independence law, which the suite does **not** extend to a single-valued
instant), and it is recorded here so no reviewer is left inferring it fell out
of the set algebra.

### Limb 2 — the TTL floor

`compose_expiry` implements `D-IMPL-31`'s formula, `min(class MAX_TTL ceiling,
derivation over constituent signal validities)`, with the ceiling computed from
the class's registered `MaxTTL` artifact and the caller's resolved anchor. No
TTL number, no freshness figure and no threshold appears in the module; the
acceptance grep for hard-coded durations returns nothing.

**A third term was added, and it is a decision worth reading.** The stalest
constituent's own validity is part of the minimum. `D-IMPL-31` makes the
derivation per-class configuration (`min` as the reference default *shape*)
under the D-IMPL-38 parameter discipline — and a class configuring something
less conservative than `min` could otherwise produce exactly the
fresher-labelled composite that same ruling exists to forbid. Under the
reference default the third term is an identity and changes nothing. This is
**not** a silent repair of a configured value: it is the stated definition of
the composition, carried in the signature's documented contract and in the
docstring, so *"a composite is no fresher than its stalest constituent"* is a
property of the function rather than a property of a configuration. The
property law is stated over an arbitrary drawn derivation for the same reason.

### Limb 3a — the mixing refusal, at assembly

`refuse_mixing` returns a `MixingRefusal` naming the breaching constituent, its
validity, and the declared floor. `ConstituentValidityFloors` was declared by
`15.5-07` and **nothing in the tree read a floor** until now; this is the code
that reads it, and it runs at assembly rather than at registration because the
mix does not exist until assembly.

Three choices inside it:

- **Load-bearing is expressed by the declared floor, not by a flag.** A
  constituent whose signal type has no declared floor is not checked, because
  `NO_VALIDITY_FLOORS` is already the positive assertion *no constituent of
  this class is load-bearing enough to refuse assembly on its staleness alone*.
  A second way to say the same thing could disagree with the first.
- **Returned, not raised**, and **not a `GateRefusal`.** The module imports
  nothing from `ccf.products.gates`. The three validation gates remain three;
  `PayloadSchemaViolation` is the precedent and
  `test_a_mixing_refusal_is_not_a_gate_verdict` is the guard.
- **Remaining validity is carried as its two instants**, not as a seconds
  figure. `expires_at - evaluated_at` is exact in `timedelta` arithmetic; a
  float duration on a value that can reach the replay record is not bit-stable,
  which is `debt.py`'s own stated reason for `Decimal`, applied to a duration.

### Limb 3b — the bind-time freshness-SLA refusal

`ccf.deployment.binder.refuse_unpromisable_freshness_sla` raises when a
composite class declares a `max_age` for a signal that one of its constituent
classes cannot offer on the same signal. The message names all three facts —
the promise, the breaching constituent, and its offer — and every non-raising
path returns `None`. Modelled clause for clause on
`refuse_operator_set_divergence`: **refused, never repaired**, because only the
deployment's author knows which of the two authored documents they meant.

Both halves of the mandatory refusal pair are tested (a binder that always
refused fails one, a binder that always admitted fails the other), and both
reach the function through **authored artifact documents parsed by
`load_artifact`**, on `tests/deployment/test_binder.py`'s discipline: the
refusal is reachable by editing one number in one document, not only by writing
a bug.

**Silence is not a weak promise.** A constituent that declares nothing for a
signal is not compared on it, because `UNMEASURED_FRESHNESS_SLA` is the
positive assertion *this class's freshness is bounded by its TTL alone and it
measures no signal separately*. Reading silence as an unbounded offer would
refuse every class registered before `D-IMPL-106` existed — a claim about their
authors' intent that nobody made. The residual is named in the docstring rather
than closed: a constituent that measures nothing cannot be *held* to anything
here either, and only a declaration can fix that.

**ERGO-02 unbroken.** `ccf.deployment` still imports nothing from
`ccf.products.gates`; the comparison reads `ProductClassDeclaration` only, so
the no-allowlist `cinv02-deployment-gates` rule needed no widening and none was
attempted. `tests/lints` passes and `python -m tools.lints --all` exits 0.

### The property laws

Five laws in `tests/properties/test_aggregate_composition_laws.py`, generated
by hypothesis strategies rather than drawn from the banking fixtures, with the
module docstring naming per law the weaker implementation it falsifies:
survivors, order independence **on the set axes only**, strict growth, floor
monotonicity, and refusal completeness — plus refusal completeness's converse
half, because a function that always refused would satisfy the completeness law
alone. The single-valued anchor gets its own law rather than being folded into
order independence, so the suite does not pretend an instant commutes.

**Falsification observed, then reverted** (acceptance criterion):

| Substitution | Laws failed |
| --- | --- |
| `compose_expiry` returns the ceiling (constant-shaped) | floor monotonicity, plus 2 concrete cases |
| `compose_lineage` folds to `lineages[-1]` (replaces instead of unioning) | survivors, order independence, strict growth, plus 4 concrete cases |

Each was applied to `src/ccf/products/composition.py`, observed, and reverted
with `git checkout -- src/ccf/products/composition.py`. The tree was confirmed
clean and green after each revert.

## Deviations from Plan

### [Rule 3 — blocking] The plan's premise that `15.5-07` added a per-class expiry derivation is false

- **Found during:** Task 1, reading `src/ccf/products/contract.py` before
  writing.
- **Issue:** The plan says *"with the derivation function per class read from
  the declaration `15.5-07` added"*. It was not added. Neither
  `RegisteredProductClass` nor `ProductClassDeclaration` carries a derivation
  field; `15.5-07` added `freshness_sla`, `discovery`, `addressability`,
  `constituent_validity_floors` and the payload-schema pin, and no derivation.
- **Fix:** `compose_expiry` takes the derivation as a keyword parameter typed
  `ExpiryDerivation`, defaulting to `min` — which `D-IMPL-31` names as *"the
  reference default shape"*, so the default is licensed by the ruling and is a
  shape rather than a value. The absence is recorded on the `ExpiryDerivation`
  alias itself, naming where the field belongs when the deferred
  aggregate-product surface arrives.
- **Why not add the field:** `src/ccf/products/contract.py` belongs to
  `15.5-11` this wave, and adding a governance field is a declaration change
  rather than a computation. This is reported rather than made, per the wave
  hazard instruction. **No change to `contract.py` is being requested** — the
  parameter is the correct home until an aggregate product exists to declare
  against.
- **Files modified:** `src/ccf/products/composition.py` (the alias and its
  note). **Commit:** `1d11d66`.

### [Rule 2 — missing critical functionality] The stalest-constituent term in `compose_expiry`

Described in full above. Recorded as a deviation because `D-IMPL-31`'s formula
as literally quoted has two terms and this function has three. The third makes
the ruling's own stated purpose structural instead of configuration-dependent,
and it is an identity under the ruled reference default. **Commit:** `1d11d66`.

### [Rule 2 — missing critical functionality] The empty-composition refusal covers `refuse_mixing` too

The plan's behaviour list names the empty case only for composition. It was
extended to `refuse_mixing`: a mix of zero constituents returning `None` would
report an empty composite as legally mixed, which is a check that cannot refuse.
**Commit:** `1d11d66`.
