<objective>
Deliver Context Debt to the consuming agent, without making a single replay
diverge.

Purpose: `DebtAssessment` is computed by `ccf.halflife.debt`, exercised in five
shipped examples, and appears on **neither** the product **nor** the replay
record. It is calculated and discarded. Five of the six axes of
`G(E, R, T, P, Π, L)` reach the wire; the sixth — the one that says how much to
trust the other five — does not, so an agent asking about Acme cannot tell
whether the ownership data was refreshed this morning or eighteen months ago.
The whitepaper is unambiguous at `docs/CCF_Whitepaper_1-2.md:2696`: debt *"is a
runtime signal the agent must consume as a first-class decision input, not a
log-line to be read after the fact."*

This is the phase's most constrained plan. `ReplayEnvironment.rehydrate_product`
rebuilds the product from the record plus the re-executed projection and the
engine raises `ReplayDivergence` on a content-address mismatch, so **any field
added to `ContextProductContract` must be reconstructible from the record, the
product class, or the projection output — and debt is none of the three.**
`tests/replay/test_engine_rehydration.py` is the tripwire, it already exists,
and plan `15.5-01` measured every candidate placement against it.

Output: debt reaching the consumer at the placement Dave ruled on Q3, in the
shape he ruled on Q2 and Q4, with replay byte-exact.
</objective>
