Eight design decisions gate the rest of Phase 15.5. Three of them gate the
phase's shape; five gate one track each.

**Q1 — Is the request surface a typed request contract per product class?
(gating)** Dave's shape: *"maybe it's just the company name and 'top investors
above 25%'."* Whitepaper §6.3 *Addressability* commits it directly at
`docs/CCF_Whitepaper_1-2.md:1465`: *"every product a well-defined interface the
agent queries with intent, role, and freshness parameters"*, worked as
`/context-products/credit-risk/counterparty/{id}?intent=…&freshness=…`. This is
the one that makes a product **callable** rather than configurable, and it
collapses the fixed-subject and fixed-instant defects into one design instead
of two patches. Options (a) typed request contract per class as a Control Plane
artifact; (b) no contract, subject and instant become explicit parameters of
the existing serve path; (c) defer the contract, add the parameters now.

**Q3 — Where does debt land? (structurally constrained)** Plan `15.5-01`
measured the four placements against `ReplayEnvironment.rehydrate_product`'s
content-address check. **Product-only is not viable** — it makes every replay
of every product raise `ReplayDivergence`. Record-only is deterministic but
fails criterion 1 and whitepaper `:2696` (*"a runtime signal the agent must
consume"*). Both is viable at the cost of changing a public Protocol. A fourth
option, derived from the tree during research and **absent from
`CONTEXT_PRODUCT_REVISION.md` §9**: debt travels on `ServedProduct` beside the
product, reaching the consumer without touching a content address.

**Price Q3 against three doors, not one.** Every delivery path in the tree
serializes `servable.product` and none touches `ServedProduct.replay_ref`:
`runtime/ccf_runtime/mcp/egress.py:160-163` (fabric-internal MCP egress),
`runtime/ccf_runtime/http_api/routes/product.py:35-42` (the HTTP route), and
`runtime/ccf_runtime/mcp/experience.py:560-570` (the **external front door** for
third-party agent runtimes). Under the `ServedProduct`-beside option debt
reaches an in-process caller and no wire consumer at any of the three. Criterion
1 says *no* delivery path drops it; a ruling priced against one door understates
the cost by two.

**Q7 — Is debt per-(product, principal)?** If entitlement bounds what an
assembler may consult on a caller's behalf, completeness and therefore debt
differ per principal. The whitepaper supports the premise: `:1529` commits
*entitlement-formative* construction, so different principals legitimately
receive different products. The open part is whether debt is one of the things
that differs. Research offers a reading nobody had put forward: **option (b)
dissolves the coupling rather than resolving it** — if a principal is not
entitled to sanctions data, sanctions data is not part of what *their* decision
requires, so its absence is not a shortfall and debt stays one computation per
assembled product. A new threat, named for the first time here: under option
(a) a caller can infer *what they are not allowed to see* from the completeness
figure.

**Q2** debt's shape on the wire, and whether recency and salience are one field
or two. **Q4** what *"a material event transpired"* means mechanically without
domain values in the core — with plan `15.5-01`'s verdict on whether the
supersession option is reachable at all. **Q5** payload validation as a fourth
gate, a registration-time bind, or both, plus the unavoidable sub-question that
`SkillArgumentSchema` is one level deep by ruling and a UBO payload with a list
of owner objects is not expressible in it. **Q6** debt composition weighting for
aggregate products — noting criterion 8 as written does not mention debt
composition, so option (d) may already be the intended scope; plan `15.5-12`
depends on this plan and reads Q6's ruling before it builds. **Q8** where
`freshness_sla` lives, arising from a whitepaper internal inconsistency: §7.6 at
`:2381` says the base's seven fields include freshness SLA per Fig-CH07-13's
sidebar, then unpacks seven fields that include `plan_id` and not freshness SLA
— the two enumerations of *"the same seven fields"* differ by exactly one
element.
