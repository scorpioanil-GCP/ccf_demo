## The mid-execution correction to Q5

The Q5 ruling was corrected by the coordinator while this run was in progress.
The first-pass entry recorded option (c) — a fourth gate in `run_gates` **and** a
registration bind. Dave's actual ruling is different in kind, not degree:

> *"Q5 payload validation → Schema validity at registration + payload conformity
> at Bind (request time). Gate scripts remain structural only."*

`D-IMPL-102` was rewritten before commit. Three things changed and all three
matter downstream:

1. **No fourth gate.** The three shipped validation gates stay at three, which
   keeps the whitepaper's three-validation-gate commitment intact rather than
   widening it. The first-pass entry had recorded the widening as deliberate;
   that framing is gone.
2. **Conformity moves to bind, and therefore couples to `D-IMPL-98`.** The typed
   request contract is what a request binds to, so bind is where a payload meets
   its declared schema. Q5 and Q1 describe one surface, not two.
3. **A measurement was retired with the option it supported.** The § A8 headroom
   figures (80 / 230 / 507 ns on `perf/suites/micro/test_gates.py`) were measured
   because a fourth gate would have landed on the serve path. No gate lands, so
   the entry states explicitly that those figures are **not** the relevant
   measurement for this ruling and must not be quoted as though one were.
   `D-IMPL-107` was amended by one clause to agree.
