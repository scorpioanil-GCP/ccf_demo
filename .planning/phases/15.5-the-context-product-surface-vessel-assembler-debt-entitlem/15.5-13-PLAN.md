<objective>
Make the reference example assemble instead of aggregate.

Purpose: this example is what commissioned the phase. It pins
`subject_entity_ref: "http://example.org/fibo/AcmeCorp"`,
`temporal_boundary: 2026-08-01T00:00:00Z` and `now: 2026-08-01T00:05:00Z` as
literals — one company, one frozen instant, one answer — against Chapter 1's
own thesis that context is *"assembled at decision time, not precomputed against
design-time queries… It is not knowable at design time; it is shaped by the
decision."* And `serve()` is called with no `query_pin_writer` and no
`executed_queries`, so the mechanism that pins **which question a decision
answered** is built and unused.

The reference use case, in Dave's words: an `InvestmentBankingAssistantAgent`
whose job is to answer, about **any company at any time**, *"Who ultimately owns
Acme Corporation, and does any natural person's stake cross the KYC reporting
threshold?"*

Output: an example a reader copies and gets a callable product, not a frozen
answer.
</objective>
