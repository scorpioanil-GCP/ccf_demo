## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the touched module(s) with the quick command; `make lint` whenever `src/` changed (CINV-06 catches an unmarked new contract field immediately).
- **After every plan wave:** chunk A (products, mcp, invariants, surface) and chunk B (replay); `make typecheck`; the three gate-tools suites separately; `make schemas` / `make surface` / `make surface-changes` whenever a contract field or export moved.
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work` / phase gate:** all four chunks, `make product-gates`, `make docs-strict`, and `make leg-postgres` + `make leg-fluree` to OBSERVE the quarantine roster red-then-quarantined — the only way the § Priority 4 rosters are confirmed rather than reasoned.
- **Max feedback latency:** ~300 s (chunk D).
