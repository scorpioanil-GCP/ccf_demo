<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase makes the project's proof machinery **simple, reusable and itself tested** — once,
at the smallest size that discharges `D-IMPL-269` (5). It is the answer to a measured fact:
across 24.8's five waves roughly ten defects surfaced and **every one was in proof machinery;
none was in the mechanism.**

**Three core items. Nothing else.**

1. **Fix `24.8-C-03`** — narrow the `CLAUDE.md` claim in
   `tests/invariants/test_determination_authority_disclosure_delivery.py` to the invariant it
   was actually meant to protect.
2. **Promote the `_scratch_egress` shape to ONE tested sabotage harness** — the scratch-copy,
   mutate, import-under-its-own-name pattern rebuilt at 46 sites across 40 test modules.
3. **Unify the real-tree guard under ONE canonical mechanism** across the measured population
   of 16 defining modules under 4 names.

Call-site migration is **opportunistic only, and only where a migrated site is needed to prove
the harness.** It is not a goal of the phase.

**This is a phase, not a chore** — deliberately, because the deliverable is itself novel proof
code, this project's highest-risk category, and a chore has no criteria lock.

### D-01 — The phase exists, and it is a phase

**REJECTED:** the tooling-chore-off-`main` alternative; the phase-plus-P-6 alternative; skipping
it and fixing only the pin.

### D-02 — `24.8-C-03`: narrow the claim, do not retire it, do not generalize it
> *"narrow the CLAUDE.md claim to the invariant it was actually meant to protect. Do not
> generalize a one-off lifetime bug into a fence-expiry framework, and do not retire the check
> entirely if a precise durable invariant can be stated. The narrowed assertion should survive
> unrelated CLAUDE.md edits and fail only if the protected invariant itself moves improperly."*

The defect: the test pins `CLAUDE.md` byte-unchanged against `1022ad59`, while the file last
moved at `4683f245` — **24.6's own close commit.** The fence was invalidated by the phase it
was fencing. It is a category error about **lifetime**, not storage: a phase-scoped fence
encoded as an unconditional standing invariant against a document expected to change under
normal governance.

**Retiring it is permitted ONLY if no precise durable invariant can be stated** — and that is a
finding the phase must make and record with evidence, not an escape hatch it may take for
convenience.

**No fence-expiry framework.** The measured population of pinned-base permanence claims is
**ONE** (confirmed at this sitting).

### D-03 — Nothing else is admitted
> *"nothing else. P-6 stays unmeasured and out. C-02 belongs to a phase that legitimately opens

> rewrite."*

### D-04 — The fence (follows from D-03)
`tests/` and `tools/` **OPEN**. **`src/` CLOSED** — stated directly by Dave in excluding
`24.8-C-02`. `runtime/` **CLOSED** by the same logic. `examples/` **CLOSED** — the rungs stay
frozen under `D-IMPL-261`; `tests/examples/` open ONLY for quarantine or expected-failure
marking under the standing `D-IMPL-263` rule.

### D-05 — The harness must make the proof HARDER TO MISUSE
> *"the shared harness should not merely centralize the five-step scratch-import pattern. It
> should **make the proof harder to misuse** than the local copies were. In particular, the
> harness must fail if the requested mutation did not actually alter the scratch source, and
> its real-tree guard should come from one canonical mechanism/roster rather than
> conventionally named local tests."*

**This is what distinguishes the phase from a refactor.** Two binding consequences:
- **(a)** the harness FAILS when `mutated == source`;
- **(b)** the real-tree guard is derived from **one canonical mechanism or roster**, never from
  a naming convention.

### D-06 — `PROOF-INVENTORY.md` merged to `main`, rejected candidates kept
> *"merge `PROOF-INVENTORY.md` to `main` as durable evidence, after correcting P-2 to the
> measured 16 modules / 4 names. The rejected consolidation candidates are as valuable as the
> accepted ones because they prevent future re-litigation."*

Done: corrected at `13c8bc39`, merged at `309f125e`. P-4, P-5, P-6 and P-7 keep their **LEAVE
ALONE** verdicts on the record deliberately.

## Measured at This Sitting — Do Not Re-Derive, Do Not Assume

Every number below was measured on the tree at `309f125e`, with a zero-hit positive control
run beside the P-1 count. **`PROOF-INVENTORY.md` was wrong once already; these supersede it
where they differ.**

### P-1 — the sabotage harness population
- **46 sites across 40 test modules** in `tests/` (`importlib.util.spec_from_file_location`),
  plus **1 further site in `tools/`** = 47 / 41 tree-wide.
- **The shape to promote already exists and already satisfies D-05(a).**
  `tests/mcp/test_egress_refusal_channels.py::_scratch_egress` is
  `_scratch_egress(tmp_path, *, mutate: Callable[[str], str], name: str) -> types.ModuleType`
  and **already carries `assert mutated != source, "the sabotage did not take"` at line 970.**
  It parameterizes exactly the two things that vary, restores `sys.modules` on a failed exec,
  and was generalized locally and never promoted.

### P-2 — the real-tree guard population (CORRECTED)
- **16 modules define the guard, under 4 names:** `test_no_sabotage_edits_the_real_source_tree`
  (10), `test_no_control_edits_the_real_source_tree` (4),
  `test_no_control_here_edits_the_real_source_tree` (1),
  `test_no_control_in_this_module_edits_the_real_source_tree` (1).
- **2 further modules cite a guard by name in prose without defining one**
  (`tests/operators/test_traversal_direction.py`,
  `tools/product_gates_tests/test_check_product_gates.py`), so a naive `grep -rln` over the
  phrase returns **18** and over-counts by two. **Defining roster 16; mentioning roster 18.**
- Measured without a prefix assumption:
  `grep -rhno "def [a-zA-Z_]*edits_the_real_source_tree" tests/ tools/`. There is no fifth name.

### THE CENTRAL DESIGN FINDING — the 16 guards use THREE different mechanisms, and the name does not predict which

| Mechanism | What it asserts | Modules |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Byte-snapshot** | re-read the file, compare to bytes captured at import | ~10 |
| **B. Git status/diff on a path** | the working tree has no uncommitted change under a path | 4 |
| **C. Predicate re-run** | re-run the predicate the sabotage violates against the real tree | 2 |

**The naming split and the mechanism split are different partitions.**
`tests/invariants/test_present_tense_sweep.py` is named `control` and uses **A**;
`tests/conformance/test_operator_tier_differential.py` is named `control` and uses **B**. The
name mis-signals what the guard asserts.

**Scope differs too, within one mechanism.** Three git-based guards assert cleanliness of ONE
relative file; `test_determination_authority_disclosure_delivery` asserts `src` + `runtime`.

**TWO GUARDS CONTAIN EXPLICIT, REASONED ARGUMENTS AGAINST THE GIT MECHANISM THAT FOUR OTHERS
USE.** This is a documented design disagreement inside the population, and it is the reason
D-05(b) is a design question rather than a merge:
- `tests/products/test_refusal_envelope.py`, verbatim: *"A blanket `git status` check would go
  red for a developer with any unrelated uncommitted edit under `src/` — a guard that fails on
  correct work teaches people to ignore it, and the thing actually worth catching is narrower
  and exactly nameable."*
- `tests/invariants/test_delivery_boundary_replay.py`, verbatim: *"Asserted from the trees
  themselves rather than from `git`: the claim is that the shipped doors hold none of the
  forbidden shapes right now, which is the same predicate (a) runs and is the one a stray write
  would break."*

**Consequence for planning: unifying naively on the git mechanism would REGRESS a documented,
argued decision in at least two modules.** The phase must choose a canonical mechanism, state
the trade-off it is resolving, and justify what it does with the two dissenting guards.

### P-3 — the pinned-base population is ONE (confirmed)
- The only `git diff --quiet` permanence machinery in `tests/` or `tools/` is in
  `tests/invariants/test_determination_authority_disclosure_delivery.py` (3 lines: the constant

- The other `PHASE_BASE` occurrence, `tools/publish_scan_tests/test_publish_scan.py:428`, is

- **The better storage convention already exists twice and predates the defect:**

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- **`_scratch_egress` is the harness, already written.** Promotion is extraction plus a test
  suite plus the D-05(b) guard decision — not a design from scratch.
- **`tools/lints/` is the packaging template.** One runner, one signal, shared config, own
  tests. The inventory's §6 verdict is LEAVE UNCHANGED — cite it, do not touch it.

### Established Patterns
- Sabotage-by-scratch-copy, never edit-and-revert: *"a control implemented as an edit leaves
  nothing in the tree, and one implemented as a test runs on every CI run forever."*
- Every proving instrument carries a control observed RED.
- Positive controls beside counts (a token that must return zero).

### Integration Points
- 16 test modules define the guard; 2 more cite it by name in prose — **a rename must update
  the prose citations too, or it recreates the roster hazard in a new form.**
- `tests/operators/test_traversal_unresolved_reference.py`'s guard docstring cites its sibling
  in `test_traversal_admission.py` as *"the voice"* it is written in — evidence the author knew
  it was the same guard and still gave it a new name.

### The known failure modes this phase is built to prevent
- **24.8 plan 06:** an AST sabotage failed to fire because it planted only a comment.
- **24.8 plan 05:** a lint sabotage **masked 91 of its own failures** because the inserted line
  contained the token being searched for.
- **24.8 plan 02:** a sabotage written unconditionally could not red the instrument it targeted.

**The claim the phase rests on, and which `PROOF-INVENTORY.md` §11 flags as argued but not
demonstrated:** that a shared harness would actually have caught these three. **Demonstrating it
by construction is the first thing worth doing in this phase.**
