## What the post-ruling scout found — and why it matters

Reading the subjects to write CONTEXT.md turned up two things neither the inventory nor the
ruling anticipated:

1. **`_scratch_egress` already satisfies the refinement's first half.** Line 970 already carries
   `assert mutated != source, "the sabotage did not take"`. Promoting that exact shape discharges
   D-05(a) directly.
2. **The 16 guards use THREE mechanisms — byte-snapshot, git status/diff, predicate re-run — and
   the name does not predict which.** Worse, **two guards contain explicit reasoned arguments
   against the git mechanism that four others use.** `test_refusal_envelope.py`: *"A blanket
   `git status` check would go red for a developer with any unrelated uncommitted edit under
   `src/` — a guard that fails on correct work teaches people to ignore it."*

So D-05(b) is a genuine design question with a documented disagreement inside the population, not
a mechanical merge. Unifying naively on git would regress an argued decision in at least two
modules. This is the most valuable thing the sitting produced for the planner and is recorded in
CONTEXT.md under **THE CENTRAL DESIGN FINDING**.
