## THE HEADLINE — a product defect, not a test defect

Both reviewers independently found that **`OFS-G03` never serves the product**.
Chasing that: the product **ignored `artifacts/restatement.yaml` entirely**.

`_read_cells` hard-coded its restatement partition as
`(tag, unit, period_start, period_end, fiscal_period)`. The artifact declares
`(lei, tag, unit, period_end, fiscal_period)` — **without `period_start`** — and
names the longer variant explicitly to reject it: *"too strict… a filer who
restates a PERIOD BOUNDARY has restated the fact, and treating that as a
different fact hides a real correction."*

Measured against the estate through the shipped read path:

| Key | Restated cells |
|---|---|
| the artifact's declared key | **321** |
| the key the product actually used | **317** |

**Four real corrections were hidden**, all of them annual revenue whose period
boundary moved — exactly the case the artifact describes. Two belong to
`G8NWFN4YP7CR6FOP4886` and two to `B3BS7ACMDUWISF18KY76`.

**Fixed**: the partition is now built from the artifact's `fact_identity_key`.
**Verified with a positive control at cell level** — two cells flip from
`False` to `True` for the obligor that can serve. The first attempt at that
control compared *line names* and showed no difference, because the line was
already restated for other periods; the coarse check would have reported the fix
as verified when it proved nothing. Re-measured per cell.

**None of the three documented runs is affected**, so the pinned transcript
still reproduces byte-for-byte.
