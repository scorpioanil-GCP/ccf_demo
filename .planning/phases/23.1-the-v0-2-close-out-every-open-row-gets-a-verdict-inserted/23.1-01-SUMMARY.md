The plan and the CONTEXT both spell snippet-driving commands as
`PYTHONPATH=src:runtime .venv/bin/python …`. `tools/` sits at the repo root,
which is not on that path: `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'tools'`. Every

Recorded at the head of `23.1-INSTRUMENTS.md` so no later wave loses time to it.
