Close blocking finding B2 under Dave's D-IMPL-58 ruling: **reading (d)** — the merged
federated lineage persists at the boundary crossing as an immutable `AdmissionRecord`
artifact, not in graph state. `LineageEntry` and `_TemporalClaim` are not touched.

Purpose: `A2AAdmissionGate.admit` computes the union into `AdmittedProduct.lineage` and
nothing reads it — `AssemblyWorker.ingest_admitted` (`worker.py:391-489`) reads
`admitted.product` and never `admitted.lineage`, stamps the *consumer's*
`policy_bundle_version`, and `graph_snapshot_at` is referenced nowhere in `/src` outside
`products/contract.py` and `union_lineage`. Under (d) the claim becomes true *and* correctly
located: lineage continuity is a property of the federation boundary, which is what
CLAUDE.md §7.2 actually describes.

**Read the ruling's correction paragraph before writing anything.** Reading (d) was first
ruled on a false premise — that admission was "already audited and already reaches replay".
Both halves are false: `AuditLog.append` carries scalars only (`action, key, version,
resolved, occurred_at, actor, artifact_class`) with no payload field, and `src/ccf/replay/`
contains exactly one occurrence of "audit", a docstring sentence at `serve.py:63`. The audit
plane answers *was this admitted, by whom, when*; it does not answer *what was admitted*.
This plan pays the stated cost: a new artifact class, its registration, an ArtifactStore
write at admission, and a replay resolution path.
Output: closure items 1–5 of the ruling's list; item 6 is 06-22's.
</objective>

| admission → L2 | only an `AdmittedProduct` crosses; L2 remains the sole writer to L3 |
