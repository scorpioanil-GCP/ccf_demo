<objective>
Close blocking finding B2: "lineage extended by set-union" is currently a property of a
discarded wrapper. `A2AAdmissionGate.admit` computes `merged` into `AdmittedProduct.lineage`,
`AssemblyWorker.ingest_admitted` never reads it, and no `/src` consumer of
`admitted.lineage` exists — L2 strips payload records into `SignalEnvelope`s and the merged
`ProductLineage` is thrown away. ROADMAP criterion 2 and gate row 14 claim lineage continuity
as a **fabric** property (CLAUDE.md §7.2, whitepaper L-axis).

Purpose: the three axes of `ProductLineage` have no home in fabric state today.
`LineageEntry` carries `(source, envelope_id, observed_at, passage_anchor)` and nothing
else; `ingest` stamps the **consumer's** `policy_bundle_version` and derives `observed_time`
from `temporal_boundary`, never `graph_snapshot_at`. Every faithful fix is therefore a
`/src` contract change — which, by the D-IMPL-55 precedent in this very phase, carries a
numbered decision entry rather than arriving incidentally. This plan surfaces it, stops at
the decision boundary, and implements Dave's ruling.
Output: `D-IMPL-58`, Dave's ruling, and either the implementation of it or a recorded
narrowing handed to 06-22.
</objective>
