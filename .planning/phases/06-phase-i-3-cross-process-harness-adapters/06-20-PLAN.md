<objective>
Close blocking finding B3's evidence half. The consumption axis of the portability proof is
real and has teeth — the product is recovered out of each harness-native carrier and
mutating ADK's delivered bytes turns the suite red. The emission axis is not: all three
adapters' governed `capture_effect` is `return agent.emit_effect(output)`, so feeding the
same payload three times *must* hash-equal and the equality assertion cannot detect
harness-specific corruption. The seam-4 axis is weaker on the Claude leg, whose
`execution()` is a hand-built `ResultMessage` (advisory A3).

Purpose: CLAUDE.md §7.3 says "portability is demonstrated, not asserted". The defect the
reviewer found is scope, not fraud. This plan raises the evidence where it can be raised —
option (i) of the triage — so the narrowing in 06-22 is as small as it honestly can be.
Output: adversarial legs that give the emission and trace equalities a real failure mode,
and a module docstring that states what is driven.
</objective>
