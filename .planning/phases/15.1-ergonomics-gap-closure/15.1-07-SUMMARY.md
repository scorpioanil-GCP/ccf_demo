## Decisions Made

### 1. The `grep … is 0` criterion is not met literally, and must not be

The plan's acceptance criteria ask that
`grep -c "operators_invoked=execution.operators_invoked"` be **0** in
`examples/06-decision-replay/run.py` and `examples/08-e2e-demo/run.py`, and that the phase-wide
`grep -rc` report 0 in every file. **Both files still report 1, and that occurrence is correct.**

The research ledger enumerates nine CR-2 sites, every one of them a
`ProductLineage(operators_invoked=execution.operators_invoked, …)`. The greps are broader than the
ledger: in eight of the nine files a *second*, non-lineage occurrence exists — the demonstration
dataclass's field, which hands **the engine's resolution** to the test module:

```
examples/06-decision-replay/run.py:1109    ReplayDemonstration(operators_invoked=execution.operators_invoked, …)
examples/08-e2e-demo/run.py:1102           (the e2e demo's equivalent)
```

Those fields are what makes

```python
assert result.product.lineage.operators_invoked == result.operators_invoked
```

a comparison of **two sources** after this plan rather than one object against itself. Before the
rewrite both sides traced to `execution`; now the left is the product class's claim and the right
is the engine's report. Rewriting the dataclass field to anything else — or renaming it to dodge
the grep — would either destroy that or be grep-gaming. Neither was done.

`examples/external/00-hello-context/serve.py` has no such field and *does* report **0**.

**For plan 15.1-10, which runs `grep -rn "operators_invoked=execution.operators_invoked"
examples/` and expects no output** (15.1-10-PLAN.md:282): expect **8 lines**, one per example that
returns a demonstration object, and none of them a `ProductLineage` construction. The
substantive check is `grep -n "ProductLineage(" -A 2 examples/` — over this plan's files it shows
three lineage sites reading `frozenset(declared.required_operator_keys)` and two reading
`record.content_generation_path.operator_plan`, with nothing reading `execution`.

### 2. `test_ext00_serve_output.py`'s operator assertion is now a transcription check

`test_the_lineage_declares_exactly_the_operators_the_class_requires` compares
`servable.product.lineage.operators_invoked` against `declared.required_operator_keys`. After this
rewrite the lineage **is** `frozenset(declared.required_operator_keys)`, so both sides read one
artifact and the assertion is an identity through one transcription step.

That is a real loss of reach, and the honest response is to say so rather than leave a docstring
claiming a propagation check. The assertion was **kept** (it still catches a `serve.py` that filled
the lineage from something other than the claim) and its docstring rewritten to state: what it now
is; that the `assembled` fixture existing at all is the evidence the binder admitted, because a
divergence stops `serve.py` before it composes; that Gate 2 downstream is assembly-path integrity;
and that the transcription residual is unchanged in kind. No assertion was added — the plan asked
for a docstring amendment, and an extra assertion asserting the two artifacts agree would only
restate what the binder already refuses on.

### 3. Every new artifact is authored, not transcribed — but the residual stands

The three `projection-requirements.yaml` documents carry the same four operator keys their
sibling `product-class.yaml` carries (they must, or the binder refuses). Each one's header states
the authorship residual in D-IMPL-86's own terms: an author who transcribes one document out of
the other collapses the check, and no gate can tell an authored literal from a transcribed one.
The documents were written key by key against each example's registry, not by copying the file.

### 4. `role: register`, not `load`

Each new manifest entry is `role: register` with `registration: typed`. The operator set a
projection depends on is part of what the fabric was permitted to do and belongs in replay
dimension 2 — the same argument `product-class.yaml` already carries. The manifest comments state
it at each site.
