## Decisions Made

- **Append, never edit.** The re-approval is a third entry. The precedent it follows is the
  one the log already runs on (the D-GATE-15 second amendment; the D-IMPL-86 correction), and
  the two prior entries are byte-unchanged.
- **Re-verify against the tree, not against the prior verification.** The 2026-08-05
  clause-by-clause check under ERGO-19 cites `artifacts.py:759` and `manifest.py:335`; those
  are now `:833` and `:436`. Copying them forward would have produced a record that reads
  authoritative and points at the wrong lines.
- **Report only what was run.** Every figure in the gate entry's verification matrix is one
  this executor ran on 2026-08-06. The withdrawn entry's `make test` figure of 2059 is stated
  as *superseded, not contradicted* — it was true of the tree it described.
- **Do not soften a residual to make the record tidier.** R5-WR-01 is recorded with both
  measured directions, the renamed-target controls, the `6cf00d3` comparison, and the
  reviewer's own sentence about why Critical would have been inconsistent — because the
  classification's dependence on R4-WR-03 staying open is a fact a later reader needs. The
  entry says so explicitly: closing one without revisiting the other leaves the record
  inconsistent.
- **State what the approval did not cover.** Dave issued no separate written rulings on the
  residuals other than R5-WR-01/R5-WR-02; the entry says they carry forward in the state the
  withdrawn entry and the review documents already record them in, and that nothing in it
  should be read as closing a residual the approval did not close.
- **R-1 is superseded in substance, not answered.** The pre-withdrawal cross-AI round's
  thinness (one working reviewer of three) is no longer the condition under which the phase
  closes — five deep execution-based rounds have run against it since — and the entry says
  that rather than declaring R-1 resolved.
