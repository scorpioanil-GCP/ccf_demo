## 2. What the fix is, and the one thing the review's remedy did not close

`_binding_records` enumerates the binding forms; `_bindings` walks the body through it. **A form
this module cannot read through records `path=None` — present but unreadable.** That distinction is
the whole finding: a *skipped* binding leaves the previous one in force, so the checks go on
describing an object the name no longer holds and answer **affirmatively**; a binding recorded as
unreadable makes property (2) say "the engine runs something I cannot describe", which is loud.

**`with … as` is closed.** The review measured its own remedy and stated the caveat plainly:
`ast.withitem` carries no `lineno`, so a naive `getattr(node, "lineno", 0)` records the binding at
line 0, which sorts before every other binding and is therefore never in force — the spelling stayed
admitted under its patch. Dispatching at the enclosing `With`/`AsyncWith` statement takes the line
from the statement. Measured: `with cm as plan:` is REJECTED, on a probe and on a real-tree
mutation. `test_every_binding_form_binds_and_the_unreadable_ones_say_so` asserts `lineno > 0` for
every unreadable form, which is the trap stated as an assertion.

**Four forms the review's sketch did not name are covered too** — imports, nested `def`/`class`,
`match` captures, and comprehension targets — each measured in the table above and each REJECTED as
a rebinding in §3.

**A comprehension target is a *scoped* binding, and neither of the two obvious answers is right.**
Skipping it leaves the CR-01 hole in one more spelling: `[ProjectionEngine(reader=r).execute(plan=plan)
for plan in alts]` runs every plan in `alts` while resolving to the plan the binder was handed.
Recording it unscoped is **red on correct code**, because in Python 3 a comprehension target does not
leak: `plan = P(); bind(plan); [str(plan) for plan in alts]; run(plan)` runs the bound plan.
`_Binding.until` carries the comprehension's last line, and `_binding_before` puts a scoped binding
in force for `lineno <= line <= until` and nowhere else. Both directions measured:

| Shape | Expected | Observed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[execute(plan=plan) for plan in alts]` after binding a different plan | REJECT | **REJECTED** |
| `[str(plan) for plan in alts]` then `execute(plan=plan)` | ADMIT | **ADMITTED** |
| a read of the shadowed name *inside* the comprehension | the comprehension's target | `a@3` |
| a read of it *after* the comprehension | the binding above | `('y','k')` |

The `<=` is the one place the resolution walk can ask about a line it is already at, so termination
is asserted rather than argued: every `until`-carrying binding is constructed with `path=None`,
`_resolve_at` stops at an unreadable binding, and
`test_alias_resolution_terminates_on_a_cyclic_assignment_chain` now drives a shadowing comprehension
under the same 5-second wall-clock bound as the rotations.
