This was the most expensive phase of the project to date. The overrun was not caused
by the work being harder than estimated; it was caused by defects being found *after*
approval and by an unbounded review→fix→review loop.

## What was delivered

**CR-1 (ERGO-18).** Deployment manifests declare `registration: typed | versioned` as
a required, no-default field on every entry. `load_and_boot` dispatches on it and
refuses an author-versioned SDK class asking for `typed`; a refusal never yields a pin.
`ControlPlaneProtocol` declares `register_versioned`, so a conforming plane cannot drop
the D-05 bump check. The `ccf.` namespace is reserved as a prefix check rather than a
fixed id list.

**CR-2 (ERGO-19), design E2.** The projection's required operator set is now its own
`ccf.projection-requirements` Control Plane artifact, authored separately from the
product-class declaration. `refuse_operator_set_divergence` binds the two between plan
construction and `ProjectionEngine.execute`, compares them as sets, and **refuses
rather than repairs** — it will not choose which authored document is right. Gate 2 is
documented for what it verifies: assembly-path integrity.

**Beyond the original scope**, forced by review: a manifest path-containment fix
(absolute and `../` escapes were readable), AST tripwires making the old shape
unwritable, and a mechanical **citation lint** in `tools/lints/`.

## Lessons

**1. A gate presentation must carry its own code review.**
`/gsd:execute-phase` runs `code_review_gate` *after* `verify_phase_goal` and the phase
gate. Dave was therefore asked to approve Phase 15.1 before any review of it existed;
the review then returned three verified Criticals and the approval had to be withdrawn.
This is a workflow-ordering defect, not a judgment error, and Phase 16 will hit it
unless the ordering is changed.

**2. Bound iterative loops before entering them.**
Five review rounds ran with no stated stopping rule, and a fresh human decision was
requested after each. The right shape was one ruling up front — *stop when a round
returns zero Criticals, or when remaining findings require contrived code to trigger* —
then run to it. Criticals per round went 3 → 1 → 1 → 1 → 0, which was converging from
round two onward and could have been predicted and priced.

**3. Every defect found in this phase had one shape: a check narrower than its own
docstring claimed.**
`_names_the_binder` said it checked the binder; it checked a name. `_is_plan_construction`
said it found plan constructions; it found a naming convention. `_bindings` said "every
assignment to every local name"; it read three node types. The durable fix is not more
coverage — it is that a check's docstring must state what it *cannot* see, and be tested
against that statement. `test_a_loop_carried_rebinding_is_out_of_reach_and_says_so` is
the pattern: a limit that fails loudly if it ever stops being a limit.

**4. Reviewers were measurably wrong six times; implementers caught each.**
A stated reproduction that did not hold; a proposed replacement that would have
re-created the defect one layer down; a proposed rekey matching 67 calls tree-wide with
53 false positives; a case declared unclosable that was closed; an arithmetic slip
repeated from a prior document. **Treat a review's proposed remedy as a hypothesis to
measure, never as a specification.**

**5. Sweeps must measure their scope, not inherit it.**
Four consecutive rounds of the stale-claim sweep failed the same way — each took its
predecessor's tree list as the territory. `public/` (published adopter docs) was in no
round's scope until goal-backward verification found it. The final round broke the
pattern for phrasings but kept it for trees, and both surviving findings sat exactly in
the trees the list omitted.

**6. Claim classes without a mechanical guard rot silently.**
Surface names have a drift gate, call shapes have AST tripwires, docstrings have schema
regeneration, docs have `docs-strict`. Cross-file citations had nothing — so they drifted
four times, twice *inside the commits fixing them*, and survived five reviews. The answer
was the same as the binder's: a lint that refuses.

**7. Numbers propagate.**
An expected grep count of "8" travelled through two SUMMARYs and an orchestrator briefing
without re-measurement. The real figure was 5. Several other figures were withdrawn in
marked corrections. In a phase about unsupported claims, every stated number must be one
the writer ran.

## Carried forward to Phase 16

`deferred-items.md` in this directory is the authoritative inheritance list — **not**
`docs/SURFACE-AUDIT.md` §6.4, which predates the last four remediation rounds.

Open: **R-2** (the binder is adopter-invoked and enforced only inside `examples/`),
**R-3** (authoring-boundary transcription, AST-undetectable), **R-4** (`/schema` 404s for
six classes whose schemas ship), **R4-WR-03** (loop-carried rebinding), **R5-WR-01** (a
docstring at `tests/products/test_gate_two_call_shape.py` still states the opposite of
what the code does — two candidate remedies, neither measured), **R5-WR-02**, the
`examples/01–04` sweep remainder, and **IN-F1/F2/F4** (`src/` boundary validation;
IN-F1 is two lines).

Two standing constraints: Phase 16 **must not document a self-satisfying gate** — Gate 2
is assembly-path integrity — and `make ci` does not reach `tools/` though CI does, where
three suites fail pre-existing.
