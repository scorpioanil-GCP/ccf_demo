Widen CINV-01 (No-Hardcoding) to cover the `tests/` tree, per D-IMPL-78.

domain expectations read from `expected:` artifact blocks. `tests/` is not a linted tree
today, so relocating them would move domain values out of a structural invariant's reach
while every CI gate stayed green — a CI-invisible weakening arrived at as a side effect of
an ergonomics change. This plan closes the hole structurally, before any relocation happens.

Output: the two-line-not-one config change, its paired directional tests, and a
surface-change entry.
