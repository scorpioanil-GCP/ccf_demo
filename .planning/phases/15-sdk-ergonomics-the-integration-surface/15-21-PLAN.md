   found and which was expected — a mismatched direction must not read as a missing entry.

   its `Surface` name appears in the correspondingly-keyed diff list. `behavior` entries are
   exempt and only they are: that class exists precisely because a signature diff cannot see
   D-02's change. Any other entry with no diff line is a claim that did not happen — a defect,
   not a tolerance.

   awkward in the test harness, assert it in the acceptance criteria below with `git log` and
   keep the test to a content check.

Module docstring: this test *is* ROADMAP Success Criterion 6's completeness guarantee. The
standing drift gate and this test are designed as a pair — the gate is cheap and always-on
during the phase, this is exact and runs once at phase close and thereafter. Weakening either
without the other is a regression.
