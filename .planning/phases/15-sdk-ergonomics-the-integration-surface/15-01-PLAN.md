<objective>
Capture the phase-start public-surface baseline and stand up its drift gate, plus the
home for the curated surface-change log.

Purpose: ROADMAP Success Criterion 6 ("every removed or reshaped public surface is
recorded") cannot be satisfied honestly if the baseline is taken after the first surface
change. This plan is the **sole wave-1 plan**; nothing that touches a public surface — and
nothing that adds a module with an `__all__`, which includes `ccf.deployment` in 15-02 — may
start until it lands. Every other plan in the phase depends on it directly or transitively.

This plan also establishes the **single-owner regeneration rule** and the **two-file naming
scheme** the whole phase depends on:

- `docs/surface/v0.2-surface.json` is the **live** manifest. It is the path `dump_surface.py`
  writes, the path `--check` compares against, and the path `tests/test_surface_drift.py`
  reads — at phase start, throughout the phase, and after 15-21. It is written by exactly two
  plans, this one (phase start) and 15-21 (phase close). No plan in between lists it in
  `files_modified` or runs `make surface`. Eleven plans each regenerating the whole-tree
  manifest in its own worktree would pass `--check` pre-merge and leave the tree stale after
  the second merge, on a file no plan then owns.
- `docs/surface/v0.2-surface-baseline.json` is a **frozen byte-for-byte copy** of the same
  content, written once here and never again. It is the immutable "before" side of the
  Success Criterion 6 diff 15-21 produces.

The naming is deliberate and load-bearing. The obvious alternative — freeze the plain name and
give the live manifest a qualified one — would leave every future contributor and Phase 16
reaching for "the surface manifest", finding `v0.2-surface.json`, and getting the **stale**
one. Qualifying the *frozen* file instead means the unqualified path is always the live path,
no tool or test is ever re-pointed, and a stale read is impossible by naming.

Output: `tools/dump_surface.py` (with `--check` and `--report`), the live
`docs/surface/v0.2-surface.json`, the frozen `docs/surface/v0.2-surface-baseline.json`,
`tests/test_surface_drift.py`, a `make surface` target, and the `docs/surface-changes/`
fragment directory with its stated entry shape.
</objective>
