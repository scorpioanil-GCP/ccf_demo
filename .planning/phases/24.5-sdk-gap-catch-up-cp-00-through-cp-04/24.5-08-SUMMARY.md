# Phase 24.5 Plan 08: The product-gate coverage checker (`24.5-L-10`, `L-18` folded) Summary

`tools/check_product_gates.py` judges every `examples/cp-*` rung from its DECLARED `artifacts/gates.yaml`
(closed schema first) and a REAL `pytest --collect-only -q` over `tests/examples/`, reading gate-id
claims only in the form the roster declares — docstring form or name form, one canonical phrase each,
never inferred — so the real cp-04 tree measures clean under its name form while the two phantom ids in
`tests/examples/test_cp_04_seams.py`'s meta-test docstrings (`CFT-G17`, `CFT-G99`) are not observations;
26 tests, three sabotages of the checker itself watched RED naming the id and restored byte-identical,
nothing under `src/`, `runtime/` or `examples/` touched, and the checker deliberately NOT yet wired into
`make ci` (plan 09 wires it after the four rosters exist).
