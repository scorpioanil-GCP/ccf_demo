# Phase 24.5 Plan 13: One structural gate for declared obligations — a class-declared, request-tightenable debt ceiling and a class-declared lineage obligation, in both runners Summary

Ledger rows `24.5-L-14` and `24.5-L-26` are closed by ONE serve-time gate mechanism: a
product class now declares its obligations as data (`ServeObligations` — a `D_c` ceiling
and the payload members that must carry lineage), the registration carries them to the
serve path, and both `run_gates` and `run_federated_gates` hold a served product to them
LAST, after every universal gate, refusing by name (`declared-obligations`) with a typed
`DeclaredObligationRefusal` the HTTP door can map to the Standard's `409` (plan 14).
The ceiling is class-declared and a request may only tighten it (`D-IMPL-240` (3));
the lineage half reads presence and non-emptiness of a NAMED top-level member and nothing
else (`D-IMPL-238` limb 3); a class that declares nothing never reaches a comparison, so
every pre-phase registration and call site is unchanged. The token roster of both runners
is re-enumerated by a test against `D-IMPL-238`'s five plus the one `D-IMPL-237` licenses.

## Deviations from Plan
