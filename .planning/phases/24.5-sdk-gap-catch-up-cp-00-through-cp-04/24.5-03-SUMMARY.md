# Phase 24.5 Plan 03: The lineage carries the store pin, and replay resolves exactly it — Summary

`ProductLineage`, `ExecutionResult` and replay dimension 1 each carry a store-native
`StoreSnapshotPin` — read once by the engine before its first query, sentinel-defaulted so
every pre-phase construction site says what it always said — and `SnapshotReader` now
answers every read at the recorded pin through the live reader's `query_pinned`, or raises
`SnapshotPinUnresolvable` rather than reading the head; a pin that moved between record
and rehydration is `ReplayDivergence` naming both. Proven on `fluree/server:4.1.4` by
advancing a fresh ledger between the reads: pinned at `N` re-renders the `N` view
byte-identically after the advance, pinned at `N+1` does not. cp-04's two sealed
perspective addresses moved for that one cause and were re-sealed in the same commit
(`D-IMPL-240` (1)).
