Ledger row `24.5-L-24` is closed by NAMING the second of two assertions that shared the

inside the shipped test on scratch copies and on the committed tree with restore proven.

operator assembled nothing is now REFUSED by name instead of served as an empty answer with
an advisory `fail_closed` debt signal. That is the Standard's sentence (*an empty assembled
set fails closed*) made structural — and it reaches two places the phase should see: the
hello-world example on a clean checkout (which by `D-32` boots an EMPTY fabric and used to
