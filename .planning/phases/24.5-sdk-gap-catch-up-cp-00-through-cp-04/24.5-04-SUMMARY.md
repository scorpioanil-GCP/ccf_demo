# Phase 24.5 Plan 04: Node-class admission on the traversal walk — every backend honors or declines it, the vocabulary at 1.1.0 Summary

`TraversalNeighborhood` can now stop at a class boundary: `admitted_entity_classes: frozenset[str] | None = None`
on both `TraversalSpec` and `TraversalNeighborhoodParameters` admits a hop only onto an entity whose
`entity_class` is a member, never filters a walk start, and under `None` asks exactly the 1.0.x question.

Fluree honor it in Python by narrowing the admissible node list to admitted-class survivors plus the starts;
MongoDB declines to the fallback. `GQ-10` gained its sixth bullet inside its existing twenty lines, so all
35 clause headings kept their cited line numbers; the semantics version is 1.1.0 with a `GQ-35` entry. All
four live legs answered the admission corpus byte-identical to the oracle on a dedicated per-backend twin,
