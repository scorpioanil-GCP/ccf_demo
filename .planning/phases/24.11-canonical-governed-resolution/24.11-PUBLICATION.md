## The disclosure rule, and how it is held

The page shows each consumer **exactly what the fabric disclosed to it**. The
withheld panel is fed from the refused transcript alone; the served figures
(family 77, gross 10,125,000,000, adjusted 9,325,000,000) appear only in the
served panel, which is **lifted verbatim** from the scenario transcript by
`_served_excerpt` rather than retyped, so the two panels are the same kind of
evidence rather than one verbatim and one asserted.

`_refuse_cross_disclosure` refuses a build whose refused transcript carries
those figures. **Its first draft probed for the bare string `77` and fired on
every build**, because the transcript's replay reference is 128 hex characters
and contains `77` twice — a guard that cannot be satisfied is a guard that gets
deleted, and the figure it watched then travels unwatched. The probes are now
anchored to the form the served transcript actually prints (`family 77`, and
the grouped-thousands figures with digit/comma boundaries).
