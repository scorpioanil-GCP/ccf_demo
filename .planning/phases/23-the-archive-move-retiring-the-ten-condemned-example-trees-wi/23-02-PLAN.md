The measured defect: `PATH_TOKEN`'s negative lookbehind is `[A-Za-z0-9_.-]` and
omits `/`, so `archive/examples/04-cross-process/producer_child.py` yields the
token `examples/04-cross-process/producer_child.py` — the `archive/` prefix
silently stripped — which is then resolved against a path that will not exist
after the move. Proven end-to-end against the shipped function on a page whose
paths are CORRECTLY re-pointed: `prose_path_offenders([page])` returned two
paths **the page never wrote**.

**Fix by adding an optional `archive/` prefix to the alternation, NOT by adding
`/` to the lookbehind class.** Both are one edit; they are not equivalent.
Adding `/` to the lookbehind makes `archive/examples/X` match nothing at all —
which turns a false-offender bug into total blindness over the archive, and the
docstring's stated reason for the lookbehind (stopping the inner `examples` of
`tests/examples/x` being re-read) is served by leftmost-match consumption, not
by the lookbehind alone. Record the rejected alternative and this reason in the
constant's comment, which today asserts *"verified at HEAD, where no
`<prefix>/examples/` token exists anywhere in the published tree"* — a sentence
that stops being true at Wave 4 and must be rewritten now.

Then write the controls. Criterion 18 requires them in **BOTH** directions,
because a one-character lookbehind widening can blind the check entirely:

1. **Correct-tree direction.** A synthetic page under `tmp_path` naming
   `archive/examples/...` targets that EXIST → zero offenders. This is the test
   that is RED today and GREEN after the fix.
2. **Stale-archive direction.** A synthetic page naming
   `archive/examples/does-not-exist` → at least one offender, and the assertion

   count cannot distinguish "reported the right path" from "reported the
   stripped path"; that distinction is the whole defect.
3. **Stale-bare direction.** A synthetic page naming a stale bare
   `examples/does-not-exist` → at least one offender. Unchanged behaviour,
   asserted so a future widening cannot lose it.

Apply the same fix to BOTH halves of the module — the prose walk and the
include walk read the same token — and confirm which functions consume
