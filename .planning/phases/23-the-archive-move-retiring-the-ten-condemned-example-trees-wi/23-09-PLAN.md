<objective>
Take the ten trees out of the `examples:` aggregate and out of every command
surface that invites a reader to run them — and keep the documented-commands
bijection intact in both directions while doing it.

Purpose: criterion 3 is the concrete benefit the whole phase exists for, and it
is stated as a figure rather than an adjective: the always-on `examples:` job is
**ten of eleven** condemned trees — 91% — and the sole survivor is
`example-hw1-harness-clients`. `D-04` deletes the ten targets rather than
renaming them, because *"a target is an invitation to run, and these are no
longer the teaching path."*

The trap in this wave is a bijection.
`tools/docs_tests/test_documented_commands_exist.py` checks published `make
<target>` invocations against `.PHONY` in one direction and against `examples:`
in the other. **13 published `make example-*` command strings name a target this
plan deletes**, and they are neither snippet includes nor prose paths, so
criterion 4's "102 + 9" never counted them. They must change in the SAME wave or
the bijection is red in one direction or the other.

Output: one aggregate member, ten fewer targets, and every command surface true.
</objective>

   This is the single most likely silent miss in the plan.
