<objective>
Re-point the retirement sweep so an archived path still counts as a live
coupling — and prove the sweep cannot go blind to the archive.

Purpose: `D-05` (`D-IMPL-191` ruling 3) is the phase's most load-bearing small
change. The table hardcodes the trees against owners `16-15`/`16-17`/`16-18` —
a phase `D-IMPL-152` RETIRED — and `17-08`, a plan blocked at task 3. Nobody
owns those rows today. The ten moved trees re-point to Phase 23 plans, and an
archived path CONTINUES to count, *"because deletion is still owed and the
coupling is still a real obligation; making the sweep blind to the archive would
reproduce the exact defect Phase 22.1 was commissioned to fix — a tool that
reports clean because it did not look."*

Criterion 10 demands that be proven by a control rather than asserted. The
researcher found the gap precisely: **keeping the schedule pointed at the OLD
`examples/…` paths after the move makes the sweep report ZERO rows for the ten
trees and exit 0 — a confident clean total over a tree it did not look at — and
the current gates would NOT catch it.** Closing that is the work here.

Output: a re-pointed, owned table; a resolution control that did not exist; and
a 41-test module that still collects.
</objective>
