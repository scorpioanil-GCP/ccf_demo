## Rulings settled at planning, so they are not re-litigated in execution

1. **The restored claim, in the words `C-10` fixes it in.** The client detects *an answer
   to a different question*, and *a vessel that does not match the address its producer
   computed* — against **corruption, truncation, mis-routing, a re-serializing intermediary
   and producer/consumer canonicalization drift**. It does **not** detect an adversary who
   can rewrite the envelope, because the claim rides in the envelope. Signing is out of
   scope and **no signature member is reserved on the wire**. Say exactly this and no more;
   `19-RESEARCH.md` §5.3 enumerates the ticks and the two crosses and is the specification.
2. **Three further cases are not covered and are named.** A producer that *consistently*
   mis-canonicalizes states a matching address for its own wrong bytes (caught by the
   contract check and by cross-adapter parity, not by this comparison); two *identical*
   in-flight questions crossing is knowingly accepted; and — **`C-18`'s residual** — on a
   question asked with `as_of=None`, a transport that alters only the door's `asked_at` is
   undetected, because the client resolved the clock (`C-15`) so the instant crosses the
   wire exactly once and there is no second copy to compare it against. All three belong in
   the claim. **State the residual's boundary as precisely as `C-18` states it**: a
   **concrete** `as_of` crosses twice, is cross-checked at the door, and a disagreement is
   refused by name; only the `None` path is open. A sentence that declared the whole
   temporal axis unprotected would be as false as the sentence this phase is replacing, in
   the other direction.
3. **`C-15`'s amendment says what is true and names the defect.** The client resolves
   `as_of=None` to `now(UTC)` on both transports, so the deployment's clock is **not** what
   answers on the default path; what the deployment does answer against is reported on the
   served vessel. The amended sentence says that, cites the deferred decision entry
   `19-08` mints, and does **not** promise a fix in this phase.
4. **`consume.py` gets a word, not a line.** `19-RESEARCH.md` §9.3 measures the file at 80
   lines against a budget gate (`CLNT-14`). The `int`-not-`float` comment at `:32-35`
   currently describes a hypothetical and becomes a description of the shipped path. Change
   the tense; do not add a line. Verify the budget gate before and after.
5. **The surface fragment names EVERY moved module.** `tests/test_surface_drift.py` is
   module-granular and direction-blind: once a fragment names `ccf.client.wire`, later drift
   in that module is claimed for the rest of the phase, **including a removed export**.
   Enumerate `ccf.client.wire`, `ccf.products.door`, `ccf.client.adapters.mcp_stdio`,
   `ccf.client.adapters.inprocess` and `ccf_runtime.http_api.routes` explicitly, and add
   any other module the regenerated manifest shows drifting.
6. **Regenerate the manifest with `make surface`, never by hand**, and do it in this plan's
   last task so it captures every earlier wave.
