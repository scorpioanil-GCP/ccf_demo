## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** run the module(s) the task named in `<automated>` (quick run command).
- **After every plan wave:** the four chunks in the foreground plus the two store legs; `tools/check_product_gates.py`; `make lint`; `make typecheck`; `make gate-tools`.
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** full suite green except reds carved by a dated ruling; `docs/surface/examples/RETIREMENT-COUPLINGS.md` regenerated LAST.
- **Max feedback latency:** 120 s per task (one module). **Declared exception:** plan 07 Task 2 (criterion 23's suite) — its `<automated>` is the fast subset (citation ceiling + one hw1 module, < 120 s); the four chunks, the four store legs and the `make` gates are its ACTION, run in the foreground and pasted with `rc=`, and exceed the ceiling by design.
- **Wave 2 is sequential:** plans 02 → 04 → 05 on one tree (they share the gate checker, `tools.lints`, the ROADMAP ticks and the retirement report); plan 05 regenerates `RETIREMENT-COUPLINGS.md` once for the wave.
