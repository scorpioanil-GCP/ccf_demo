<what_the_rewrite_will_meet — measured at planning time, and not a prediction>
Four differences between what `spine.py` does today and what `GovernedResolver.__call__` does
were **measured by reading both**. They are listed so the executor recognizes each on sight and
classifies it rather than discovering it as an obstacle at 2am. None of them is a licence to
change the resolver: each is either bridged from the deployment side without changing s1-01's
result, or it is a finding.

1. **Seeds.** `spine.py` passes `seeds=request_seeds(scenario.request)` to
   `ProjectionEngine.execute` (the `D-IMPL-111` limb (a) ruling: *the request seeds the
   traversal*). `grep -n "seeds" src/ccf/products/resolver.py` returns **nothing**. Two s1-01
   tests exist precisely to catch this —
   `test_a_different_company_gets_a_different_answer` and
   `test_a_later_instant_pins_a_different_question_to_the_graph`. If they go red on the rewrite,
   that is the measurement, not a nuisance.
2. **Three product members the resolver's candidate does not carry.**
   `debt_evidence`, `source_refresh_recency` and `obligation_in_force` are all set by `spine.py`
   and none of `debt_evidence`, `source_refresh_recency`, `obligation_in_force` appears in
   `src/ccf/products/resolver.py`. They are inside the product's content address, so dropping one
   changes the served answer — which D-23 forbids.
3. **The plan/product-class binder.** `spine.py` calls
   `refuse_operator_set_divergence(declared=scenario.product_class, plan=plan)` between
   constructing Π and executing it (design E2, `D-IMPL-86`). The resolver builds its plan from
   `declaration.required_operator_keys` and calls no binder.
4. **Ingestion.** `GovernedResolver`'s docstring states plainly that it *"hands no envelope to
   the L1 writer"* — the deployment it is handed is already booted **and already populated**. So
   s1-01's `ingest` region moves out of the resolved path and into the caller, exactly as cp-05's
   `populate_and_traverse` does. That is a restructure of the example, not a behavior change, and
   it is expected.

**How to classify.** For each of the four, and for anything else the rewrite meets:
- **Bridgeable from the deployment side without changing the answer** → do it, and say in the
  plan SUMMARY how.
- **Not bridgeable without changing s1-01's business result** → **stop, preserve, classify.**
  Record it as finding `24.10-F-NN` with the measurement that establishes it (the test that goes
  red, the field that disappears from the content address, the two addresses side by side). Leave
  the evidence executable — a quarantined or `xfail`-marked test that still runs is evidence; a
  deleted assertion is not. Do **not** add the missing behavior to `GovernedResolver` in this
  plan: that is the API-surface change D-25 says has to be earned by exactly this measurement,
  and earning it is the finding's job, not this executor's.
</what_the_rewrite_will_meet>
