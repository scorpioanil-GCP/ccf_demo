<objective>
`R-12` — give a deployment's replay binding a declared home in the Control Plane, and make
`build_governed_resolver` consume it.

Purpose: cp-05 measured that `ccf_config.yaml`'s section vocabulary is closed and that a
`replay:` section is refused by name. Its own words, in
`examples/cp-05-credit-exposure-rollup/artifacts/replay.yaml`: *"it leaves a deployment's own
replay binding with nowhere declared to live. cp-04 put these three strings in `deployment.py`
as module constants; this rung puts them in an artifact instead. Neither is the Control Plane."*
This plan makes the third place exist and makes it load-bearing.

Output: one new SDK artifact class, one new reserved section, one consumption path with a ruled
precedence, and the tests that prove the declaration reaches the thing that uses it.
</objective>
