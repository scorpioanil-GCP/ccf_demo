<objective>
`R-11` — give a product's own declared invariant a legitimate runtime home on the governed path.

Purpose: `D-IMPL-224` made product gates **declared and CI-checked**; nothing executes one.
cp-05's reconciliation guard — *"the one gate that catches a plausible wrong answer"*, the failure
mode its spec §A says is invisible in the output — is called from the example's own spine and
would be **stranded** by adopting `GovernedResolver`. Dave: *"cp-05 cannot honestly adopt the
canonical governed spine until its reconciliation guard has a legitimate runtime home."*

Output: a Protocol-typed callable supplied to `build_governed_resolver`, a governed identity read
from the Control Plane, one new `GateRefusal` sibling, and the tests that watch the guard fire.
</objective>
