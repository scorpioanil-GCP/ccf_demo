<objective>
Land the stage-1 read translation — the **working floor** D-04 commissioned, so a
stage-2 failure degrades to a shipped stage 1 and never to nothing — join both
conformance suites, and make the leg always-on in CI.

Purpose: D-04's staging is the reason this phase cannot fail whole. Stage 1 is
fully conformant on its own; SUBQ-12's hedge says so explicitly. But conformance
is not the same claim as pushdown, and the 15.2 §3.1 review caught exactly that
trap on Neo4j: **byte-identity to the fallback is a conformance bar, not a
pushdown bar**, because `GQ-34` makes the degraded posture byte-identical *by
design*. A `_translate_query` that always returned `None` would pass the whole
differential corpus. That is why layer 2 and layer 3 are both required here and
why the telemetry test ships in the same plan as the translation.

**Stage 1 is NOT "nearly verbatim" from 15.2-12.** Two things are new and both
are named findings: the policy family cannot use `c[$key]` dynamic property
access (§R5 Break 3 — probe F-A6 in plan 15.3-01 settled which resolution
applies), and the documented 1 MB query-body cap is small enough that a large
`allowed_entities` set can reach it, so a byte-budget decline is a new named
mechanism on the `MAX_DEPTH_CEILING` precedent.

**`docs/GRAPH_QUERY_SEMANTICS.md` (semantics 1.0.1) must not change.** Fluree
implements existing clauses and cites the same `GQ-NN` anchors. This is not a
preference: the citation gate carries **line numbers** into anchored cites across
`/src` and `/tests`, and the 15.2 record notes that a naive insert near the top
produced **124 citation-guard findings in one edit**. If an amendment ever turns
out to be genuinely necessary it goes in the `GQ-35` amendment lane,
line-number-neutral.

Output: `_translate_query` stage 1, the `fluree` param on
`test_substrate_differential.py`, the `ci.yml` fourth always-on leg, and a
discriminating stage-1 pushdown-telemetry test.
</objective>
