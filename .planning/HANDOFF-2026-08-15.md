product over a wire.

There is also a naming artifact at `deployment.py:63` — a constant named
`MANIFEST` pointing at `ccf_config.yaml`, kept only so `consume.py` needed no
edit.

### Why it happened — the mechanism, not the excuse

Every example-shape ruling was individually sound and was made **in isolation
about a gate or an invariant**, never against *"would a developer read this and
want to use it?"* Each added structure; the total was never re-examined. The
example ended up serving the gates rather than the reader.

The blocking human checkpoint existed to catch exactly this and **did reject
twice** — but each fix answered that round's specific complaint instead of the
accretion, so the total kept growing.
