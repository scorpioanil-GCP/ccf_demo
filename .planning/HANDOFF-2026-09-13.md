## What rung 13 established, and which of it generalizes

**The central trap: a correct span that cannot be audited.** All 49
`ex:CovenantDefinition` nodes carry offsets landing on exactly the right words
and none carries a recorded hash or a named passage. **48 extractable, 47
reading as genuine definitions, 0 auditable.** A wrong citation is caught by a
hash; an unauditable one is caught only by a product that refuses to emit what
it cannot check — *while the text on screen is visibly correct*. **Generalizes:
whenever evidence is text, "extractable" and "auditable" are different
properties and the gap between them is where the product earns its keep.**

**`FIXED-upstream` is now in the finding vocabulary** (Dave, 2026-09-13), beside
`D-IMPL-261`'s four. The estate or source owner repaired the data after the
finding was raised. **Such a finding keeps its original measurement and its
causal correction** — no rewriting the past as though the defect never existed.

**My causal story was wrong while my numbers were right.** I attributed the
anchor collision to RDF being unable to pair literals; the truth was a URI that
omitted the document. **The repair I would have proposed would have made the
data look repaired without fixing it.** The peer checked the source before
implementing the agreed fix. **Generalizes: a correct measurement does not
validate the explanation attached to it, and the explanation is what the repair
gets designed against.**

**Four gates were green under their first sabotage and all four were TEST
defects.** Compared served output where no divergence existed; asserted `<=`
where only `<` separated the mechanisms; tested a limb with an empty population;
checked a constant instead of the real code path. **The principle: a gate must
assert the thing the product DOES, on a population where doing it wrong would
look different.**

**A set was read as a sequence, for the second time in two rungs.**
`operators_invoked` is a `frozenset` and cannot prove order; the gate passed by
incidental ordering and failed when a sibling module shared the interpreter.
Ordered replay data is the correct source. After cp-12's `next(iter(a_set))`,
**treat any unordered collection used where order is the claim as a defect
until proven otherwise.**

**`CP13-F-15` is not a calibration choice.** Reference expectation 365-day
